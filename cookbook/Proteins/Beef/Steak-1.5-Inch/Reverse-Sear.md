# Reverse-Sear Steak (1.5" Thick)

## Overview
⭐ **MOST FOOLPROOF METHOD** - Slow roast then sear. Nearly impossible to overcook. Restaurant secret for perfectly even doneness edge-to-edge.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **TOTAL TIME**: 45-60 minutes - don't start if rushed
- **MUST HAVE**: Meat thermometer and wire rack
- **FINAL SEAR**: Will smoke heavily - exhaust fan required
- **TEMP CHECK**: Check every 10 minutes in oven
- **PULL EARLY**: Remove at 10-15°F BELOW target (serious carryover cooking)

## Why This Method Wins
- Most even cooking (no gray band around edges)
- Easier timing (slower = more control)
- Best crust (dry surface from oven)
- Harder to mess up than pan-only method

## Target Temps (BEFORE final sear)
- Rare: 110°F (finish to 120°F)
- Medium-Rare: 115-120°F (finish to 130°F) ⭐ **RECOMMENDED**
- Medium: 125-130°F (finish to 140°F)

## Equipment
- Wire rack over rimmed baking sheet
- Instant-read thermometer (MANDATORY)
- Heavy skillet (cast iron ideal)
- Tongs

## Ingredients (per steak)
- 1 steak, 1.5" thick (12-16 oz)
- Kosher salt (1 tsp per side)
- Fresh cracked black pepper
- 2 tbsp high smoke-point oil
- 2 tbsp butter
- 2 garlic cloves, smashed
- Fresh herbs (thyme/rosemary)

## Preparation (1+ hours before)
1. Remove steak from fridge - must reach room temp
2. Pat DRY thoroughly with paper towels
3. Season generously with salt on all sides
4. Place on wire rack, uncovered, in fridge for 1+ hours
   - 🚨 This dries the surface = BETTER CRUST
   - Can do this up to 24 hours ahead

## Method

### Step 1: Slow Roast in Oven
1. Preheat oven to 275°F (NOT hotter)
2. Remove steak from fridge, let sit 20 min
3. Pat dry again, season with pepper
4. Place on wire rack over baking sheet
5. Insert thermometer into thickest part (leave it in)
6. Roast until 10-15°F below target
   - **For medium-rare: Pull at 115-120°F**
   - 🚨 **SET TIMER FOR 20 MINUTES** - check temp
   - Typically takes 25-40 minutes total
   - Check temp every 10 minutes after first 20

### Step 2: Rest (While Heating Pan)
1. Remove from oven when target temp reached
2. Let rest on rack 10 minutes
3. **DURING REST**: Heat cast iron over HIGH heat (5 min)
4. Add oil - should shimmer/smoke slightly
5. 🚨 Turn on exhaust fan NOW

### Step 3: Final Sear (1-2 minutes per side)
1. Pat steak dry ONE LAST TIME (critical!)
2. Place in screaming hot pan
3. **SET TIMER FOR 60-90 SECONDS**
4. Sear without moving - should sizzle violently
5. Flip, sear other side 60-90 seconds
6. Add butter, garlic, herbs to pan
7. Tilt pan, baste for 30 seconds
8. 🚨 **CHECK TEMP**: Should be 5-10°F higher than pre-sear
   - Medium-rare target: 130-135°F

### Step 4: Final Rest (5 minutes)
1. Transfer to cutting board
2. Tent with foil
3. **SET TIMER FOR 5 MINUTES**
4. Let rest - temperature will even out

## Serving
- Slice against grain or serve whole
- Top with compound butter if desired
- Serve immediately

## Troubleshooting
- **Oven taking too long?** Temp too low or steak was cold
- **Overcooked in final sear?** Went too high in oven - pull at 115°F next time
- **Not enough crust?** Surface wasn't dry enough - pat more thoroughly
- **Gray band around edges?** Oven temp too high - use 250°F for thicker steaks

## Pro Tips
- This method works for ANY thickness (adjust oven time)
- Can hold steaks after oven for up to 30 min before searing
- Perfect for cooking multiple steaks - sear all at once after oven
