# Broiled Steak (1.5" Thick)

## Overview
Indoor alternative to grilling. Intense top-down heat creates excellent crust. Perfect for winter when grilling isn't an option.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **BROILER TEMPS VARY**: Every broiler is different - watch closely first time
- **POSITION MATTERS**: Too close = burnt outside, raw inside
- **SMOKE ALERT**: Will smoke - exhaust fan on, windows open
- **OVEN DOOR**: Check manual - some leave cracked, others closed
- **HOT PAN**: Broiler pan gets 500°F+ - use oven mitts
- **WATCH CONSTANTLY**: Can burn in seconds under broiler

## Target Internal Temps (at removal)
- Rare: 120°F
- Medium-Rare: 130°F ⭐ **RECOMMENDED**
- Medium: 140°F

## Equipment
- Broiler pan or wire rack over baking sheet
- Instant-read thermometer (MANDATORY)
- Tongs
- Timer
- Oven mitts

## Ingredients (per steak)
- 1 steak, 1.5" thick (12-16 oz)
- Kosher salt (1 tsp per side)
- Fresh cracked black pepper
- 1 tbsp neutral oil (to coat)

## Preparation (40+ minutes before)
1. Remove steak from fridge - reach room temp (40-60 min)
2. Pat DRY thoroughly with paper towels
3. Rub with oil on both sides
4. Season generously with salt and pepper
5. Let sit at room temp

## Oven Setup

### Positioning (CRITICAL)
**For 1.5" steak:**
- Position rack 4-6 inches from broiler element
- Too close (2-3"): Burns outside, raw inside
- Too far (8"): Won't develop crust

### Test Your Broiler First
1. Turn broiler to HIGH
2. Let preheat 10 minutes (MANDATORY)
3. 🚨 Every broiler is different - adjust rack after first attempt

## Method

### Step 1: Preheat Broiler (10 minutes)
1. Move oven rack to position (4-6" from element)
2. Set broiler to HIGH
3. Let preheat 10 full minutes
4. Place broiler pan in oven to preheat (optional but helps sear)
5. 🚨 Turn on exhaust fan and open windows NOW

### Step 2: First Side (5-7 minutes)
1. Pat steak dry one more time
2. Place on broiler pan or wire rack
3. Slide into oven
4. **SET TIMER FOR 4 MINUTES**
5. 🚨 CHECK AT 4 MINUTES - should be browning
6. If pale: move rack closer
7. If charring black: move rack down
8. Continue broiling until deeply browned (5-7 min total)

### Step 3: Flip and Second Side (4-6 minutes)
1. Flip steak with tongs
2. **SET TIMER FOR 4 MINUTES**
3. Insert thermometer through side into center
4. Check temp at 4 minutes
5. 🚨 **PULL WHEN 5-10°F BELOW TARGET**
   - For medium-rare: Remove at 120-125°F
   - Will rise to 130-135°F during rest

### Step 4: Rest (MANDATORY - 8-10 minutes)
1. Transfer to cutting board using oven mitts
2. Tent loosely with foil
3. **SET TIMER FOR 8 MINUTES**
4. Temperature will continue rising

## Serving
- Slice against grain or serve whole
- Top with compound butter to finish

## Troubleshooting
- **Smoking excessively?** Normal but can reduce by trimming excess fat
- **Burning on top?** Rack too close - move to 6" distance
- **Not browning?** Broiler not preheated or rack too far
- **Overcooked?** Pulled too late - carryover cooking is real
- **Smoke detector going off?** Open windows, fan on high - this is high-heat cooking

## Pro Tips
- **Broiler + Reverse Sear**: Roast at 275°F first, finish under broiler
- Pat surface DRY before broiling for better crust
- Some ovens' broilers are bottom drawer - check yours
- Gas broilers pulse on/off - adjust timing accordingly
