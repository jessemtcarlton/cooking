# Grilled/BBQ Steak (1.5" Thick)

## Overview
Two-zone grilling for perfect steakhouse char with controlled doneness. Works on gas or charcoal.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **FLARE-UPS**: Fat dripping = flames - have spray bottle ready
- **GRILL MUST BE HOT**: 500°F+ or you'll steam not sear
- **LID MANAGEMENT**: Close lid for thick steaks or won't cook through
- **TEMP CHECK**: Use thermometer - grill temp varies wildly
- **DON'T PRESS**: Pressing releases juices and causes flare-ups
- **WIND AFFECTS TIMING**: Adjust for weather

## Target Internal Temps (at removal)
- Rare: 120°F
- Medium-Rare: 130°F ⭐ **RECOMMENDED**
- Medium: 140°F

## Equipment
- Gas or charcoal grill
- Instant-read thermometer (MANDATORY)
- Long tongs
- Spray bottle with water (for flare-ups)
- Timer
- Grill brush

## Ingredients (per steak)
- 1 steak, 1.5" thick (12-16 oz)
- Kosher salt (1 tsp per side)
- Fresh cracked black pepper
- 1 tbsp neutral oil (coat steak, not grill)
- Optional: garlic powder, onion powder

## Preparation (40+ minutes before)
1. Remove steak from fridge - reach room temp (40-60 min)
2. Pat DRY with paper towels
3. Rub with oil on both sides
4. Season generously with salt, pepper (and optional spices)
5. Let sit at room temp

## Grill Setup

### Two-Zone Fire (CRITICAL)
**Gas Grill:**
- Half burners on HIGH, half on LOW/OFF
- Preheat 15 minutes minimum
- Target: 500-550°F on hot side

**Charcoal Grill:**
- Pile all coals on one side
- Let coals ash over (white coating)
- 🚨 Wait until coals are FULLY ashed - 20-30 minutes
- Should be able to hold hand over coals for only 2-3 seconds

### Why Two Zones?
- HOT ZONE: Searing, crust development
- COOL ZONE: Finishing, preventing burning

## Method

### Step 1: Clean and Oil Grill
1. Heat grill to target temp
2. Brush grates clean with grill brush
3. 🚨 DO NOT oil grates (causes flames)
4. Oil is already on the steak

### Step 2: Initial Sear (3-4 minutes per side)
1. Place steak on HOT ZONE at 45° angle to grates
2. **SET TIMER FOR 3 MINUTES**
3. 🚨 CLOSE LID (for thick steaks)
4. Don't move, don't press, don't peek
5. If major flare-up: move to cool zone briefly
6. After 3 min, rotate 90° for crosshatch marks
7. **SET TIMER FOR 1-2 MINUTES**
8. Flip to other side
9. Repeat pattern (3 min, rotate, 1-2 min)

### Step 3: Check Temperature
1. Insert thermometer through SIDE into center
2. If below target by more than 15°F: Move to COOL ZONE

### Step 4: Finish on Cool Zone (if needed)
1. Move to indirect heat (cool zone)
2. Close lid
3. Check temp every 2 minutes
4. 🚨 **PULL AT TARGET TEMP MINUS 5°F**
   - For medium-rare: Pull at 125°F
   - Will rise to 130-135°F while resting

### Step 5: Rest (MANDATORY - 8-10 minutes)
1. Transfer to cutting board
2. Tent loosely with foil
3. **SET TIMER FOR 8 MINUTES**
4. 🚨 DO NOT SKIP - lets juices redistribute

## Serving
- Slice against grain if sharing
- Serve immediately with chimichurri or compound butter

## Troubleshooting
- **Burning outside, raw inside?** Grill too hot - use cool zone to finish
- **No char marks?** Grill not hot enough or moved steak too early
- **Stuck to grates?** Not ready to flip - wait 30 more seconds
- **Constant flare-ups?** Too much oil or fat cap - spray with water
- **Dry/overcooked?** Didn't use thermometer or cooked over direct heat entire time

## Pro Tips
- For extra smoke flavor (charcoal): Add wood chips
- **Reverse sear on grill**: Start on cool zone, finish on hot
- Don't flip more than once (unless controlling flare-ups)
- Wind increases cooking time - add 2-3 minutes
