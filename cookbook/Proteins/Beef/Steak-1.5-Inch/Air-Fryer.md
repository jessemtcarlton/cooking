# Air Fryer Steak (1.5" Thick)

## Overview
Surprisingly effective for steak. High-velocity hot air creates crust without smoking up your kitchen. Perfect for apartments or hot summer days.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **NOT ALL AIR FRYERS ARE EQUAL**: Temps vary 25-50°F between models
- **MUST PREHEAT**: Cold air fryer = steamed steak
- **SIZE LIMITS**: Steak must fit without touching sides
- **CHECK EARLY**: Air fryers cook fast - check at minimum time
- **CARRYOVER COOKING**: Pulls 5-10°F below target
- **FIRST TIME?**: Watch closely - every model differs

## Target Internal Temps (at removal)
- Rare: 120°F
- Medium-Rare: 130°F ⭐ **RECOMMENDED**
- Medium: 140°F

## Equipment
- Air fryer (basket or oven style)
- Instant-read thermometer (MANDATORY)
- Tongs
- Timer

## Ingredients (per steak)
- 1 steak, 1.5" thick (12-16 oz)
- Kosher salt (1 tsp per side)
- Fresh cracked black pepper
- 1 tsp oil (very light coat)
- Optional: garlic powder, smoked paprika

## Preparation (40+ minutes before)
1. Remove steak from fridge - reach room temp (40-60 min)
2. Pat DRY with paper towels (moisture = no crust)
3. Rub with tiny amount of oil
4. Season generously with salt, pepper, optional spices
5. Let sit at room temp

## Method

### Step 1: Preheat Air Fryer (CRITICAL)
1. Preheat air fryer to 400°F
2. Let run empty for 5 full minutes
3. 🚨 DO NOT SKIP PREHEATING or steak will steam

### Step 2: First Side (6 minutes)
1. Pat steak dry ONE MORE TIME
2. Place in air fryer basket (not touching sides)
3. Set to 400°F
4. **SET TIMER FOR 6 MINUTES**
5. Don't open during cooking

### Step 3: Flip and Continue (5-7 minutes)
1. Flip steak with tongs
2. **SET TIMER FOR 5 MINUTES**
3. At 5 minutes: Check internal temp through SIDE
4. 🚨 **TARGET TEMPS (before removal):**
   - Rare: Pull at 115°F
   - Medium-rare: Pull at 120-125°F
   - Medium: Pull at 135°F
5. If not at temp, continue cooking in 2-min increments
6. Check temp every 2 minutes

### Step 4: Optional High-Heat Sear (1-2 minutes)
For extra crust:
1. When internal temp is 10°F below target
2. Increase air fryer to 450°F (if your model allows)
3. Cook 1-2 minutes per side
4. 🚨 WATCH CLOSELY - can burn fast

### Step 5: Rest (MANDATORY - 8-10 minutes)
1. Transfer to cutting board
2. Tent loosely with foil
3. **SET TIMER FOR 8 MINUTES**
4. Temp will rise 5-10°F during rest

## Serving
- Slice against grain or serve whole
- Top with compound butter

## Air Fryer Model Adjustments

### Basket-Style (Ninja, Cosori, etc.)
- Use temps as written above
- Single layer only

### Oven-Style (Breville, Cuisinart, etc.)
- Use middle rack position
- May need to increase time by 2-3 minutes
- Can cook multiple steaks

### Small/Compact Models
- Reduce temp to 375°F
- Increase time by 3-4 minutes
- Only fits smaller steaks

## Troubleshooting
- **No crust?** Surface too wet or air fryer not preheated
- **Overcooked?** Air fryer runs hot - reduce temp by 25°F next time
- **Undercooked?** Air fryer runs cool - increase temp by 25°F
- **Uneven cooking?** Steak too thick for air fryer or touching sides
- **Smoke?** Normal if fatty steak - some smoke acceptable

## Pro Tips
- **Air Fryer Reverse Sear**: Cook at 250°F until 115°F, then blast at 450°F to finish
- Leave space around steak for air circulation
- Don't overcrowd basket
- Can cook from frozen: Add 5-7 minutes per side
- Write down YOUR air fryer times/temps first time and keep notes
