# Pan-Seared Steak (1.5" Thick)

## Overview
Perfect for ribeye, NY strip, or filet mignon. Restaurant-quality crust with butter basting.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **TEMP CHECK**: Use meat thermometer - no exceptions!
- **SMOKE ALERT**: Will smoke heavily - turn on exhaust fan BEFORE starting
- **TIMING**: This will take 8-10 minutes total - set timer NOW
- **DON'T FLIP**: Resist urge to flip before 4 minutes or crust won't form
- **CARRYOVER COOKING**: Steak rises 5-10°F after removal - pull early!

## Target Temps (BEFORE resting)
- Rare: 120°F
- Medium-Rare: 130°F ⭐ **RECOMMENDED**
- Medium: 140°F
- Medium-Well: 150°F (Don't do this to expensive steak)

## Equipment
- Heavy cast iron or stainless steel skillet
- Instant-read thermometer (MANDATORY)
- Tongs (not fork - don't pierce meat)
- Timer

## Ingredients (per steak)
- 1 steak, 1.5" thick (12-16 oz)
- 2 tbsp high smoke-point oil (avocado or grapeseed)
- Kosher salt (1 tsp per side)
- Fresh cracked black pepper
- 2 tbsp butter
- 2 garlic cloves, smashed
- 2 sprigs fresh thyme or rosemary

## Preparation (DO THIS 40+ MINUTES BEFORE COOKING)
1. Remove steak from fridge - must reach room temp (40-60 min)
2. Pat DRY with paper towels (moisture = no crust)
3. Season generously with salt on both sides
4. Let sit uncovered on plate

## Method

### Step 1: Preheat (5 minutes before cooking)
- Heat skillet over HIGH heat for 5 full minutes
- 🚨 Turn on exhaust fan NOW
- Add oil - should shimmer immediately
- If oil smokes instantly, it's ready

### Step 2: Sear First Side (4 minutes)
- Pat steak dry ONE MORE TIME
- Season with pepper (both sides)
- Lay steak away from you (avoid splash)
- **SET TIMER FOR 4 MINUTES**
- 🚨 DO NOT TOUCH OR MOVE STEAK
- Should hear aggressive sizzle - if not, heat wasn't high enough

### Step 3: Flip and Sear (3-4 minutes)
- Check bottom - should be deep brown crust
- Flip once with tongs
- **SET TIMER FOR 3 MINUTES**
- Add butter, garlic, thyme to pan
- 🚨 WATCH BUTTER - will burn if too hot, reduce to medium-high

### Step 4: Butter Baste (1-2 minutes)
- Tilt pan, spoon melted butter over steak continuously
- Insert thermometer through SIDE of steak into center
- **PULL AT TARGET TEMP MINUS 5°F**
- 🚨 For medium-rare, pull at 125°F (will reach 130-135°F resting)

### Step 5: Rest (MANDATORY - 8-10 minutes)
- Transfer to cutting board or plate
- Tent loosely with foil
- 🚨 DO NOT SKIP RESTING - juices need to redistribute
- **SET TIMER FOR 8 MINUTES**

## Serving
- Slice against the grain if sharing
- Spoon pan butter/garlic over top
- Serve immediately

## Troubleshooting
- **No crust?** Steak was too wet or pan not hot enough
- **Steak stuck to pan?** Not ready to flip - wait 30 more seconds
- **Smoke detector going off?** Normal - open windows, this is high-heat cooking
- **Overcooked?** Use thermometer next time - no guessing allowed
