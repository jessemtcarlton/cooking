# Perfect Pan-Seared Chicken Breast

## Overview
The most commonly overcooked protein in home kitchens. This method gives juicy, tender chicken with golden crust every time. The secret: pounding to even thickness and pulling at 160°F (not 165°F).

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **MUST POUND EVEN**: Thick end overcooks before thin end cooks
- **PULL AT 160°F**: Carryover brings to 165°F (safe) - cooking to 165°F = dry
- **DON'T SKIP BRINING**: Even 15 minutes makes huge difference
- **ONE FLIP ONLY**: Multiple flips = dry, no crust
- **REST IS MANDATORY**: Cut immediately = all juices run out

## Target Temperature
- **Pull at**: 160°F (71°C) internal temp
- **After resting**: 165°F (74°C) - USDA safe temp
- **165°F is the TARGET, not cooking temp** - carryover gets you there

## Equipment
- Meat mallet or rolling pin
- Plastic wrap or zip-top bag
- Large skillet (cast iron or stainless)
- Instant-read thermometer
- Tongs
- Timer

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- 4 boneless, skinless chicken breasts (6-8 oz each)
- 4 cups (960ml) water
- 1/4 cup (72g) kosher salt (for brine)
- 2 tbsp (30g) sugar (for brine)
- 2 tbsp (30ml) neutral oil (avocado or vegetable)
- 2 tbsp (28g) butter
- 1 tsp (5g) kosher salt (for seasoning)
- 1/2 tsp (2g) black pepper
- 1 tsp (2g) garlic powder (optional)
- 1 tsp (2g) paprika (optional)

## Method

### Step 1: Brine the Chicken (15 minutes minimum, up to 2 hours)
1. Dissolve 1/4 cup (72g) salt + 2 tbsp (30g) sugar in 4 cups (960ml) cold water
2. Submerge chicken breasts in brine
3. Refrigerate 15 minutes minimum (30-60 minutes ideal)
4. 🚨 **WHY IT MATTERS:**
   - Adds moisture (chicken absorbs brine)
   - Seasons throughout (not just surface)
   - Changes protein structure (stays tender when cooked)
   - Makes almost impossible to dry out

**Timing:**
- **Minimum**: 15 minutes (noticeable improvement)
- **Optimal**: 30-60 minutes (juicy, well-seasoned)
- **Maximum**: 2 hours (longer can get too salty)

### Step 2: Pound to Even Thickness (CRITICAL)
1. Remove chicken from brine, rinse briefly, pat DRY
2. Place one breast in zip-top bag or between plastic wrap
3. Using flat side of meat mallet (or rolling pin):
   - Pound thick end to match thin end
   - Target: 3/4" (2cm) even thickness throughout
4. 🚨 **THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT STEP**
   - Uneven thickness = thick part raw when thin part is overcooked
   - Even thickness = cooks uniformly, perfect doneness
5. Repeat with all breasts

### Step 3: Season and Rest (10-40 minutes)
1. Pat chicken DRY again with paper towels
2. Season both sides:
   - 1/4 tsp (1.5g) kosher salt per breast
   - Black pepper
   - Optional: garlic powder, paprika
3. Let sit at room temp 10-40 minutes
4. Pat dry one more time before cooking (moisture = no crust)

### Step 4: Preheat Pan (5 minutes before cooking)
1. Heat skillet over MEDIUM-HIGH heat
2. Let heat 4-5 minutes (must be hot)
3. Add 2 tbsp (30ml) oil
4. Oil should shimmer but not smoke
5. Tilt pan to coat

### Step 5: Sear First Side (5-7 minutes)
1. Pat chicken dry ONE FINAL TIME
2. Place chicken smooth-side DOWN in pan
3. Should sizzle immediately - if not, pan not hot enough
4. 🚨 **DON'T MOVE** - let crust form
5. **SET TIMER FOR 6 MINUTES** (for 3/4" thickness)
6. Resist urge to peek or move
7. Reduce heat to MEDIUM after 2 minutes (prevents burning)

### Step 6: Flip and Finish (5-7 minutes)
1. After 6 minutes, chicken should release easily
2. Flip with tongs (should have golden-brown crust)
3. 🚨 **FLIP ONLY ONCE**
4. Add 2 tbsp (28g) butter to pan
5. **SET TIMER FOR 5 MINUTES**
6. Tilt pan, baste chicken with butter
7. At 5 minutes, check internal temp:
   - Insert thermometer through SIDE into thickest part
   - Looking for 160°F (71°C)
   - If not there, cook 1-2 more minutes

### Step 7: Rest (MANDATORY - 5-8 minutes)
1. When internal temp hits 160°F (71°C), remove from pan
2. Transfer to plate or cutting board
3. Tent loosely with foil
4. **SET TIMER FOR 5 MINUTES**
5. 🚨 **MUST REST**:
   - Temp rises to 165°F (safe)
   - Juices redistribute (not on cutting board)
   - Carryover cooking finishes

### Step 8: Slice and Serve
1. After resting, check temp: Should be 165°F (74°C)
2. Slice on diagonal (looks nicer, more tender)
3. Serve immediately
4. Any accumulated juices = spoon over chicken

## Perfect Chicken Breast Indicators
- Internal temp: 165°F (74°C) after resting
- Golden-brown crust both sides
- Juices run clear (not pink) when cut
- Moist interior (not dry)
- Tender texture (not rubbery)

## Timing for Different Thicknesses

| Thickness | Side 1 | Side 2 | Total Time |
|-----------|--------|--------|------------|
| 1/2" (1.3cm) | 4-5 min | 3-4 min | 7-9 min |
| 3/4" (2cm) | 6 min | 5-6 min | 11-12 min |
| 1" (2.5cm) | 7-8 min | 6-7 min | 13-15 min |

**Always verify with thermometer** - these are estimates

## Troubleshooting

**Dry chicken:**
- Cooked past 165°F (pull at 160°F next time)
- Didn't brine (adds moisture buffer)
- Cooked too long
- Chicken breast was too thick (uneven cooking)

**Not golden brown:**
- Pan not hot enough
- Chicken too wet (didn't pat dry)
- Moved chicken before crust formed
- Heat too low

**Raw in middle, burned outside:**
- Heat too high (reduce to medium after initial sear)
- Chicken too thick (pound to 3/4")
- Didn't use thermometer

**Stuck to pan:**
- Pan not hot enough
- Not ready to flip (wait 30 more seconds, will release when crust forms)
- Pan not well-seasoned (if cast iron)

## Quick Brine (if no time)
**15-Minute Emergency Brine:**
1. Double salt concentration: 1/2 cup (144g) salt + 4 tbsp (60g) sugar per 4 cups water
2. Brine only 15 minutes
3. Rinse well (more salty surface)
4. Still better than no brine

**Dry Brine (if even less time):**
1. Salt chicken all over (both sides)
2. Refrigerate uncovered 1-24 hours
3. Pat dry, season, cook
4. Salt draws out moisture, then reabsorbs (self-brine)

## Serving Suggestions

**As Is:**
- Slice and serve over salad
- Serve whole with sides
- Slice for sandwiches/wraps

**With Pan Sauce:**
After removing chicken, make quick sauce:
1. Add 1/4 cup (60ml) white wine to hot pan
2. Scrape up brown bits
3. Add 1/2 cup (120ml) chicken stock
4. Reduce by half
5. Mount with 1 tbsp (14g) butter
6. Add lemon juice, herbs
7. Spoon over chicken

## Pro Tips
- **Buy air-chilled** chicken if possible (better texture, less water added)
- **Save brines** in labeled freezer bags
- **Butterfly very thick breasts** instead of pounding (cut horizontally to create 2 thin cutlets)
- **Use meat thermometer** - no exceptions, only way to guarantee doneness
- **Brining math**: 6% salt solution = 1/4 cup salt per 4 cups water
- **Add aromatics to brine**: garlic, peppercorns, herbs (more flavor)
- **Can use buttermilk** instead of brine (acid tenderizes)
- **Freeze in brine** for meal prep (thaws pre-brined)

## Food Safety Notes
- **USDA safe temp**: 165°F (74°C) internal
- **Instant kill**: Bacteria dies instantly at 165°F
- **Pasteurization**: Can achieve safety at lower temps with time:
  - 150°F (66°C) for 3 minutes
  - 155°F (68°C) for 47 seconds
  - 160°F (71°C) for 15 seconds
  - 165°F (74°C) instantly
- Our method (pull at 160°F, rest to 165°F) is safe AND juicy
