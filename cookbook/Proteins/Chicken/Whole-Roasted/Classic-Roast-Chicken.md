# Perfect Whole Roast Chicken

## Overview
The ultimate test of a cook. Perfect roast chicken has crispy skin, juicy breast (165°F), and fully cooked thighs (175°F). The secret: high heat start, lower heat finish, and understanding white vs dark meat temps.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **TWO TARGET TEMPS**: Breast 160°F (pulls to 165°F), Thigh 175°F minimum
- **TRUSSING MATTERS**: Untrussed = overcooked breast before thighs done
- **DRY SKIN CRITICAL**: Wet skin = rubbery, not crispy
- **REST 15 MINUTES**: Carving immediately = all juices on board
- **PAN WILL SMOKE**: High heat start = smoky kitchen, exhaust fan mandatory

## Target Temperatures
- **Breast**: Pull at 160°F (71°C), rests to 165°F (74°C)
- **Thigh**: 175-180°F (79-82°C) - higher temp = more tender dark meat
- **Thickest part of thigh** is last to cook - check there

## Equipment
- Roasting pan with rack (V-rack ideal)
- Kitchen twine (for trussing)
- Instant-read thermometer
- Paper towels
- Basting brush (optional)
- Timer

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- 1 whole chicken, 4-5 lbs (1.8-2.3kg)
- 2 tbsp (30ml) neutral oil or melted butter
- 1 tbsp (15g) kosher salt
- 1 tsp (4g) black pepper
- 1 tsp (2g) garlic powder
- 1 tsp (2g) paprika
- 1 lemon, halved
- 1 head garlic, halved crosswise
- 4-5 sprigs fresh thyme or rosemary
- 1 onion, quartered (for roasting pan)
- 2 carrots, roughly chopped (for roasting pan)
- 2 celery stalks, roughly chopped (for roasting pan)

## Method

### Step 1: Dry the Chicken (1-24 hours before - CRITICAL)
1. Remove chicken from packaging
2. Remove giblets from cavity (save for stock)
3. Pat DRY inside and out with paper towels
4. 🚨 **CRITICAL FOR CRISPY SKIN:**
   - Place on wire rack over plate
   - Refrigerate UNCOVERED 1-24 hours
   - Air-drying = crispy skin
   - Minimum 1 hour if no time
   - 24 hours = crispiest possible skin
5. Pat dry again before cooking

### Step 2: Bring to Room Temp (1 hour before)
1. Remove from fridge
2. Let sit at room temp 45-60 minutes
3. Ensures even cooking (cold chicken = raw inside when skin is done)

### Step 3: Truss the Chicken (prevents overcooked breast)
1. Cut 3 feet of kitchen twine
2. Place chicken breast-up
3. Tuck wing tips behind back
4. Cross drumsticks, tie together with twine
5. Wrap twine around chicken, tie tight under breast
6. 🚨 **WHY TRUSSING MATTERS:**
   - Compact shape = even cooking
   - Legs protect breast (breast doesn't overcook)
   - Professional appearance
   - Without trussing: breast overcooks before thighs done

**Quick Truss Method:**
- Cross drumsticks
- Tie together tight
- Tuck wings back
- Good enough if no time for full truss

### Step 4: Season (30 minutes before roasting)
1. Rub 2 tbsp (30ml) oil or melted butter all over
2. Season generously with:
   - 1 tbsp (15g) kosher salt (all surfaces)
   - 1 tsp (4g) black pepper
   - 1 tsp (2g) garlic powder
   - 1 tsp (2g) paprika
3. Stuff cavity with:
   - Lemon halves
   - Garlic head halves
   - Thyme/rosemary sprigs
4. Let sit 30 minutes (salt penetrates)

### Step 5: Prep Roasting Pan
1. Scatter vegetables in roasting pan:
   - 1 onion, quartered
   - 2 carrots, chopped
   - 2 celery stalks, chopped
2. Add 1 cup (240ml) water to pan
3. Place V-rack or regular rack over vegetables
4. Vegetables form aromatic base, prevent burning drippings

### Step 6: High-Heat Start (15 minutes at 450°F)
1. Preheat oven to 450°F (232°C)
2. Place chicken breast-UP on rack
3. 🚨 **Turn on exhaust fan - will smoke**
4. Roast 15 minutes at 450°F (232°C)
5. **SET TIMER FOR 15 MINUTES**
6. High heat jump-starts browning, renders fat
7. DO NOT OPEN OVEN

### Step 7: Lower Heat and Continue (45-75 minutes)
1. After 15 minutes, reduce oven to 375°F (190°C)
2. Continue roasting:
   - 4 lb chicken: 45-60 minutes more
   - 5 lb chicken: 60-75 minutes more
3. **SET TIMER FOR 45 MINUTES**, check then
4. Chicken is done when:
   - Breast temp: 160°F (71°C)
   - Thigh temp: 175-180°F (79-82°C)
   - Juices run clear (not pink) when thigh pierced
   - Legs wiggle easily in socket

### Step 8: Check Temperature (at timer)
1. Insert thermometer in THICKEST part of thigh (avoid bone)
2. Check breast too (insert from side into thickest part)
3. 🚨 **BOTH must be at temp:**
   - Thigh: 175°F+ (79°C+)
   - Breast: 160°F (71°C)
4. If not there:
   - Continue roasting
   - Check every 10 minutes
   - If breast hits 160°F but thighs aren't 175°F:
     - Cover breast loosely with foil
     - Continue roasting until thighs reach 175°F

### Step 9: Rest (MANDATORY - 15-20 minutes)
1. Remove from oven when both temps reached
2. Transfer to cutting board (tilt to drain cavity juices into pan)
3. Tent loosely with foil
4. **SET TIMER FOR 15 MINUTES**
5. 🚨 **DO NOT SKIP RESTING:**
   - Temps equalize (breast rises to 165°F safe temp)
   - Juices redistribute
   - Easier to carve
   - Carving immediately = dry chicken, juices on board

### Step 10: Make Pan Gravy (during rest - optional)
1. Pour pan drippings through strainer into fat separator
2. Let fat rise (2-3 minutes)
3. Pour drippings from bottom (fat stays in top)
4. Return drippings to pan over medium heat
5. Add 2 cups (480ml) chicken stock
6. Scrape up browned bits
7. Simmer, reduce by half (10 minutes)
8. Optional: Thicken with roux or cornstarch slurry
9. Season with salt, pepper

### Step 11: Carve and Serve
1. Remove twine
2. Cut legs off at joint
3. Separate thighs from drumsticks
4. Remove breasts:
   - Cut along breastbone
   - Follow ribcage down
   - Remove whole breast
5. Slice breast against grain
6. Arrange on platter
7. Serve with pan gravy

## Total Cooking Time by Weight

| Weight | High Heat (450°F) | Low Heat (375°F) | Total Time |
|--------|-------------------|------------------|------------|
| 3-4 lbs | 15 min | 40-50 min | 55-65 min |
| 4-5 lbs | 15 min | 50-65 min | 65-80 min |
| 5-6 lbs | 15 min | 65-80 min | 80-95 min |

**Always verify with thermometer** - ovens vary

## Perfect Roast Chicken Indicators
- **Skin**: Golden brown, crispy (not rubbery)
- **Breast**: 165°F (74°C), juicy, white meat
- **Thigh**: 175-180°F (79-82°C), tender, dark meat
- **Juices**: Run clear (not pink) when thigh pierced
- **Legs**: Wiggle easily in socket

## Troubleshooting

**Dry breast meat:**
- Cooked past 165°F (pull breast at 160°F)
- Didn't truss (breast overexposed)
- Didn't rest
- Started from cold (not room temp)

**Undercooked thighs:**
- Pulled too early (need 175°F minimum)
- Chicken was cold when started
- Oven temp too low

**Rubbery skin:**
- Skin wasn't dry (must air-dry 1+ hours)
- Oven temp too low
- Too much liquid in pan (steaming)

**Uneven cooking:**
- Didn't truss
- Started from cold
- Oven hot spots (rotate pan halfway through)

**Burnt vegetables in pan:**
- Not enough liquid in pan (add more water)
- Oven too hot
- Not a problem if gravy tastes good

## Flavor Variations

### Herb Butter Under Skin
1. Mix 4 tbsp (56g) softened butter + 2 tbsp minced herbs
2. Gently separate skin from breast (use fingers)
3. Spread herb butter under skin
4. Roast as directed
5. Results: Ultra-moist breast, herby flavor

### Lemon-Herb
- Stuff with lemon, garlic, herbs (as written)
- Add lemon zest to salt rub

### Garlic-Rosemary
- Stuff with whole garlic head + rosemary
- Rub with garlic-infused oil

### Spatchcock/Butterflied (faster, more even)
- Remove backbone with kitchen shears
- Press flat
- Roast 45-55 minutes at 425°F (no temp change)
- More even cooking but less dramatic presentation

## Pro Tips
- **Air-dry overnight** = crispiest skin possible
- **Brine for 4 hours** before air-drying = juiciest chicken
- **Use probe thermometer** that stays in while cooking (set alarm for 160°F)
- **Rotate pan** halfway through if oven has hot spots
- **Save carcass** for chicken stock (adds to freezer bag)
- **Best chicken** = organic, air-chilled, free-range (tastes better, less water)
- **V-rack** > flat rack (better air circulation)
- **Tent with foil** if browning too fast
- **Don't baste** (opens oven, drops temp, makes skin less crispy)

## Food Safety
- **Don't wash chicken** (spreads bacteria via splashing)
- **Sanitize all surfaces** that touched raw chicken
- **Breast: 165°F safe** (our method gets there via rest)
- **Thigh: 165°F safe** but better at 175°F (dark meat benefits from higher temp)
- **Use separate cutting board** for raw chicken
