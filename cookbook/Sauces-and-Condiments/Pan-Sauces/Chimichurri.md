# Chimichurri Sauce

## Overview
Argentinian herb sauce - bright, garlicky, acidic. Perfect for grilled meats. Foolproof and makes everything taste like high-end steakhouse.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **MAKE AHEAD**: Tastes better after sitting 2+ hours (flavors meld)
- **DON'T BLEND**: Should be chopped, not pureed - texture matters
- **ACID BALANCE**: Too much vinegar = harsh, too little = flat
- **GARLIC STRENGTH**: Raw garlic gets stronger over time
- **KEEPS 2 WEEKS**: Refrigerated - flavor improves over first 3 days

## Equipment
- Chef's knife and cutting board OR food processor
- Measuring cups/spoons
- Jar or container with lid
- Spoon for stirring

## Ingredients (makes ~1.5 cups)
- 1 cup fresh parsley, packed (flat-leaf preferred)
- 1/3 cup fresh oregano (or 2 tbsp dried)
- 6-8 garlic cloves, minced
- 2/3 cup extra virgin olive oil (good quality)
- 1/4 cup red wine vinegar
- 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes (adjust to taste)
- Optional: 1 tsp ground cumin

## Method

### Step 1: Prep Herbs
**By Hand (Traditional - BEST texture):**
1. Remove thick stems from parsley
2. Chop parsley very finely
3. Chop oregano finely
4. Mince garlic to paste

**With Food Processor (Faster):**
1. Pulse parsley and oregano until finely chopped
2. Don't over-process - want texture, not paste
3. 🚨 **PULSE, DON'T BLEND** - 10-15 short pulses max
4. Add garlic, pulse 3-4 more times

### Step 2: Combine All Ingredients
1. Place chopped herbs in bowl or jar
2. Add minced garlic
3. Add olive oil
4. Add red wine vinegar
5. Add lemon juice
6. Add salt, pepper, red pepper flakes
7. Stir well to combine

### Step 3: Rest (CRITICAL FOR FLAVOR)
1. Cover and let sit at room temp 2 hours minimum
2. **Better after 12-24 hours** in fridge
3. 🚨 **FLAVOR DEVELOPS OVER TIME**
4. Garlic mellows, herbs infuse oil

### Step 4: Adjust Seasoning
Before serving:
1. Taste sauce
2. Adjust salt (may need more)
3. Too thick? Add more oil
4. Too oily? Add vinegar or lemon
5. Want spicier? Add red pepper flakes
6. Should be: Bright, garlicky, slightly spicy, herbaceous

## Using Chimichurri
- Spoon over grilled steak
- Marinade for chicken or fish (2-4 hours)
- Drizzle over roasted vegetables
- Mix with mayo for sandwich spread
- Toss with roasted potatoes

## Variations

### Chimichurri Rojo (Red)
- Add 1 roasted red bell pepper, minced
- Add 1 tsp smoked paprika
- Slightly sweeter

### Cilantro Chimichurri
- Replace parsley with cilantro
- Add juice of 1 lime instead of lemon
- More Mexican flavor profile

### Mild Version
- Reduce garlic to 3-4 cloves
- Omit red pepper flakes
- Add 1 tsp honey for sweetness

### Extra Garlicky
- Increase garlic to 10 cloves
- Let sit 24 hours before using

## Storage
- **Room temp**: Day of making only (2-4 hours max)
- **Refrigerated**: 2 weeks in airtight jar
- **Frozen**: 3 months (texture changes slightly)
- Bring to room temp 30 min before serving

## Troubleshooting
- **Too garlicky?** Let sit longer (mellows) or add more oil/herbs
- **Too acidic?** Add more oil or pinch of sugar
- **Too oily?** Add more vinegar and herbs
- **Bitter?** Over-processed or old herbs - make fresh
- **Bland?** Needs more salt and vinegar

## Pro Tips
- Use FRESH herbs - dried oregano OK, dried parsley is not
- Quality olive oil matters - you'll taste it
- Shake/stir before each use (ingredients settle)
- Save herb stems for stock
- Double the batch - it keeps well and gets better with age
- Best at room temp (take out 30 min before serving)

## Traditional Argentine Serving
- Serve in small bowl alongside meat
- Let guests spoon over their steak
- Always have extra - it's addictive
