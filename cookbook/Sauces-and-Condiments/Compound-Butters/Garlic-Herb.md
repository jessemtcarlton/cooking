# Garlic Herb Compound Butter

## Overview
Restaurant secret for instant flavor. Make ahead, slice, top anything hot. Melts into perfect sauce.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **BUTTER MUST BE SOFT**: Not melted, not cold - room temp for 1+ hour
- **FREEZE LEFTOVERS**: Keeps 3 months frozen, only 1 week in fridge
- **RATIO MATTERS**: Too much garlic = bitter, too little = bland
- **RAW GARLIC**: Strong flavor - use less if sensitive

## Equipment
- Mixing bowl
- Fork or spatula
- Plastic wrap or parchment paper
- Microplane or garlic press

## Ingredients (makes 1/2 lb log)
- 1/2 lb (2 sticks) unsalted butter, softened
- 4 garlic cloves, minced or pressed
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp fresh thyme leaves
- 1 tbsp fresh chives, minced
- 1 tsp lemon zest
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- Optional: 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes

## Method

### Step 1: Soften Butter (1 hour before)
1. Remove butter from fridge
2. Let sit at room temp 1 hour
3. Should be soft enough to press finger in easily
4. 🚨 **NOT MELTED** - if melted, won't come together right

### Step 2: Prep Herbs and Garlic
1. Mince all herbs very finely
2. Press or mince garlic to paste
3. Zest lemon (avoid white pith)
4. Have all ingredients ready

### Step 3: Mix (2 minutes)
1. Place soft butter in bowl
2. Add all ingredients
3. Mix with fork or spatula until fully combined
4. Should be uniform green color throughout
5. 🚨 **TASTE**: Should be flavorful - add more salt if needed

### Step 4: Form Log
1. Place butter on plastic wrap or parchment
2. Shape into log about 1.5" diameter
3. Roll tightly in wrap
4. Twist ends to seal (like candy wrapper)
5. Roll on counter to even out shape

### Step 5: Chill
**For immediate use (30 min):**
- Refrigerate 30 min until firm

**For storage (3 months):**
- Refrigerate 1 hour until firm
- Transfer to freezer in freezer bag
- Label with date

## Using Compound Butter
- Slice into 1/2" rounds
- Place on hot steak, chicken, fish immediately
- Will melt into instant pan sauce
- Use for:
  - Topping steaks
  - Basting vegetables
  - Finishing sauces
  - Garlic bread
  - Tossing with pasta

## Variations

### Blue Cheese Butter
- Add 1/4 cup crumbled blue cheese
- Reduce salt to 1/4 tsp
- Perfect with steak

### Lemon-Dill (for fish)
- Replace herbs with 3 tbsp fresh dill
- Increase lemon zest to 2 tsp
- Add 1 tsp lemon juice

### Cajun
- Add 1 tbsp Cajun seasoning
- Reduce salt (Cajun is salty)
- Great for shrimp

### Truffle
- Add 1 tsp truffle oil
- Reduce herbs to 1 tbsp total
- Fancy steakhouse style

## Storage
- **Fridge**: 1 week in airtight container
- **Freezer**: 3 months wrapped tightly
- **From frozen**: Slice while frozen, use immediately

## Troubleshooting
- **Won't come together?** Butter too cold - let warm up
- **Separating/oily?** Butter was melted - chill and try again
- **Too garlicky?** Use roasted garlic next time (milder)
- **Not flavorful enough?** Needs more salt or herbs

## Pro Tips
- Make multiple batches, freeze in logs
- Can mix in stand mixer (paddle attachment)
- Press into small ramekins for spreading
- Slice frozen with hot knife for clean cuts
- Write flavor/date on wrap before freezing
