# Classic Crème Brûlée

## Overview
Silky custard with crackling caramelized sugar top. Easier than it looks. The secret: low oven temp and water bath.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **WATER BATH MANDATORY**: No water bath = curdled, eggy custard
- **LOW TEMP CRITICAL**: Too hot = grainy, curdled custard
- **WATCH CLOSELY**: Overbaked by 5 minutes = scrambled eggs
- **CHILL FULLY**: Must be cold before torching or sugar won't caramelize
- **TORCH TECHNIQUE**: Too close = burnt, too far = melted sugar not caramelized
- **TIMING**: Needs 4+ hours chilling - make day ahead ideal

## Equipment
- 4-6 ramekins (6 oz shallow ramekins best)
- Large roasting pan (for water bath)
- Kitchen torch (MANDATORY for proper brûlée)
- Fine mesh strainer
- Whisk
- Measuring cups
- Kettle for boiling water

## Ingredients (serves 6)

**Custard:**
- 2 cups heavy cream
- 1 vanilla bean (or 2 tsp vanilla extract)
- 5 egg yolks (save whites for soufflé!)
- 1/3 cup granulated sugar
- Pinch of salt

**For Topping:**
- 6 tbsp granulated sugar (or superfine sugar)

## Method

### Step 1: Infuse Cream (15 minutes)
1. Preheat oven to 325°F
2. Pour cream into saucepan
3. Split vanilla bean, scrape seeds
4. Add pod and seeds to cream (or extract if using)
5. Heat over medium until just steaming (NOT boiling)
6. Remove from heat
7. Let steep 15 minutes
8. Remove vanilla pod

### Step 2: Make Custard Base
1. In bowl, whisk egg yolks and 1/3 cup sugar
2. Whisk until pale yellow and slightly thick
3. 🚨 **TEMPER THE EGGS** (prevents scrambling):
   - Very slowly drizzle warm cream into yolks
   - Whisk constantly
   - Add cream little by little
   - If added too fast, eggs will scramble
4. Add pinch of salt
5. Strain mixture through fine mesh strainer
6. This removes any cooked egg bits and vanilla pod pieces

### Step 3: Prepare Water Bath
1. Place ramekins in large roasting pan
2. Pour custard into ramekins (fill 3/4 full)
3. 🚨 **SKIM BUBBLES** from tops with spoon
4. Boil water in kettle
5. Pull oven rack out slightly
6. Place roasting pan on rack
7. Carefully pour hot water into pan around ramekins
8. Water should come halfway up sides of ramekins
9. 🚨 **DON'T SPLASH WATER INTO CUSTARDS**

### Step 4: Bake (35-45 minutes)
1. Carefully slide rack into oven
2. **SET TIMER FOR 35 MINUTES**
3. 🚨 **CHECK DONENESS**:
   - Edges should be set
   - Center should jiggle like Jell-O when gently shaken
   - Should NOT be liquid in center
   - Should NOT be fully firm
4. If still very jiggly, bake 5 more minutes
5. Check every 5 minutes after that
6. 🚨 **BETTER SLIGHTLY UNDER THAN OVER**

### Step 5: Cool (Critical!)
1. Remove roasting pan from oven carefully
2. Let custards sit in water bath 30 minutes
3. Remove from water bath
4. Let cool to room temp (1 hour)
5. Cover each with plastic wrap
6. Refrigerate minimum 4 hours (overnight is best)
7. 🚨 **MUST BE COMPLETELY COLD** before brûlée-ing

### Step 6: Brûlée the Top (right before serving)
1. Remove custards from fridge
2. Blot any condensation from tops with paper towel
3. Sprinkle 1-2 tsp sugar evenly over each
4. Tilt and tap to distribute evenly
5. 🚨 **TORCH TECHNIQUE**:
   - Hold torch 3-4 inches from surface
   - Keep moving in circular motion
   - Sugar will melt, bubble, then caramelize
   - Should turn golden-brown (not black)
   - Watch closely - happens fast
6. Let sugar harden (2-3 minutes)
7. Serve within 30 minutes (sugar softens over time)

## Perfect Crème Brûlée Indicators

**Custard:**
- Silky smooth (not grainy)
- Just set (jiggles but doesn't slosh)
- Creamy yellow color
- No bubbles or cracks

**Brûlée Top:**
- Even golden-brown color
- Crackles when tapped with spoon
- Thin layer (not thick)
- No burnt spots

## Serving
- Serve in ramekins on small plates
- Can garnish with berries
- Provide small spoon
- Crack through sugar with spoon

## Troubleshooting
- **Grainy/curdled?** Oven too hot or overbaked
- **Eggy taste?** Overcooked or didn't strain
- **Won't set?** Underbaked or not enough egg yolks
- **Cracked top?** Oven too hot or baked without water bath
- **Sugar won't caramelize?** Custard not cold or too much moisture on top
- **Sugar burned?** Torch too close or held in one spot

## Without a Torch (Broiler Method)
1. Preheat broiler to HIGH
2. Place rack 2-3 inches from element
3. Place cold ramekins on baking sheet
4. Sprinkle with sugar
5. Broil 1-3 minutes, watching CONSTANTLY
6. Rotate for even browning
7. 🚨 **WATCH EVERY SECOND** - can burn instantly
8. Not as even as torch but works

## Variations

### Vanilla Bean (Classic)
- As written above ⭐ **BEST**

### Espresso
- Add 2 tbsp instant espresso to hot cream

### Lavender
- Add 1 tbsp dried lavender to cream, strain well

### Chocolate
- Add 4 oz melted dark chocolate to custard base

### Citrus
- Add zest of 1 lemon or orange to cream
- Strain well

## Make-Ahead Timeline
- **Day before**: Make custards, chill overnight
- **2 hours before**: Remove from fridge
- **Right before serving**: Brûlée the tops (15 min before)

## Storage
- **Before brûlée**: 3 days covered in fridge
- **After brûlée**: Serve within 30 min (sugar gets soft)

## Pro Tips
- Superfine sugar caramelizes more evenly than granulated
- Can brûlée twice for extra-thick sugar layer (wait 5 min between)
- Shallow ramekins give better custard-to-sugar ratio
- Room temp eggs mix better
- Pass custard through strainer twice for ultra-smooth
- Can flavor with any extract, citrus, coffee, liqueur

## Equipment Notes
- **Torch**: $20-30 investment, worth it
- **No torch?** Use broiler (less consistent but works)
- **Ramekins**: Shallow and wide better than tall and narrow
