# Chocolate Soufflé

## Overview
The most intimidating dessert that's actually not that hard. Light, airy, dramatic. Impresses everyone. Timing is EVERYTHING.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **TIMING NON-NEGOTIABLE**: Must go from oven to table in 2 minutes or deflates
- **DON'T OPEN OVEN**: Opening door = temperature drop = collapsed soufflé
- **EGG WHITES MUST BE STIFF**: Soft peaks = flat soufflé
- **BOWL MUST BE CLEAN**: Any fat = whites won't whip
- **FOLD GENTLY**: Overmixing = dense, undermixing = separated
- **SERVE IMMEDIATELY**: Soufflés wait for no one

## Equipment
- 4 ramekins (6-8 oz each) or 1 large soufflé dish
- Stand or hand mixer
- 2 mixing bowls (one completely clean and dry for whites)
- Rubber spatula
- Pastry brush
- Baking sheet

## Ingredients (serves 4)

**For Ramekins:**
- 1 tbsp soft butter (for coating)
- 2 tbsp granulated sugar (for coating)

**For Soufflé:**
- 4 oz dark chocolate (60-70% cacao), chopped
- 2 tbsp butter
- 3 egg yolks
- 5 egg whites (room temperature)
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar (for egg whites)
- Pinch cream of tartar (helps stabilize)
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt
- Powdered sugar for dusting (optional)

## Preparation (Can do 2 hours ahead)

### Step 1: Prep Ramekins (CRITICAL)
1. Brush ramekins with soft butter
2. 🚨 **BRUSH UPWARD** - helps soufflé rise
3. Add sugar, rotate to coat completely
4. Tap out excess
5. Refrigerate until needed

### Step 2: Make Chocolate Base
1. Melt chocolate and butter in double boiler
2. Stir until smooth
3. Remove from heat
4. Let cool 5 minutes
5. Whisk in egg yolks one at a time
6. Add vanilla and pinch of salt
7. Set aside (can be room temp for 2 hours)

## Making the Soufflé (30 minutes before serving)

### Step 3: Preheat Oven
1. Preheat to 375°F
2. Place baking sheet on middle rack
3. Must be fully preheated

### Step 4: Whip Egg Whites (CRITICAL)
1. Ensure bowl and beaters are COMPLETELY clean and dry
2. Add egg whites and cream of tartar
3. Beat on medium-high until foamy
4. Gradually add sugar (1 tbsp at a time)
5. 🚨 **BEAT TO STIFF PEAKS**:
   - Should stand straight up when beaters lifted
   - Should NOT slide if bowl tipped
   - Should be glossy, not dry
6. **DON'T OVERBEAT** - will become grainy

### Step 5: Fold Together (Gentle!)
1. Add 1/3 of egg whites to chocolate base
2. Stir vigorously to lighten (this portion can be rough)
3. Add remaining whites
4. 🚨 **FOLD GENTLY**:
   - Cut down center with spatula
   - Scoop along bottom
   - Fold over top
   - Rotate bowl, repeat
5. Fold just until no white streaks remain
6. Should be light and airy
7. **DON'T OVERMIX** - deflates soufflé

### Step 6: Fill Ramekins
1. Divide mixture evenly among ramekins
2. Fill to just below rim
3. Run thumb around inside edge (creates "top hat" effect)
4. 🚨 **WIPE RIMS CLEAN** - drips prevent rising

### Step 7: Bake (14-16 minutes)
1. Place ramekins on preheated baking sheet
2. **SET TIMER FOR 14 MINUTES**
3. 🚨 **DON'T OPEN OVEN** - seriously, don't
4. At 14 min, peek through window:
   - Should be puffed above rim
   - Top should have slight jiggle
   - Edges should be set
5. If not ready, bake 2 more minutes

### Step 8: Serve IMMEDIATELY
1. Remove from oven
2. Dust with powdered sugar
3. **SERVE WITHIN 2 MINUTES**
4. Will deflate after 3-5 minutes (this is normal)

## Perfect Soufflé Indicators
- Risen 1-1.5" above rim
- Top is domed and golden
- Slight jiggle in center
- Edges are set and firm
- Interior should be creamy (not fully set)

## Serving
- Serve on plate with spoon
- Crack open top with two spoons
- Can serve with:
  - Vanilla ice cream
  - Whipped cream
  - Crème anglaise
  - Fresh berries

## Troubleshooting
- **Didn't rise?** Egg whites not stiff enough or overmixed when folding
- **Collapsed immediately?** Undercooked or opened oven during baking
- **Dense and heavy?** Overmixed or whites underbeaten
- **Lopsided?** Oven has hot spots - rotate pan 180° halfway
- **Stuck to ramekin?** Didn't butter and sugar properly

## Pro Tips
- Ramekins can be prepped day ahead
- Chocolate base can be made 2 hours ahead (room temp)
- Room temp egg whites whip better
- Separate eggs when cold, then bring whites to room temp
- Can make individual soufflés or one large (bake 25-30 min)
- For extra rise: Fill ramekins, refrigerate 30 min before baking

## Timing for Dinner Party
1. **2 hours before**: Prep ramekins, make chocolate base
2. **30 min before**: Whip whites, fold, fill
3. **16 min before serving**: Put in oven
4. **Immediately**: Serve from oven to table

## Variations

### Grand Marnier Soufflé
- Add 2 tbsp Grand Marnier to chocolate base
- Add 1 tsp orange zest

### Coffee Soufflé
- Add 1 tbsp instant espresso powder to chocolate base

### Individual Molten Center
- Reduce baking time to 11-12 minutes
- Center will be completely liquid

## Make-Ahead Option
1. Fill ramekins completely
2. Cover with plastic wrap
3. Refrigerate up to 4 hours
4. Bake directly from fridge (add 2-3 min to baking time)
5. 🚨 Still must serve immediately after baking

## What "Correctly Fallen" Looks Like
- After 5-10 minutes, will deflate to near-original height
- This is NORMAL and expected
- Still delicious, just not as dramatic
- Why restaurants time them perfectly to table
