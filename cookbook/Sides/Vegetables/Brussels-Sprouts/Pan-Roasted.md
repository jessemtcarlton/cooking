# Pan-Roasted Brussels Sprouts with Garlic Butter

## Overview
Crispy caramelized exteriors, tender centers. Restaurant technique that beats oven-roasting.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **HIGH HEAT REQUIRED**: Medium heat = soggy sprouts
- **DON'T STIR TOO MUCH**: Need to sit on heat to caramelize
- **WATCH GARLIC**: Garlic burns fast - add at end only
- **HALVE EVENLY**: Uneven sizes = uneven cooking
- **BUTTER BURNS**: Add only after initial sear

## Equipment
- Large cast iron or stainless skillet
- Sharp knife
- Lid or baking sheet (to cover)
- Timer

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- 1.5 lb Brussels sprouts
- 2 tbsp neutral oil (avocado or grapeseed)
- 3 tbsp butter
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- Optional: balsamic glaze, parmesan, bacon

## Method

### Step 1: Prep Brussels Sprouts
1. Trim stem ends
2. Remove any loose/damaged outer leaves
3. Halve lengthwise through core
4. 🚨 **MAKE SIZES UNIFORM** - quarter any large ones
5. Pat dry thoroughly (moisture = no crisp)

### Step 2: Initial Sear (5-7 minutes)
1. Heat skillet over MEDIUM-HIGH heat (2 minutes)
2. Add oil - should shimmer
3. Place sprouts cut-side DOWN in single layer
4. 🚨 **DON'T MOVE THEM** - let sit undisturbed
5. **SET TIMER FOR 5 MINUTES**
6. Should hear steady sizzle - if not, increase heat

### Step 3: Check First Side
1. Peek under one sprout at 5 minutes
2. Should be deep golden-brown (almost black spots OK)
3. If pale: Let go 2 more minutes
4. If charred black: Reduce heat slightly

### Step 4: Flip and Steam (5-6 minutes)
1. Flip sprouts (tongs or spatula)
2. Add 1/4 cup water to pan
3. 🚨 **WILL STEAM AND SPLATTER** - be careful
4. Immediately cover with lid
5. **SET TIMER FOR 5 MINUTES**
6. Reduce heat to MEDIUM
7. Steam until tender when pierced with knife

### Step 5: Garlic Butter Finish (2 minutes)
1. Remove lid - water should be evaporated
2. If water remains, cook uncovered 1-2 min to evaporate
3. Reduce to MEDIUM-LOW
4. Add butter and garlic
5. **SET TIMER FOR 1 MINUTE**
6. 🚨 **WATCH GARLIC** - stir constantly or will burn
7. Toss sprouts in garlic butter until coated
8. Remove from heat immediately

### Step 6: Season and Serve
1. Season with salt and pepper
2. Optional: drizzle balsamic glaze or grate parmesan
3. Serve immediately (don't let sit or lose crisp)

## Doneness Check
- Cut side: Deep caramelized brown
- Inside: Tender, not crunchy
- Leaves: Crispy, some charred
- Should NOT be: Mushy, gray, or raw-tasting

## Variations

### With Bacon
- Cook 4 strips bacon first, crumble
- Use bacon fat instead of oil
- Top with crumbled bacon

### Balsamic Glazed
- After butter, add 2 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- Let reduce 30 seconds until syrupy

### Parmesan
- Grate fresh parmesan over hot sprouts
- Add 1 tbsp lemon juice

### Spicy
- Add 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes with garlic

## Troubleshooting
- **Not caramelizing?** Heat too low or sprouts not dry
- **Burning?** Heat too high - reduce to medium-high
- **Soggy?** Too much water added or not dried before cooking
- **Bitter?** Overcooked - reduce steam time
- **Garlic burned?** Added too early - only add last 1-2 minutes

## Pro Tips
- Can blanch sprouts first for guaranteed tender centers (boil 3 min, then sear)
- Smallest/tightest sprouts are sweetest
- Don't crowd pan - work in batches if needed
- Save any loose leaves that fall off - they get extra crispy
