# Oven-Roasted Asparagus

## Overview
Perfectly crisp-tender asparagus in 12 minutes. Nearly foolproof but timing is everything.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **THICKNESS MATTERS**: Thin spears = 8 min, thick = 15 min
- **OVERCOOKING**: Goes from perfect to mushy in 2 minutes
- **WATCH AT 10 MIN**: Check and adjust - ovens vary
- **PREHEAT PAN**: Cold pan = steamed asparagus

## Equipment
- Rimmed baking sheet
- Tongs or spatula
- Timer

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- 1 lb asparagus (medium thickness ideal)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- Optional: lemon zest, parmesan, garlic

## Method

### Step 1: Prep Asparagus
1. Preheat oven to 425°F
2. Place baking sheet in oven to preheat (optional but better)
3. Snap off woody ends (bend until they break naturally)
4. Pat dry if washed
5. Toss with oil, salt, pepper in bowl

### Step 2: Roast
1. Remove hot pan from oven (use mitts!)
2. Spread asparagus in single layer
3. 🚨 **TIMING BY THICKNESS**:
   - Thin (pencil): **SET TIMER FOR 8 MINUTES**
   - Medium: **SET TIMER FOR 10 MINUTES**
   - Thick (thumb): **SET TIMER FOR 12 MINUTES**
4. Don't stir or flip

### Step 3: Check Doneness (at timer)
1. Spears should be bright green with brown spots
2. Pierce with fork - should have slight resistance
3. 🚨 **IF NOT DONE**: Add 2 minutes max
4. Better slightly undercooked than mushy

### Step 4: Finish and Serve
1. Remove immediately when done
2. Optional: squeeze fresh lemon, grate parmesan
3. Serve within 5 minutes (gets soggy if sitting)

## Perfect Doneness Signs
- Bright green color (not drab olive)
- Charred spots on tips and ridges
- Tender but still has snap
- NOT limp or mushy

## Variations

### Garlic Roasted
- Add 4 minced garlic cloves, toss halfway through

### Parmesan
- Grate parmesan over top immediately after removing from oven

### Lemon
- Zest lemon over asparagus before roasting, squeeze juice after

### Balsamic
- Drizzle with balsamic glaze after roasting

## Troubleshooting
- **Mushy?** Overcooked - reduce time by 2 min next time
- **Too crunchy?** Undercooked - add 2 minutes
- **Not browning?** Oven not hot enough or too crowded on pan
- **Soggy?** Too much oil or not dried before roasting

## Pro Tips
- Preheat the pan for better char
- Space out spears - touching = steaming
- Thicker spears are more forgiving (less overcook risk)
- Can cook at same temp as other dishes (400-450°F) - adjust time
