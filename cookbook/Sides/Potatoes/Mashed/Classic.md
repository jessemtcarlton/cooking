# Classic Mashed Potatoes

## Overview
Restaurant-quality creamy, fluffy mashed potatoes. The secret: proper potato choice and hot dairy.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **POTATO TYPE CRITICAL**: Russet or Yukon Gold ONLY - red/waxy = gluey
- **DON'T OVERMIX**: Overworking = gluey paste, not fluffy
- **DAIRY MUST BE HOT**: Cold dairy = gummy potatoes
- **RICE WHILE HOT**: Let potatoes cool = dense, gluey texture
- **SALT THE WATER**: Heavily - like ocean water

## Equipment
- Large pot
- Potato ricer or food mill (BEST) or masher
- Small saucepan (for dairy)
- Colander
- Timer

## Ingredients (serves 6)
- 3 lbs russet or Yukon gold potatoes
- 6 tbsp butter (3/4 stick)
- 1/2 to 3/4 cup heavy cream or whole milk (warm)
- 2 tbsp kosher salt (for boiling water)
- 1 tsp salt (for seasoning)
- 1/2 tsp white pepper (or black)

## Method

### Step 1: Prep and Boil (20-25 minutes)
1. Peel potatoes, cut into 2" chunks
2. 🚨 **MAKE CHUNKS UNIFORM SIZE** - uneven = uneven cooking
3. Place in pot, cover with cold water by 1"
4. Add 2 tbsp salt to water
5. Bring to boil over high heat
6. **SET TIMER FOR 15 MINUTES** once boiling
7. Reduce to medium - gentle boil, not rolling
8. Test doneness: Fork should slide through easily
9. If not done, check every 2-3 minutes

### Step 2: Heat Dairy (during boiling)
1. In small saucepan, heat butter and cream over LOW
2. Don't boil - just get hot and melted
3. Keep warm until needed
4. 🚨 **MUST BE HOT** when adding to potatoes

### Step 3: Drain and Dry (critical step)
1. Drain potatoes in colander
2. Return to hot pot
3. Place pot over LOW heat for 2 minutes
4. Shake pot gently - lets steam escape
5. 🚨 **REMOVING MOISTURE = FLUFFIER POTATOES**

### Step 4: Rice or Mash (WHILE HOT)
**With Ricer (BEST METHOD):**
1. Rice potatoes back into pot
2. Don't press chunks twice - one pass only

**With Masher:**
1. Mash until no lumps remain
2. Use up-and-down motion
3. 🚨 **STOP as soon as smooth** - don't overmix

### Step 5: Add Dairy (carefully)
1. Add half the hot butter/cream mixture
2. Stir gently with wooden spoon or spatula
3. Add more until desired consistency
4. 🚨 **FOLD, DON'T BEAT** - overmixing = gluey
5. Should be creamy but hold shape

### Step 6: Season and Serve
1. Add salt and pepper to taste
2. Stir gently to incorporate
3. Serve immediately
4. Optional: Make well in center, add extra butter pool

## Perfect Texture Indicators
- Smooth and creamy (no lumps)
- Light and fluffy (not dense)
- Holds shape but not stiff
- NOT: Gluey, sticky, or pasty

## Variations

### Garlic Mashed
- Roast 1 head garlic at 400°F for 40 min
- Squeeze out cloves, mash into hot dairy

### Loaded (Bacon & Cheese)
- Fold in 1 cup shredded cheddar
- Top with crumbled bacon, chives, sour cream

### Horseradish
- Add 2-3 tbsp prepared horseradish (perfect with steak)
- Add with dairy

### Herb
- Fold in 1/4 cup chopped fresh herbs (chives, parsley, thyme)

## Troubleshooting
- **Gluey/Gummy?** Overworked or used wrong potatoes or cold dairy
- **Lumpy?** Didn't mash enough or potatoes undercooked
- **Watery?** Too much liquid or didn't dry after draining
- **Dense/Heavy?** Overmixed or didn't rice while hot
- **Bland?** Didn't salt water enough or need more seasoning

## Pro Tips
- **Make ahead**: Make fully, spread in baking dish, cover. Reheat at 350°F for 20-30 min
- Potato ricer makes THE difference - worth buying
- Save potato water for making gravy
- For extra richness: Use all heavy cream and add cream cheese
- Yukon Gold = slightly sweet, buttery (less cream needed)
- Russet = classic fluffy texture (more cream needed)

## Timing for Dinner
- Can hold in warm pot, covered, for 20-30 minutes
- If getting stiff, stir in splash of hot cream
