# Hasselback Potatoes (Oven-Roasted)

## Overview
Accordion-sliced potatoes with crispy edges and creamy center. Restaurant-impressive, actually easy.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **SLICING**: Don't cut all the way through or they fall apart
- **CHOPSTICK TRICK**: Place chopsticks on sides as cutting guide
- **TIMING**: Takes 60-75 minutes - not a last-minute side
- **WATCH END**: Last 15 minutes they can burn quickly
- **BUTTER BASTING**: Must baste 3x or won't get crispy

## Equipment
- Sharp knife
- Chopsticks (cutting guides)
- Baking sheet
- Pastry brush
- Timer

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- 4 medium russet or Yukon gold potatoes
- 4 tbsp butter, melted
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- Fresh thyme or rosemary
- Optional: Parmesan cheese, breadcrumbs

## Method

### Step 1: Prep Potatoes
1. Preheat oven to 425°F
2. Scrub potatoes, leave skin on
3. Place chopsticks parallel on cutting board
4. Place potato between chopsticks
5. Slice every 1/8" - chopsticks prevent cutting through
6. 🚨 **CRITICAL**: Fan out slightly to check slices - if cut through, use for mashed potatoes instead

### Step 2: First Roast (30 minutes)
1. Place potatoes on baking sheet
2. Mix melted butter, oil, garlic, salt, pepper
3. Brush generously over potatoes (get butter in cuts)
4. **SET TIMER FOR 30 MINUTES**
5. Roast until starting to soften

### Step 3: First Basting (at 30 min)
1. Remove from oven
2. Brush with more butter mixture
3. Use spoon to drizzle butter into cuts
4. **SET TIMER FOR 20 MINUTES**
5. Return to oven

### Step 4: Second Basting (at 50 min)
1. Remove from oven
2. Brush with remaining butter
3. Potatoes should be golden and fanning out
4. 🚨 **WATCH CLOSELY** - next 15 min they can burn
5. **SET TIMER FOR 15 MINUTES**

### Step 5: Optional Crispy Finish
For extra crispy:
1. At 60 min, sprinkle with parmesan and breadcrumbs
2. Increase oven to 450°F
3. **SET TIMER FOR 5-10 MINUTES**
4. Watch constantly - can burn in seconds
5. Remove when edges are crispy and golden brown

### Step 6: Rest and Serve (5 minutes)
1. Remove from oven
2. Let cool 5 minutes (will firm up)
3. Garnish with fresh herbs
4. Serve immediately

## Troubleshooting
- **Sliced through?** Use for mashed potatoes or roasted wedges
- **Not fanning out?** Slices too thick - make thinner next time
- **Burning on top?** Cover loosely with foil last 10 minutes
- **Not crispy?** Not enough butter or oven temp too low
- **Taking too long?** Potatoes too large - use medium size

## Pro Tips
- Can prep sliced potatoes 2 hours ahead - keep in cold water, pat dry before roasting
- Smaller potatoes cook faster (45-50 min)
- Sweet potatoes work too - reduce time to 40-50 min
