# 🌟 Cracker Barrel Mac and Cheese (Copycat)

## Overview
Ultra-creamy, sharp cheddar mac and cheese that's both baked and saucy. Cracker Barrel's version is legendary - not the dry baked kind, but creamy and cheesy with a golden top. Colby cheese is the secret. This copycat creates the exact creamy, tangy, comfort food perfection.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **COLBY CHEESE IS THE SECRET**: Mix of sharp cheddar and Colby (Colby makes it creamy)
- **SHRED CHEESE YOURSELF**: Pre-shredded has anti-caking agents (won't melt smooth)
- **DON'T OVERCOOK PASTA**: Cook 2 minutes LESS than package (finishes in oven)
- **BAKE UNCOVERED**: Want golden top but sauce stays creamy
- **EVAPORATED MILK**: Not regular milk - this is key to creaminess
- **SERVE IMMEDIATELY**: Best fresh from oven (sauce thickens as it cools)

## The Cracker Barrel Secret
1. **Colby cheese** (melts creamy, not grainy)
2. **Sharp cheddar** (flavor depth)
3. **Evaporated milk** (creates creamy texture without breaking)
4. **Velveeta** (small amount for ultimate creaminess and stability)
5. **Eggs** (binds sauce, creates custardy texture)
6. **Baked** (golden top, creamy inside)
7. **Butter** (richness throughout)

## Equipment
- Large pot (for pasta)
- 9x13" baking dish (or 3-quart casserole)
- Large mixing bowl
- Whisk
- Grater (for cheese)
- Colander

## Ingredients (serves 8-10)

**For Mac and Cheese:**
- 1 lb (450g) elbow macaroni
- 4 cups (450g) sharp cheddar cheese, shredded
- 2 cups (225g) Colby cheese, shredded
- 4 oz (113g) Velveeta cheese, cubed small
- 1 can (12 oz/354ml) evaporated milk
- 1 1/2 cups (360ml) whole milk
- 2 large eggs
- 1/4 cup (56g) butter, melted
- 1 tsp (6g) kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp (1g) black pepper
- 1/4 tsp (0.5g) dry mustard powder (optional but recommended)
- Pinch of cayenne pepper (optional)

**For Topping:**
- 1 cup (113g) sharp cheddar cheese, shredded
- 2 tbsp (28g) butter, melted
- Paprika for dusting (optional)

## Method

### Step 1: Preheat and Prep (5 minutes)
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (177°C)
2. Grease 9x13" baking dish with butter
3. Bring large pot of salted water to boil

### Step 2: Cook Pasta (6-7 minutes)
1. Add 1 lb (450g) elbow macaroni to boiling water
2. Cook 2 minutes LESS than package directions
3. Usually 6-7 minutes (should be quite firm)
4. Drain in colander (don't rinse)
5. Set aside
6. 🚨 **UNDERCOOK PASTA** - it finishes in oven

### Step 3: Mix Cheese Mixture

**In large mixing bowl, combine:**
1. 4 cups (450g) shredded sharp cheddar
2. 2 cups (225g) shredded Colby cheese
3. 4 oz (113g) Velveeta, cubed small
4. Add drained hot pasta
5. Toss to combine
6. Cheese will start to melt from pasta heat

### Step 4: Mix Liquid Ingredients

**In separate bowl, whisk together:**
1. 1 can (12 oz) evaporated milk
2. 1 1/2 cups (360ml) whole milk
3. 2 large eggs
4. 1/4 cup (56g) melted butter
5. 1 tsp (6g) salt
6. 1/2 tsp (1g) black pepper
7. 1/4 tsp dry mustard powder
8. Pinch cayenne
9. Whisk until eggs completely incorporated
10. Should be smooth, yellow-ish

### Step 5: Combine and Transfer
1. Pour liquid mixture over pasta and cheese
2. Stir gently to combine
3. All pasta should be coated
4. Mixture will look soupy (this is correct)
5. Pour into greased 9x13" baking dish
6. Spread evenly
7. Top with 1 cup (113g) shredded sharp cheddar
8. Drizzle with 2 tbsp (28g) melted butter
9. Dust lightly with paprika
10. 🚨 **SHOULD BE VERY WET** - don't worry, it sets in oven

### Step 6: Bake (30-35 minutes)
1. Bake at 350°F (177°C) for 30-35 minutes
2. **Check at 30 minutes**:
   - Top golden brown and bubbling
   - Center set but still jiggly
   - Edges pulling away slightly
3. Should be golden on top
4. Still creamy inside (not dry)
5. Don't overbake or it dries out
6. 🚨 **DON'T OVERBAKE** - want creamy, not dry

### Step 7: Rest and Serve
1. Remove from oven
2. Let rest 10 minutes (sets up slightly)
3. Still should be creamy when scooped
4. Serve hot
5. 🚨 **BEST FRESH** - sauce thickens as it cools

## Troubleshooting

**Too dry:**
- Overbaked (check at 30 min)
- Cooked pasta too long before baking
- Not enough liquid (evaporated milk is key)
- Baked uncovered too long

**Grainy texture:**
- Cheese overheated (oven too hot)
- Used pre-shredded cheese (has anti-caking agents)
- Didn't use Colby (it melts smoother)
- Skipped Velveeta (helps prevent graininess)

**Too soupy:**
- Underbaked (needs full 30-35 min)
- Too much liquid
- Didn't let rest after baking

**Bland flavor:**
- Not enough salt
- Used mild cheddar (need sharp)
- Skipped mustard powder (adds depth)
- Not enough cheese

**Eggs scrambled:**
- Liquid mixture too hot when added eggs
- Make sure milk mixture is room temp before whisking in eggs
- Whisk eggs very well

## Variations

### Extra Sharp
- Use all extra sharp cheddar
- More tangy, pronounced cheese flavor

### Add Bacon
- Top with crumbled crispy bacon before baking
- Or mix in before baking
- Smokier flavor

### Add Breadcrumb Topping
- Mix 1 cup panko with 3 tbsp melted butter
- Sprinkle on top last 10 min of baking
- Crunchy topping

### Lighter Version
- Use 2% milk instead of whole
- Reduce butter to 2 tbsp
- Skip Velveeta
- Still creamy but less rich

### Stovetop Version
- Make all on stovetop (skip eggs and baking)
- Melt butter, add flour, make roux
- Add milk slowly, make cheese sauce
- Mix with cooked pasta
- Serve immediately

## Storage and Reheating

**Refrigerator:**
- Store covered up to 4 days
- Sauce will thicken (normal)

**Reheating:**
- Oven: Cover with foil, 350°F for 20-25 min
- Add splash of milk before reheating
- Stir halfway through
- Microwave: Add splash of milk, heat in 1-min intervals, stirring

**Freezing:**
- Freeze unbaked up to 3 months
- Thaw in fridge overnight
- Bake as directed (may need extra 10 min)
- Or freeze baked, thaw and reheat
