# 🌟 KFC Coleslaw (Copycat)

## Overview
The creamy, tangy, slightly sweet coleslaw that perfectly complements KFC's fried chicken. Finely chopped cabbage and carrots in a mayo-based dressing. Simple, classic, and exactly what you crave with fried chicken. This copycat creates the exact flavor and texture.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **FINELY CHOPPED**: Cabbage must be chopped fine (not shredded like grocery store slaw)
- **DRAIN EXCESS LIQUID**: Cabbage releases water - drain before mixing with dressing
- **CHILL 2+ HOURS**: Flavors must meld (tastes bland if served immediately)
- **MIX DRESSING SEPARATELY**: Don't add ingredients directly to cabbage
- **DON'T SKIP SUGAR**: Needs sweetness to balance tang (not optional)

## The KFC Secret
1. **Finely chopped cabbage** (not coarse shreds - specific texture)
2. **Grated carrots** (fine, not chunks)
3. **Sweet and tangy dressing** (mayo, buttermilk, vinegar, sugar)
4. **Chilled overnight** (flavors develop, cabbage softens)
5. **Simple ingredients** (no exotic spices - pure comfort)
6. **Slightly soupy** (thinner than traditional slaw)

## Equipment
- Large mixing bowl
- Medium bowl (for dressing)
- Sharp knife or food processor
- Box grater
- Whisk
- Measuring cups and spoons
- Plastic wrap or lid

## Ingredients (serves 8-10)

**For Slaw Base:**
- 8 cups (600g) green cabbage, finely chopped
- 1/4 cup (30g) carrots, finely grated
- 2 tbsp (20g) white onion, minced (optional but authentic)

**For Dressing:**
- 1/2 cup (120ml) mayonnaise
- 1/3 cup (80ml) buttermilk
- 1/4 cup (60ml) whole milk
- 1/4 cup (50g) granulated sugar
- 2 1/2 tbsp (37ml) white vinegar
- 2 1/2 tbsp (37ml) lemon juice (fresh squeezed)
- 1/2 tsp (3g) kosher salt
- 1/4 tsp (0.5g) black pepper

## Method

### Step 1: Prep Cabbage and Carrots (15 minutes)

**Chop Cabbage:**
1. Remove outer leaves from cabbage
2. Cut in half, remove core
3. Chop VERY FINELY:
   - Not shredded like store-bought
   - Pieces should be 1/8" to 1/4" size
   - Use food processor pulse method OR
   - Chop by hand with sharp knife
4. Should have 8 cups (600g) finely chopped
5. 🚨 **FINE CHOP IS CRITICAL** - texture defines KFC slaw

**Grate Carrots:**
1. Peel 1-2 medium carrots
2. Grate on fine holes of box grater
3. Should be very fine, almost paste-like
4. Need 1/4 cup (30g)

**Optional Onion:**
1. Mince 2 tbsp (20g) white onion very fine
2. This is authentic but optional
3. Adds slight bite

### Step 2: Combine Vegetables
1. In large mixing bowl, combine:
   - 8 cups (600g) finely chopped cabbage
   - 1/4 cup (30g) finely grated carrots
   - 2 tbsp (20g) minced onion (if using)
2. Toss to distribute carrots evenly
3. Let sit 10 minutes
4. If cabbage releases water, drain it off
5. Pat cabbage dry with paper towels if very wet

### Step 3: Make Dressing

**In medium bowl, whisk together:**
1. 1/2 cup (120ml) mayonnaise
2. 1/3 cup (80ml) buttermilk
3. 1/4 cup (60ml) whole milk
4. 1/4 cup (50g) sugar
5. 2 1/2 tbsp (37ml) white vinegar
6. 2 1/2 tbsp (37ml) lemon juice
7. 1/2 tsp (3g) salt
8. 1/4 tsp (0.5g) black pepper

**Whisk vigorously:**
- Should be smooth, creamy
- Sugar should dissolve completely
- Should be pourable (thinner than typical slaw dressing)
- Taste: sweet, tangy, creamy balanced

### Step 4: Combine and Chill (THE CRITICAL STEP)
1. Pour dressing over cabbage mixture
2. Toss thoroughly to coat all pieces
3. Should look very wet (not dry)
4. Cover bowl tightly with plastic wrap
5. Refrigerate 2 hours MINIMUM
6. **OVERNIGHT IS BETTER** (flavors meld, cabbage softens)
7. Stir before serving
8. 🚨 **MUST CHILL** - tastes bland and crunchy if served immediately

### Step 5: Serve
1. Remove from fridge
2. Stir well (dressing settles)
3. Taste and adjust:
   - More salt if needed
   - More vinegar for tang
   - More sugar for sweetness
4. Serve cold
5. Should be creamy, slightly soupy, flavorful
6. 🚨 **SERVE COLD** - this is a cold side dish

## Perfect KFC Coleslaw Indicators
- **Texture**: Finely chopped (not shredded), softened but not mushy
- **Consistency**: Creamy, slightly soupy (not dry)
- **Color**: Pale green-white with orange flecks (carrots)
- **Flavor**: Sweet and tangy, balanced, creamy
- **Temperature**: Cold
- **Appearance**: Creamy coating on every piece

## Timing

**Total Time: 2+ hours (includes chilling)**
- Prep vegetables: 15 min
- Make dressing: 5 min
- Chill: 2+ hours (overnight preferred)
- **Total active time: 20 min**

**Make-Ahead:**
- Best made 1 day ahead
- Flavors improve overnight
- Store up to 3 days refrigerated
- Stir before serving

## Troubleshooting

**Too watery:**
- Cabbage released water during storage
- Didn't drain cabbage before mixing
- Drain excess liquid, add more mayo
- Or let sit (cabbage absorbs dressing over time)

**Too dry:**
- Not enough dressing
- Add mixture of milk and mayo (2:1 ratio)
- Stir well

**Tastes bland:**
- Didn't chill long enough (needs 2+ hours)
- Not enough salt or sugar
- Taste and adjust seasoning
- Add more vinegar for tang

**Too sweet:**
- Reduce sugar next time
- Add more vinegar and lemon juice
- Add pinch of salt

**Too tangy:**
- Add more sugar (1 tbsp at a time)
- Add more mayo (balances acid)

**Cabbage too crunchy:**
- Didn't chill long enough
- Chop finer next time
- Let sit overnight

**Wrong texture:**
- Cabbage not chopped finely enough
- Should be small pieces, not long shreds
- Food processor on pulse works well

## Variations

### Extra Creamy
- Increase mayo to 2/3 cup
- Reduce milk to 2 tbsp
- Richer, thicker

### Tangy Version
- Increase vinegar to 1/4 cup
- Reduce sugar to 3 tbsp
- More bite

### Add Celery Seed
- Add 1/2 tsp celery seed to dressing
- Traditional coleslaw flavor
- Not KFC authentic but tasty

### Lighter Version
- Use light mayo
- Use low-fat buttermilk
- Reduce sugar to 3 tbsp
- Still good, less rich

## Storage

**Refrigerator:**
- Store in airtight container 3-5 days
- Gets better day 2-3
- May need to drain excess liquid
- Stir before serving

**Not Freezer Friendly:**
- Don't freeze (cabbage gets mushy, dressing separates)
- Make fresh

## Pro Tips

**Restaurant Secrets:**
- KFC chops cabbage very fine (signature texture)
- Dressing is thinner than traditional slaw (intentional)
- Made in large batches, refrigerated overnight
- Sugar and vinegar balance is specific
- Served cold always (never room temp)
- Simple recipe - no fancy ingredients

**For Best Results:**
- Chop cabbage finely (don't just shred)
- Use fresh lemon juice (not bottled)
- Real buttermilk (not milk + vinegar substitute)
- Let chill overnight if possible
- Drain excess liquid before serving
- Taste and adjust seasoning
- Serve cold

**Food Processor Method:**
- Cut cabbage into chunks
- Pulse 8-10 times
- Check size frequently
- Want fine chop, not puree
- Easier than hand chopping
