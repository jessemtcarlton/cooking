# 🌟 Boston Market Creamed Spinach (Copycat)

## Overview
Rich, creamy, garlicky creamed spinach that's the perfect side dish. Boston Market's version is legendary - velvety cream sauce, tender spinach, subtle garlic. Not heavy or overwhelming, just perfectly creamy. This copycat creates the exact balance of cream, cheese, and spinach.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **SQUEEZE SPINACH DRY**: Excess water = watery sauce (critical step)
- **LOW HEAT FOR CREAM**: High heat can break cream sauce
- **FRESH GARLIC ONLY**: Powdered garlic won't give the same flavor
- **PARMIGIANO-REGGIANO**: Real Parmesan, freshly grated (not green can)
- **DON'T OVERCOOK SPINACH**: Just until wilted (stays bright green)
- **NUTMEG IS TRADITIONAL**: Tiny amount adds depth (not optional)

## The Boston Market Secret
1. **Frozen chopped spinach** (consistent, easy, pre-washed)
2. **Heavy cream base** (rich and velvety)
3. **Cream cheese** (adds tang and thickness)
4. **Fresh garlic** (aromatic, not harsh)
5. **Parmesan cheese** (nutty, salty depth)
6. **Nutmeg** (traditional with spinach, subtle)
7. **Butter** (richness and flavor)

## Equipment
- Large skillet or sauté pan
- Saucepan (medium)
- Colander or strainer
- Clean kitchen towel (for squeezing spinach)
- Wooden spoon
- Whisk
- Grater (for Parmesan)

## Ingredients (serves 4-6)

**For Creamed Spinach:**
- 2 packages (10 oz/283g each) frozen chopped spinach
- 3 tbsp (42g) butter
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tbsp (16g) all-purpose flour
- 1 cup (240ml) heavy cream
- 4 oz (113g) cream cheese, softened and cubed
- 1/2 cup (50g) freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano
- 1/4 tsp (0.5g) ground nutmeg
- 1/2 tsp (3g) kosher salt
- 1/4 tsp (0.5g) black pepper
- Pinch of cayenne pepper (optional)

## Method

### Step 1: Prep Spinach (THE CRITICAL STEP)

**Thaw and Drain:**
1. Thaw 2 packages (20 oz total) frozen chopped spinach
2. Can thaw in microwave or overnight in fridge
3. Place in colander
4. Press out as much liquid as possible
5. Transfer to clean kitchen towel
6. Twist towel and SQUEEZE HARD
7. Continue squeezing until almost dry
8. Should reduce to about 1 1/2 cups packed spinach
9. Set aside
10. 🚨 **MUST BE VERY DRY** - this is critical or sauce will be watery

### Step 2: Make Cream Sauce (10 minutes)

**Sauté Garlic:**
1. In large skillet over MEDIUM heat:
2. Melt 3 tbsp (42g) butter
3. Add 3 cloves minced garlic
4. Sauté 1-2 minutes until fragrant (not brown)
5. 🚨 **DON'T BURN GARLIC** - it becomes bitter

**Make Roux:**
1. Sprinkle 2 tbsp (16g) flour over butter and garlic
2. Whisk constantly for 1-2 minutes
3. Should smell nutty, no raw flour smell
4. Mixture will be thick paste (this is correct)

**Add Cream:**
1. Slowly pour in 1 cup (240ml) heavy cream
2. Whisk constantly to prevent lumps
3. Bring to gentle simmer
4. Whisk frequently
5. Cook 3-4 minutes until thickened
6. Should coat back of spoon
7. Reduce heat to LOW

**Add Cream Cheese:**
1. Add 4 oz (113g) cubed cream cheese
2. Whisk until completely melted and smooth
3. No lumps should remain
4. Sauce should be thick and creamy

### Step 3: Add Cheese and Seasonings
1. Still on LOW heat
2. Add 1/2 cup (50g) grated Parmigiano-Reggiano
3. Whisk until melted
4. Add:
   - 1/4 tsp (0.5g) nutmeg
   - 1/2 tsp (3g) salt
   - 1/4 tsp (0.5g) black pepper
   - Pinch cayenne (if using)
5. Stir to combine
6. Sauce should be smooth, thick, creamy

### Step 4: Add Spinach
1. Add squeezed-dry spinach to cream sauce
2. Stir to coat spinach completely with sauce
3. Cook on LOW heat 3-5 minutes
4. Stir occasionally
5. Spinach should be heated through
6. Sauce should coat spinach evenly
7. If too thick, add cream 1 tbsp at a time
8. If too thin, simmer to reduce

### Step 5: Final Adjustments and Serve
1. Taste and adjust seasonings:
   - More salt if needed
   - More nutmeg for depth
   - More Parmesan for cheesiness
2. Should be creamy, well-seasoned, rich
3. Transfer to serving dish
4. Garnish with extra Parmesan
5. Serve hot
6. 🚨 **SERVE HOT** - gets thick when cool

## Troubleshooting

**Watery sauce:**
- Didn't squeeze spinach dry enough (most common issue)
- Didn't make roux properly
- Not enough cream cheese or Parmesan
- Fix: simmer longer to reduce liquid
- Or add 1 tbsp softened cream cheese

**Lumpy sauce:**
- Added cream too fast to roux
- Didn't whisk constantly
- Cream cheese not fully melted
- Fix: whisk vigorously or use immersion blender

**Grainy texture:**
- Heat too high (Parmesan overheated)
- Used pre-grated Parmesan
- Reduce heat, whisk in splash of cream

**Too thick:**
- Too much flour in roux
- Cooked too long
- Add cream or milk 1 tbsp at a time
- Stir well

**Bland flavor:**
- Not enough salt
- Used cheap Parmesan
- Forgot nutmeg (adds subtle depth)
- Taste and adjust

**Garlic too strong:**
- Burned garlic (cook just until fragrant)
- Too much garlic
- Can't remove once added

## Variations

### Extra Garlicky
- Increase garlic to 4-5 cloves
- Add 1/4 tsp garlic powder
- For garlic lovers

### Add Onions
- Sauté 1/4 cup diced onion with garlic
- Adds sweetness and depth
- Traditional variation

### With Artichokes
- Add 1 cup chopped artichoke hearts
- Mix in with spinach
- Spinach artichoke dip style

### Lighter Version
- Use half-and-half instead of heavy cream
- Reduce cream cheese to 2 oz
- Still creamy but less rich

### Fresh Spinach Version
- Use 2 lbs fresh spinach
- Wash, remove stems
- Wilt in batches in pan
- Squeeze dry, chop
- Proceed with recipe

## Storage and Reheating

**Refrigerator:**
- Store in airtight container 3-4 days
- Sauce thickens when cold (normal)

**Reheating:**
- Stovetop: Heat gently with splash of cream, stir frequently
- Microwave: Heat in 30-second intervals, stirring
- Add cream if too thick

**Freezing:**
- Can freeze up to 2 months
- Thaw in fridge overnight
- Texture may change slightly (still good)
- Reheat gently, whisk smooth

## Pro Tips

**Restaurant Secrets:**
- Boston Market uses frozen chopped spinach (consistent)
- Made in batches throughout day
- Kept warm (never boiled)
- Simple recipe - quality ingredients
- Always served hot
- Cream cheese is the secret to thickness

**For Best Results:**
- Squeeze spinach VERY dry (most important step)
- Use real Parmigiano-Reggiano (not domestic)
- Fresh garlic only (not jarred or powdered)
- Low heat after adding cream (prevents breaking)
- Cream cheese at room temp (melts easier)
- Don't skip nutmeg (traditional, adds depth)
- Taste and adjust seasoning at end

**Fresh vs Frozen Spinach:**
- Frozen is easier, consistent
- Fresh spinach: 2 lbs fresh = 10 oz frozen (cooked)
- Must wash, de-stem, wilt, squeeze dry
- Either works, frozen more convenient

**Nutmeg Note:**
- Nutmeg is traditional with creamed spinach
- Use freshly grated if possible (more potent)
- Start with 1/4 tsp, add more if desired
- Should be subtle background flavor
