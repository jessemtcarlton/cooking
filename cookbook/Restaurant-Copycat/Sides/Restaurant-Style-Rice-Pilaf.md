# 🌟 Restaurant-Style Rice Pilaf

## Overview
The ubiquitous steakhouse and fine-dining side. Fluffy, separate grains with toasted flavor, herbs, and sometimes vegetables. This is the technique every restaurant uses for perfect pilaf.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **TOAST RICE FIRST**: Must toast in butter until fragrant (3-4 min)
- **RATIO MATTERS**: 1 cup rice : 2 cups liquid (exact)
- **DON'T STIR WHILE COOKING**: Stirring releases starch = gluey rice
- **LET REST 10 MINUTES**: Off heat, covered - critical for fluffy rice
- **FLUFF WITH FORK**: Never stir with spoon (breaks grains)

## The Restaurant Secret
1. **Toast rice in butter** (develops nutty flavor)
2. **Sauté aromatics** (onions, garlic build base)
3. **Use stock, not water** (chicken or vegetable stock)
4. **Add bay leaf** (subtle depth)
5. **Steam undisturbed** (don't lift lid!)
6. **Rest before serving** (redistributes moisture)

## Equipment
- Heavy-bottomed pot with tight-fitting lid (2-3 quart)
- Wooden spoon
- Fork
- Measuring cups
- Timer

## Ingredients (serves 4-6)
- 2 tbsp (28g) butter
- 1 tbsp (15ml) olive oil
- 1/2 small onion (75g), finely diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1.5 cups (300g) long-grain white rice (jasmine or basmati)
- 3 cups (720ml) chicken or vegetable stock (hot)
- 1 bay leaf
- 1/2 tsp (3g) kosher salt
- 1/4 tsp (1g) white pepper
- Optional: 1/4 cup (30g) slivered almonds, toasted
- Optional: 2 tbsp (8g) fresh parsley, chopped
- Optional: 1/4 cup (35g) frozen peas (added at end)

## Method

### Step 1: Toast Aromatics and Rice (5-6 minutes)
1. Heat heavy pot over MEDIUM heat
2. Add 2 tbsp (28g) butter + 1 tbsp (15ml) olive oil
3. When butter melted and foamy, add diced onion
4. Sauté 2-3 minutes until softened (don't brown)
5. Add garlic, cook 30 seconds
6. Add 1.5 cups (300g) rice
7. Stir to coat every grain with butter/oil
8. Toast 3-4 minutes, stirring occasionally
9. Rice should smell nutty, turn slightly translucent at edges
10. 🚨 **DON'T SKIP TOASTING** - this creates the signature flavor

### Step 2: Add Liquid and Seasonings
1. Add 3 cups (720ml) hot stock (all at once)
2. Add bay leaf
3. Add 1/2 tsp (3g) salt
4. Add 1/4 tsp (1g) white pepper
5. Stir once to combine
6. Bring to boil over HIGH heat (2-3 minutes)

### Step 3: Simmer (18-20 minutes - DON'T TOUCH)
1. Once boiling, reduce heat to LOW
2. Cover with tight-fitting lid
3. **SET TIMER FOR 18 MINUTES**
4. 🚨 **DO NOT LIFT LID** - steam escapes, rice won't cook evenly
5. 🚨 **DO NOT STIR** - releases starch, makes gluey
6. Should hear gentle simmer (not boil)
7. At 18 minutes, peek quickly:
   - All liquid absorbed?
   - Rice tender?
8. If liquid remains, cook 2 more minutes

### Step 4: Rest Off Heat (10 minutes - CRITICAL)
1. Remove from heat
2. Keep lid on
3. **SET TIMER FOR 10 MINUTES**
4. Let steam redistribute
5. Rice firms up, becomes fluffy
6. 🚨 **DON'T SKIP THIS** - rice needs to rest

### Step 5: Fluff and Serve
1. Remove bay leaf
2. Add optional peas (heat from rice will warm them)
3. Fluff rice with fork (gentle lifting motion)
4. Never stir with spoon (breaks grains)
5. Fold in optional parsley and toasted almonds
6. Taste, adjust salt if needed
7. Serve immediately

## Perfect Rice Pilaf Indicators
- **Texture**: Fluffy, separate grains (not sticky)
- **Color**: White with golden toasted bits
- **Aroma**: Nutty, buttery, aromatic
- **Moisture**: Tender but not wet or gummy
- **Appearance**: Light, airy (not compact)

## Restaurant Variations

### Wild Rice Pilaf
- Use 1 cup (200g) white rice + 1/2 cup (100g) wild rice
- Increase liquid to 3.5 cups (840ml)
- Cook time: 45 minutes

### Mushroom Pilaf
- Sauté 8 oz (225g) sliced mushrooms with onions
- Use mushroom stock or add dried porcini to stock

### Vegetable Pilaf
- Add 1/2 cup (75g) diced carrots with onions
- Add 1/4 cup (35g) peas at end
- Add 1/4 cup (30g) diced red bell pepper

### Herb Pilaf
- Add 2 tbsp mixed fresh herbs (thyme, parsley, chives)
- Fold in at end

### Saffron Pilaf
- Add pinch of saffron threads to hot stock (steep 5 min)
- Gives golden color, subtle flavor

## Timing for Dinner

**Total: 40 minutes**
- Toast rice: 6 min
- Simmer: 18-20 min
- Rest: 10 min
- Fluff and serve: 2 min

**Make Ahead:**
- Can make 1-2 hours ahead
- Keep covered, warm in 200°F oven
- Fluff before serving

## Troubleshooting

**Rice is mushy:**
- Too much liquid
- Cooked too long
- Stirred while cooking

**Rice is crunchy:**
- Not enough liquid
- Didn't cook long enough
- Heat too low

**Rice is gummy/sticky:**
- Stirred while cooking (released starch)
- Didn't toast rice first
- Used wrong type of rice (short grain)

**Rice burned on bottom:**
- Heat too high
- Not enough liquid
- Didn't rest (moisture not redistributed)

**Not flavorful:**
- Used water instead of stock
- Didn't toast rice long enough
- Not enough salt

## Rice Selection

**Best for Pilaf:**
- **Long-grain white rice** (jasmine, basmati)
- Grains stay separate when cooked
- Light, fluffy texture

**Avoid:**
- Short-grain rice (sushi rice) - too sticky
- Medium-grain rice - somewhat sticky
- Arborio - for risotto only

## Pro Tips

**Restaurant Secrets:**
- Every steakhouse makes pilaf daily
- Keeps warm in hotel pan (holding oven)
- Often made with chicken base + water (instant stock)
- Bay leaf is always used (subtle but important)
- Butter + oil prevents butter from burning

**For Best Results:**
- Use good stock (homemade or quality store-bought)
- Toast rice until fragrant (don't skip)
- Precise ratio (1:2 rice to liquid)
- Never lift lid while cooking
- Always rest 10 minutes
- Fluff with fork, never stir

## Holding for Service

**Restaurant Method:**
- Make large batch
- Keep covered in warm oven (200°F)
- Fluff occasionally
- Holds up to 2 hours

**Home Method:**
- Make just before serving
- Or keep covered, reheat gently
- Add splash of stock if drying out

## Scaling

**For 2 servings:**
- 3/4 cup (150g) rice
- 1.5 cups (360ml) stock
- Same method, same timing

**For 8-10 servings:**
- 3 cups (600g) rice
- 6 cups (1.4L) stock
- Same method, cook 20-22 minutes
- Use larger pot
