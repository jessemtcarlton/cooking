# 🌟 Ruth's Chris Signature Sizzling Steak

## Overview
THE signature steakhouse experience. Steak arrives sizzling in 500°F butter on a heated plate. This copycat recipe replicates the exact technique and presentation that made Ruth's Chris famous.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **BROILER REQUIRED**: The 500°F+ finish is essential - can't substitute
- **PLATE MUST BE SCORCHING**: 500°F plate is the signature (put in 500°F oven 10 min)
- **BUTTER WILL SMOKE**: Have exhaust on high, windows open
- **STAND BACK**: Butter sizzles violently when steak hits hot plate
- **USDA Prime**: Ruth's uses Prime beef - Choice minimum, Prime preferred

## The Ruth's Chris Secret
1. **High-quality beef** (USDA Prime, well-marbled)
2. **Simple seasoning** (just butter, salt, pepper, parsley)
3. **Broiler finish** (1800°F broilers in restaurant, 500°F+ home)
4. **500°F plate** (creates the signature sizzle)
5. **Butter pool** (melted butter tableside presentation)

## Equipment
- Heavy oven-safe skillet
- Broiler
- Oven-safe serving plate (cast iron or thick ceramic)
- Instant-read thermometer
- Tongs
- Oven mitts (CRITICAL)

## Ingredients (per steak)
- 1 ribeye or NY strip, 1.5" thick (12-16 oz, USDA Prime preferred)
- 1 tbsp (14g) butter (for cooking)
- 3 tbsp (42g) butter (for plate sizzle)
- 1 tsp (5g) kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp (2g) coarse black pepper
- 1 tbsp (4g) fresh parsley, chopped
- Optional: 1 tsp (3g) garlic butter

## Method

### Step 1: Prep Steak (40 minutes before)
1. Remove steak from fridge 40 minutes before
2. Pat completely DRY with paper towels
3. Season both sides generously:
   - Kosher salt
   - Coarse black pepper
4. Let sit at room temp

### Step 2: Preheat Plate and Broiler (15 minutes before serving)
1. Place oven-safe serving plate in oven
2. Heat oven to 500°F (260°C)
3. Let plate heat 10-15 minutes
4. 🚨 **PLATE MUST BE SCORCHING HOT**
5. Set broiler to HIGH, let preheat

### Step 3: Sear the Steak (6-8 minutes)
1. Heat oven-safe skillet over HIGH heat (5 min)
2. Add 1 tbsp (14g) butter
3. Immediately add steak (butter will foam and brown)
4. Sear 3-4 minutes first side (don't move)
5. Flip, sear 3-4 minutes second side
6. Target internal temp: 120°F (49°C) for medium-rare

### Step 4: Broiler Finish (2-4 minutes - THE SIGNATURE)
1. Transfer skillet to broiler (4-6" from element)
2. Broil 1-2 minutes
3. Check temp: Looking for 125-130°F (52-54°C)
4. 🚨 **WATCH CONSTANTLY** - can go from perfect to overcooked in 30 seconds
5. Remove when internal temp hits 125°F (52°C)

### Step 5: The Sizzling Plate Presentation (SIGNATURE MOVE)
1. **VERY CAREFULLY** remove 500°F plate from oven (thick oven mitts!)
2. Add 3 tbsp (42g) butter to center of screaming-hot plate
3. Butter will instantly melt and start smoking
4. Immediately place steak on butter
5. 🚨 **BUTTER WILL SIZZLE AND SPATTER** - stand back
6. Butter bubbles and browns around steak
7. Sprinkle chopped parsley over top
8. Serve IMMEDIATELY to table while sizzling

### Step 6: Serve
1. Warn diners plate is 500°F
2. Steak continues to sizzle for 2-3 minutes
3. Butter creates instant pan sauce
4. Temperature will rise to 130-135°F (perfect medium-rare)

## Ruth's Chris Steak Doneness Guide
(Internal temp after rest on hot plate)

- **Rare**: 125-130°F - Cool red center
- **Medium-Rare**: 130-135°F ⭐ **SIGNATURE** - Warm red center
- **Medium**: 135-145°F - Warm pink center
- **Medium-Well**: 145-155°F - Slightly pink
- **Well-Done**: 155°F+ - No pink (please don't do this)

## The Science Behind the Sizzle
- **500°F plate** = Maillard reaction continues (browning, flavor development)
- **Butter basting** = Rich, nutty brown butter flavor
- **Tableside theater** = Visual and auditory experience
- **Keeps steak hot** = Stays perfect temp longer

## Troubleshooting

**Steak overcooked:**
- Pulled too late from broiler
- Plate too hot (reduce to 450°F)
- Broiled too long

**Plate not sizzling:**
- Plate wasn't hot enough (needs full 15 min at 500°F)
- Plate too thin (use cast iron or thick ceramic)
- Not enough butter

**Butter burned/black:**
- Plate TOO hot (reduce to 450°F)
- Left on plate too long before adding steak

**Smoke detector going off:**
- Normal! This is high-heat cooking
- Open windows, turn on exhaust
- This is how restaurants do it

## Pro Tips

**At the Restaurant:**
- They use 1800°F broilers (commercial grade)
- Plates heated in broiler queue area
- Butter is clarified butter (higher smoke point)
- Steaks come from specific suppliers
- All steaks are USDA Prime, never Choice

**For Best Home Results:**
- Buy USDA Prime from Costco
- Use cast iron serving plate (holds heat best)
- Clarify your butter (optional but more authentic)
- Let plate heat full 15 minutes
- Have everything ready before plating (no delays)

**Safety:**
- Wear oven mitts handling 500°F plate
- Warn everyone plate is extremely hot
- Use trivets on table
- Keep kids/pets away
- Have baking soda nearby (in case butter ignites)
