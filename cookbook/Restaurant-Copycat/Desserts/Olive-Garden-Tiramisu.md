# 🌟 Olive Garden Tiramisu (Copycat)

## Overview
The classic Italian dessert that Olive Garden is famous for - layers of espresso-soaked ladyfingers and creamy mascarpone filling, dusted with cocoa. Light, creamy, with coffee flavor throughout. This copycat creates the exact balance of cream, coffee, and cocoa that makes Olive Garden's version so beloved.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **MASCARPONE IS MANDATORY**: Cream cheese won't give authentic flavor/texture
- **ESPRESSO MUST COOL**: Hot coffee will melt cream - must be room temp
- **QUICK DIP ONLY**: 1-2 seconds per side or ladyfingers get mushy
- **CHILL OVERNIGHT**: Flavors must meld (8+ hours minimum)
- **RAW EGGS**: Traditional recipe uses raw eggs - use pasteurized if concerned
- **ZABAGLIONE METHOD**: Eggs + sugar whisked over heat (safe, authentic)

## The Olive Garden Secret
1. **Mascarpone cheese** (Italian cream cheese - rich, smooth)
2. **Zabaglione base** (eggs + sugar whisked over heat - safe, silky)
3. **Strong espresso** (coffee flavor throughout)
4. **Marsala wine** (traditional, adds depth)
5. **Savoiardi cookies** (Italian ladyfinger cookies)
6. **Heavy cream** (whipped, lightens mascarpone)
7. **Cocoa powder** (unsweetened, dusted on top)
8. **Overnight chill** (flavors meld, texture sets)

## Equipment
- 9x13" baking dish or trifle dish
- Double boiler or heatproof bowl over saucepan
- Electric mixer (hand or stand)
- Mixing bowls (3)
- Whisk
- Shallow dish (for dipping cookies)
- Fine-mesh strainer or sifter (for cocoa)

## Ingredients (serves 12-15)

**For Mascarpone Filling:**
- 6 large egg yolks
- 3/4 cup (150g) granulated sugar
- 1/3 cup (80ml) whole milk
- 1 1/2 cups (12 oz/340g) mascarpone cheese, room temperature
- 1 3/4 cups (420ml) heavy cream, cold
- 1/4 cup (60ml) Marsala wine (or dark rum)
- 1 tsp (5ml) vanilla extract

**For Coffee Soak:**
- 2 cups (480ml) strong brewed espresso or very strong coffee
- 1/4 cup (50g) granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup (60ml) Marsala wine (or coffee liqueur like Kahlúa)

**For Assembly:**
- 40-48 savoiardi (Italian ladyfinger cookies)
- 1/4 cup (25g) unsweetened cocoa powder (for dusting)
- Dark chocolate shavings (optional garnish)

## Method

### Step 1: Make Coffee Soak (Can do ahead)
1. Brew 2 cups (480ml) very strong espresso or coffee
2. While hot, stir in 1/4 cup (50g) sugar until dissolved
3. Add 1/4 cup (60ml) Marsala wine
4. Let cool to room temperature
5. Pour into shallow dish for dipping
6. 🚨 **MUST BE COOL** - hot coffee melts cream

### Step 2: Make Zabaglione (Cooked Egg Base)

**Cook Eggs and Sugar:**
1. Fill saucepan with 2" water, bring to simmer
2. In heatproof bowl, whisk together:
   - 6 egg yolks
   - 3/4 cup (150g) sugar
   - 1/3 cup (80ml) milk
3. Place bowl over simmering water (don't touch water)
4. Whisk constantly for 8-10 minutes
5. Mixture should:
   - Thicken considerably
   - Become pale yellow
   - Reach 160°F (71°C) on thermometer (safe temp)
   - Coat back of spoon
6. Remove from heat
7. Whisk in 1/4 cup (60ml) Marsala wine
8. Whisk in 1 tsp vanilla
9. Let cool to room temperature (15-20 min)
10. 🚨 **MUST REACH 160°F** - safe temp for eggs

### Step 3: Mix with Mascarpone
1. In large bowl, place 1 1/2 cups (340g) room temp mascarpone
2. Add cooled zabaglione
3. Beat with mixer on medium until smooth and creamy
4. No lumps should remain
5. Should be thick, pale, smooth
6. Set aside

### Step 4: Whip Cream
1. In separate cold bowl with clean beaters:
2. Pour 1 3/4 cups (420ml) cold heavy cream
3. Beat on medium-high until stiff peaks form
4. Should be thick, hold shape
5. Don't overbeat (will become butter)

### Step 5: Fold Together
1. Add whipped cream to mascarpone mixture
2. Fold gently with spatula
3. Use folding motion (cut down, across bottom, up and over)
4. Fold just until combined
5. Should be light, fluffy, mousse-like
6. No streaks of cream should remain
7. 🚨 **GENTLE FOLDING** - don't deflate whipped cream

### Step 6: Assemble First Layer

**Bottom Layer:**
1. Have cooled coffee mixture in shallow dish
2. Take one ladyfinger cookie
3. Dip in coffee 1-2 seconds per side
4. Just enough to soak, not falling apart
5. Place in bottom of 9x13" dish
6. Repeat until bottom covered (about 20-24 cookies)
7. Arrange tightly (no gaps)
8. 🚨 **QUICK DIP ONLY** - 1-2 seconds total

**First Cream Layer:**
1. Spread half of mascarpone mixture over cookies
2. Use offset spatula to smooth
3. Should be even layer covering all cookies

### Step 7: Assemble Second Layer

**Top Cookie Layer:**
1. Dip remaining cookies in coffee (1-2 seconds each)
2. Arrange on top of cream layer
3. Cover completely

**Final Cream Layer:**
1. Spread remaining mascarpone mixture on top
2. Smooth with spatula
3. Should be even, smooth surface

### Step 8: Dust with Cocoa
1. Place 1/4 cup (25g) cocoa powder in fine strainer
2. Dust entire surface evenly
3. Should have nice layer of cocoa
4. Optional: create pattern by dragging spoon through cocoa

### Step 9: Chill (THE CRITICAL STEP)
1. Cover tightly with plastic wrap
2. Refrigerate 8 hours MINIMUM
3. **OVERNIGHT IS BETTER** (12-24 hours)
4. Flavors meld, texture sets
5. Cookies soften, cream firms
6. 🚨 **DON'T SKIP CHILL TIME** - needs overnight

### Step 10: Serve
1. Remove from fridge 10 min before serving (easier to cut)
2. Cut into squares with sharp knife
3. Wipe knife between cuts
4. Optional: garnish with chocolate shavings
5. Dust with more cocoa just before serving
6. Serve cold

## Perfect Tiramisu Indicators
- **Texture**: Creamy, light, mousse-like (not dense)
- **Layers**: Distinct cookie and cream layers
- **Coffee**: Noticeable but not overpowering
- **Sweetness**: Balanced (not too sweet)
- **Consistency**: Firms up but still creamy
- **Appearance**: Smooth cocoa top, layered cross-section

## Olive Garden Serving Style
- Cut into squares
- Plated individually
- Fresh cocoa dusted on top
- Chocolate shavings garnish
- Served cold
- After-dinner dessert

## Timing

**Total Time: 9+ hours (includes overnight chill)**
- Make coffee: 10 min
- Make zabaglione: 15 min
- Mix filling: 10 min
- Whip cream: 5 min
- Assemble: 15 min
- Chill: 8+ hours (overnight)
- **Total active time: 55 min**

**Must Make Ahead:**
- Best made 1 day ahead
- Can make up to 2 days ahead
- Gets better as it sits
- Flavors meld overnight

## Troubleshooting

**Soggy, falling apart:**
- Dipped cookies too long (1-2 seconds only)
- Coffee was too hot
- Didn't chill long enough
- Used wrong cookies (need Italian ladyfingers)

**Too dry:**
- Didn't dip cookies enough
- Coffee soak wasn't strong enough
- Used wrong cookies

**Grainy texture:**
- Mascarpone was cold (must be room temp)
- Didn't cook zabaglione enough
- Overmixed after adding whipped cream

**Too sweet:**
- Reduce sugar in filling by 2 tbsp next time
- Use more coffee in soak
- Increase Marsala

**Not enough coffee flavor:**
- Coffee wasn't strong enough (needs espresso strength)
- Didn't add enough coffee to soak
- Dipped cookies too quickly

**Cream deflated:**
- Overmixed when folding in whipped cream
- Mascarpone mixture was too warm
- Cream overwhipped

## Variations

### No-Alcohol Version
- Omit Marsala
- Use coffee liqueur flavoring (non-alcoholic)
- Or just use strong coffee

### Chocolate Tiramisu
- Add 1/4 cup cocoa powder to mascarpone mixture
- Use chocolate shavings between layers
- More chocolate flavor

### Strawberry Tiramisu
- Replace coffee with strawberry syrup
- Add sliced strawberries between layers
- Dust with powdered sugar instead of cocoa

### Individual Tiramisu
- Make in small cups or glasses
- Layer same way
- Perfect for parties

### Vegan Tiramisu
- Use cashew cream instead of mascarpone
- Aquafaba instead of eggs
- Dairy-free cookies
- Complex but possible

## Storage

**Refrigerator:**
- Store covered up to 3 days
- Gets better day 2-3
- Cocoa will absorb moisture (redust before serving)

**Freezing:**
- Can freeze up to 1 month
- Thaw in fridge overnight
- Texture slightly changes (still good)
- Redust with cocoa after thawing

## Pro Tips

**Restaurant Secrets:**
- Olive Garden makes tiramisu in morning, serves that evening
- Zabaglione method (cooked eggs - safer than raw)
- Mascarpone imported from Italy (specific flavor)
- Strong espresso (Italian roast)
- Ladyfingers dipped quickly (1-2 seconds)
- Chilled overnight minimum
- Fresh cocoa dusted just before serving

**For Best Results:**
- Real mascarpone cheese (not cream cheese)
- Italian ladyfingers (Savoiardi brand)
- Very strong espresso or coffee
- Quick dip only (1-2 seconds per cookie)
- Room temp mascarpone (blends smooth)
- Gentle folding (keeps cream fluffy)
- Overnight chill (flavors meld)
- Fresh cocoa dust before serving

**Mascarpone vs Cream Cheese:**
- **Mascarpone**: Italian, sweeter, smoother, authentic
- **Cream Cheese**: American, tangy, denser, not authentic
- Can't substitute (flavor/texture wrong)
- Find mascarpone at most grocery stores (imported cheese section)

**Ladyfinger Cookies:**
- Use Savoiardi (Italian ladyfingers)
- Firm, dry, absorb coffee well
- Not soft bakery ladyfingers (too soft)
- Find in Italian section or international aisle
