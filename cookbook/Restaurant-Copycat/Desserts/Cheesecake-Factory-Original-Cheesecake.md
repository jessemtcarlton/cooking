# 🌟 The Cheesecake Factory Original Cheesecake (Copycat)

## Overview
THE cheesecake that made Cheesecake Factory famous. Tall, creamy, dense but smooth, with a buttery graham cracker crust. This copycat recipe replicates the exact texture and flavor of the iconic original.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **ROOM TEMP INGREDIENTS**: Cream cheese must be room temp (8 hours) or will be lumpy
- **WATER BATH MANDATORY**: No water bath = cracked, curdled cheesecake
- **BAKE LOW AND SLOW**: 325°F for 1 hour 20 min - no shortcuts
- **DON'T OPEN OVEN**: Opening = temperature drop = cracks
- **COOL GRADUALLY**: Oven→room temp→fridge or will crack
- **CHILL OVERNIGHT**: Needs 8+ hours to set properly

## The Cheesecake Factory Secret
1. **Extra cream cheese** (2 lbs for ultra-rich texture)
2. **Sour cream** (tangy balance to richness)
3. **Water bath** (even gentle cooking, no cracks)
4. **Slow bake + slow cool** (prevents cracks)
5. **9" springform** (tall, dramatic presentation)
6. **Overnight chill** (flavors meld, texture sets)

## Equipment
- 9" springform pan
- Heavy-duty aluminum foil (for water bath)
- Stand mixer or hand mixer
- Large roasting pan (for water bath)
- Rubber spatula
- Food processor (for crust)

## Ingredients

**For Graham Cracker Crust:**
- 1.5 cups (150g) graham cracker crumbs (about 10-12 crackers)
- 1/4 cup (50g) sugar
- 6 tbsp (84g) butter, melted

**For Cheesecake Filling:**
- 2 lbs (907g) cream cheese, room temp (CRITICAL - 8 hours out of fridge)
- 1 1/4 cups (250g) sugar
- 1/4 cup (30g) all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp (5ml) vanilla extract
- 1/2 tsp (3ml) lemon extract (optional but authentic)
- 4 large eggs, room temp
- 2/3 cup (160ml) sour cream, room temp

## Method

### Step 1: Bring Ingredients to Room Temp (8 hours before)
1. Remove cream cheese from fridge 8 hours before
2. Leave on counter in packaging
3. Should be soft when pressed (not cold or hard)
4. Also bring eggs and sour cream to room temp (1 hour before)
5. 🚨 **THIS IS CRITICAL** - cold cream cheese = lumpy cheesecake

### Step 2: Make Graham Cracker Crust
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (177°C)
2. Process graham crackers to fine crumbs in food processor
   - Or place in zip-top bag, crush with rolling pin
3. Mix 1.5 cups (150g) crumbs + 1/4 cup (50g) sugar
4. Add 6 tbsp (84g) melted butter
5. Mix until resembles wet sand
6. Press into bottom of 9" springform pan
7. Press firmly with measuring cup bottom (compact crust)
8. Bake at 350°F for 8-10 minutes (light golden)
9. Remove, let cool while making filling

### Step 3: Wrap Pan for Water Bath
1. Once crust cooled, wrap bottom and sides of springform pan
2. Use 2 layers heavy-duty aluminum foil
3. Wrap tightly - prevents water from seeping in
4. 🚨 **WATER LEAK = RUINED CHEESECAKE**
5. Make foil go up sides 3-4"

### Step 4: Make Filling (Mix SLOWLY - No Air Bubbles)
1. Reduce oven to 325°F (163°C)
2. In stand mixer (or with hand mixer):
   - Beat 2 lbs (907g) room-temp cream cheese on LOW speed
   - Beat 2-3 minutes until smooth and creamy
   - 🚨 **LOW SPEED ONLY** - high speed adds air bubbles = cracks
3. Add 1 1/4 cups (250g) sugar
   - Beat on LOW until combined (2 min)
4. Add 1/4 cup (30g) flour
   - Beat on LOW until combined (1 min)
5. Add 1 tsp vanilla + 1/2 tsp lemon extract
   - Beat on LOW until combined (30 sec)
6. Add eggs ONE AT A TIME:
   - Beat on LOW after each egg just until incorporated
   - Don't overbeat
   - Scrape bowl between additions
7. Add 2/3 cup (160ml) sour cream
   - Fold in gently with spatula
8. Final mix: Stir gently by hand to combine
9. Should be smooth, thick, pourable
10. Pour into cooled crust

### Step 5: Create Water Bath
1. Place wrapped springform pan in large roasting pan
2. Put roasting pan on oven rack (easier than transferring)
3. Carefully pour hot water into roasting pan
4. Water should come 1" up sides of springform pan
5. 🚨 **DON'T splash water into cheesecake**

### Step 6: Bake (1 hour 15-20 minutes)
1. Bake at 325°F (163°C) for 1 hour 15 minutes minimum
2. **SET TIMER FOR 1 HOUR 15 MINUTES**
3. 🚨 **DON'T OPEN OVEN** - temperature drop causes cracks
4. Check doneness:
   - Edges should be set and slightly puffed
   - Center should jiggle slightly (like Jell-O)
   - Should NOT be liquid in center
   - Small crack around edge OK (will settle)
5. If center too jiggly, bake 5 more minutes

### Step 7: Cool Gradually (CRITICAL - Prevents Cracks)

**In Oven (1 hour):**
1. Turn OFF oven
2. Crack oven door open 1"
3. Leave cheesecake in oven 1 hour
4. **SET TIMER FOR 1 HOUR**
5. This slow cool prevents cracks

**Room Temp (2 hours):**
1. Remove from oven
2. Remove from water bath carefully
3. Remove foil
4. Let cool at room temp 2 hours
5. Should be room temp to touch

**Refrigerator (8+ hours):**
1. Cover loosely with plastic wrap (don't let wrap touch surface)
2. Refrigerate overnight (minimum 8 hours)
3. 🚨 **MUST CHILL OVERNIGHT** - sets texture, develops flavor

### Step 8: Serve
1. Run thin knife around edge of pan
2. Remove springform ring
3. Slide onto serving plate
4. Slice with hot, wet knife (dip in hot water, wipe clean between cuts)
5. Serve cold

## Perfect Cheesecake Indicators
- **Height**: 2-3" tall
- **Top**: Smooth, no major cracks (small cracks OK)
- **Texture**: Dense, creamy, smooth (not fluffy)
- **Consistency**: Slices clean, not gummy or runny
- **Flavor**: Rich, creamy, slightly tangy
- **Crust**: Buttery, holds together

## Troubleshooting

**Cheesecake cracked:**
- Overbaked (center should jiggle slightly)
- Cooled too quickly (must cool gradually)
- Opened oven during baking
- Too much air beaten into batter
- No water bath

**Cheesecake is lumpy:**
- Cream cheese was too cold (must be room temp 8 hours)
- Didn't beat cream cheese long enough before adding other ingredients
- Added eggs too fast

**Cheesecake is gummy/dense:**
- Overbaked
- Too many eggs
- Overbeat batter (incorporated too much air)

**Crust is soggy:**
- Water leaked into springform (foil wasn't tight)
- Didn't prebake crust
- Too much butter in crust

**Center is too soft/runny:**
- Underbaked
- Didn't chill long enough (needs 8+ hours)
- Cut too soon after removing from fridge

## Topping Variations

### Fresh Strawberry
- Macerate 1 lb strawberries with 1/4 cup sugar
- Spoon over slice when serving

### Chocolate Ganache
- Heat 1 cup heavy cream
- Pour over 8 oz chopped chocolate
- Stir until smooth, let cool
- Spread over chilled cheesecake

### Caramel
- Drizzle with caramel sauce when serving

### Fruit Compote
- Simmer berries with sugar until syrupy
- Cool, spoon over slices

## Storage

**Refrigerator:**
- Cover tightly, store 5-7 days
- Quality best first 3 days

**Freezer:**
- Wrap tightly in plastic, then foil
- Freeze up to 2 months
- Thaw overnight in refrigerator
- Quality very good when frozen properly

## Pro Tips

**Restaurant Secrets:**
- Cheesecake Factory makes hundreds daily
- They use commercial ovens with precise temperature control
- All cheesecakes made day before (overnight chill mandatory)
- Water bath is non-negotiable
- They use cream cheese from specific supplier (but regular works)

**For Best Results:**
- Room temp cream cheese is #1 most important factor
- Mix on low speed only (prevents air bubbles)
- Water bath prevents cracks and ensures even cooking
- Gradual cooling is critical (oven→room temp→fridge)
- Overnight chill improves texture and flavor
- Hot knife for clean slices

**Cream Cheese Tips:**
- Use full-fat only (low-fat won't work)
- Must be SOFT (room temp 8 hours)
- Don't microwave to soften (melts unevenly)
- Test by pressing - should dent easily

## Why This Recipe Works

**Science:**
- Room temp ingredients mix smoothly (no lumps)
- Flour stabilizes filling (prevents cracks)
- Sour cream adds tang, reduces richness
- Water bath provides gentle even heat
- Gradual cool prevents thermal shock (cracking)
- Overnight chill allows proteins to set fully

**Technique:**
- Low-speed mixing prevents air incorporation
- Water bath creates humid environment
- Springform pan allows easy removal
- Foil wrap prevents water leaks
