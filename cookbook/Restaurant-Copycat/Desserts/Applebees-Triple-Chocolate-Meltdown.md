# 🌟 Applebee's Triple Chocolate Meltdown (Copycat)

## Overview
The ultimate chocolate indulgence - warm chocolate cake with hot fudge center, topped with vanilla ice cream, hot fudge, and walnuts. Similar to Chili's molten cake but with Applebee's unique twist - more fudge, more toppings, more chocolate. This copycat creates the exact triple-chocolate experience.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **FUDGE CENTER IS KEY**: Place fudge piece in middle before baking (different from Chili's)
- **DON'T OVERBAKE**: 13-15 minutes only or fudge center won't be molten
- **BUTTER RAMEKINS WELL**: Cake must release cleanly
- **TOAST WALNUTS**: Raw walnuts are bitter, toasted are nutty and sweet
- **SERVE IMMEDIATELY**: Fudge sets quickly, must serve within 3 minutes
- **HIGH OVEN TEMP**: 450°F (different from other molten cakes)

## The Applebee's Secret
1. **Fudge piece in center** (guarantees molten center every time)
2. **Three chocolates**: Cocoa powder in cake + fudge center + fudge topping
3. **Higher oven temp** (450°F - sets outside fast, center stays molten)
4. **Toasted walnuts** (signature Applebee's touch)
5. **Vanilla ice cream** (cold contrast)
6. **Hot fudge topping** (extra chocolate layer)
7. **Whipped cream** (finishing touch)

## Equipment
- 4 ramekins (6 oz/180ml each)
- Baking sheet
- Mixing bowl
- Whisk
- Small saucepan (for making fudge centers)
- Ice cube tray or small molds (for freezing fudge)
- Small skillet (for toasting walnuts)

## Ingredients (makes 4 cakes)

**For Fudge Centers (Make Ahead):**
- 1/2 cup (85g) semi-sweet chocolate chips
- 1/4 cup (60ml) heavy cream
- 1 tbsp (14g) butter

**For Chocolate Cakes:**
- 1/2 cup (113g) butter
- 4 oz (113g) semi-sweet chocolate, chopped
- 2 large eggs
- 2 large egg yolks
- 1/4 cup (50g) granulated sugar
- 2 tbsp (10g) unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1/4 cup (30g) all-purpose flour
- Pinch of salt
- 1 tsp (5ml) vanilla extract

**For Preparing Ramekins:**
- 2 tbsp (28g) softened butter
- 2 tbsp (10g) cocoa powder

**For Toasted Walnuts:**
- 1/2 cup (60g) walnuts, chopped

**For Serving:**
- 4 scoops vanilla ice cream
- 1 cup (240ml) hot fudge sauce, warmed
- Whipped cream
- Additional chocolate shavings (optional)

## Method

### Step 1: Make Fudge Centers (Do This First - Must Freeze)

**Make Ganache:**
1. In small saucepan:
2. Heat 1/4 cup (60ml) heavy cream until simmering
3. Remove from heat
4. Add 1/2 cup (85g) chocolate chips
5. Add 1 tbsp (14g) butter
6. Let sit 2 minutes
7. Whisk until smooth and glossy
8. Should be thick, fudgy

**Freeze:**
1. Pour into 4 sections of ice cube tray
2. Or use small silicone molds
3. Freeze at least 2 hours until solid
4. Can make up to 1 month ahead
5. 🚨 **MUST BE FROZEN SOLID** - soft fudge won't create molten center

### Step 2: Toast Walnuts (Can Do Ahead)
1. Heat small skillet over medium heat
2. Add 1/2 cup (60g) chopped walnuts
3. Toast 3-5 minutes, stirring frequently
4. Should smell nutty, turn golden
5. Don't burn (watch closely)
6. Remove to plate, let cool
7. Set aside for topping

### Step 3: Prep Ramekins
1. Generously butter inside of 4 ramekins
2. Dust with cocoa powder
3. Tap out excess
4. Place on baking sheet
5. Set aside

### Step 4: Make Cake Batter

**Melt Chocolate and Butter:**
1. In microwave or double boiler:
2. Melt 1/2 cup (113g) butter
3. Add 4 oz (113g) chopped chocolate
4. Stir until melted and smooth
5. Let cool 5 minutes

**Mix Batter:**
1. In mixing bowl:
2. Add 2 whole eggs
3. Add 2 egg yolks
4. Add 1/4 cup (50g) sugar
5. Whisk vigorously 2-3 minutes until thick
6. Add melted chocolate mixture
7. Add 1 tsp vanilla
8. Whisk to combine
9. Sift in:
   - 2 tbsp (10g) cocoa powder
   - 1/4 cup (30g) flour
   - Pinch salt
10. Fold gently just until combined
11. Don't overmix

### Step 5: Fill Ramekins with Fudge Centers
1. Preheat oven to 450°F (232°C)
2. Divide batter evenly among ramekins (fill 1/3 full)
3. Remove frozen fudge pieces from freezer
4. Place one frozen fudge piece in center of each
5. Cover with remaining batter (fill to 2/3 full)
6. Smooth tops
7. 🚨 **FUDGE MUST BE CENTERED** - this creates molten center

### Step 6: Bake (13-15 minutes)
1. Bake at 450°F (232°C) for 13-15 minutes
2. **TIMING**:
   - 13 min: Very molten (may be runny)
   - 14 min: Perfect molten (IDEAL)
   - 15 min: Slightly molten
   - 16+ min: No molten center
3. Edges should be set
4. Centers should still jiggle slightly
5. Tops should look dry
6. 🚨 **HIGH TEMP, SHORT TIME** - different from other recipes

### Step 7: Invert and Serve Immediately

**Invert:**
1. Remove from oven
2. Let rest 1 minute
3. Run knife around edges
4. Place serving plate upside down on ramekin
5. Using oven mitt, flip together
6. Tap ramekin gently
7. Lift off ramekin
8. Cake should release with fudge center intact

**Top (The Triple Chocolate):**
1. **First Chocolate**: The cake itself (cocoa powder)
2. **Second Chocolate**: Cut into cake - fudge flows out
3. **Third Chocolate**: Drizzle hot fudge sauce over top

**Complete Plating:**
1. Place inverted cake in center of plate
2. Add scoop of vanilla ice cream beside cake
3. Drizzle warm hot fudge over cake and ice cream
4. Sprinkle toasted walnuts on top
5. Add dollop of whipped cream
6. Optional: chocolate shavings
7. **SERVE IMMEDIATELY** - within 2-3 minutes
8. 🚨 **FUDGE SETS FAST** - must serve hot

- Make fudge centers: 10 min + 2 hrs freezing
- Toast walnuts: 5 min
- Prep ramekins: 5 min
- Make batter: 10 min
- Bake: 14 min
- Serve: 3 min
- **Active time: 35 min**

**Make-Ahead:**
- Fudge centers: Make up to 1 month ahead, freeze
- Toasted walnuts: Make up to 1 week ahead
- Fill ramekins: Up to 4 hours ahead, refrigerate
- Bake just before serving (add 1-2 min if cold)

## Troubleshooting

**No molten center:**
- Overbaked (reduce time by 1 min)
- Fudge piece wasn't frozen solid
- Oven temp too low (needs 450°F)

**Center too liquidy:**
- Underbaked
- Increase time by 1 minute
- Fudge piece was too large

**Cake stuck in ramekin:**
- Didn't butter and dust well enough
- Waited too long to invert
- Didn't run knife around edge

**Cake collapsed:**
- Overmixed batter
- Opened oven door during baking
- Fudge piece sank to bottom

**Fudge didn't flow:**
- Fudge wasn't centered (sank to bottom)
- Overbaked
- Waited too long to serve (fudge set)

- Make weeks ahead

- Store in freezer bag
- Use from frozen

**Day-Of:**
- Toast walnuts in morning
- Prep ramekins morning
- Fill ramekins up to 4 hours ahead
- Refrigerate
- Bake just before serving (add 1-2 min)

**Complete Freezer Stash:**
- Fill ramekins
- Freeze unbaked
- Wrap in plastic, then foil
- Freeze up to 1 month
- Bake from frozen: 17-19 minutes
- Perfect for unexpected guests

**Baked:**
- Not recommended
- Must serve fresh from oven
- Fudge sets as it cools
