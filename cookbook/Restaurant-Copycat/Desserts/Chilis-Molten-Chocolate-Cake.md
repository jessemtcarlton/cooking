# 🌟 Chili's Molten Chocolate Cake (Copycat)

## Overview
The dessert that made molten lava cakes famous at chain restaurants. Warm chocolate cake with a gooey, molten center, served with vanilla ice cream and topped with hot fudge. When you cut into it, warm chocolate oozes out. This copycat creates the perfect molten center every time.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **TIMING IS EVERYTHING**: Bake 12-14 minutes exactly (sets outside, molten inside)
- **BUTTER RAMEKINS THOROUGHLY**: Cake must release or you lose the molten center
- **BATTER MUST BE ROOM TEMP**: Cold batter won't create molten center
- **SERVE IMMEDIATELY**: Within 2-3 minutes of removing from oven
- **DON'T OVERBAKE**: 1-2 minutes too long = no molten center
- **HIGH-QUALITY CHOCOLATE**: Semi-sweet or bittersweet (not chips)

## The Chili's Secret
1. **Individual ramekins** (perfect portion, molten center)
2. **High-quality chocolate** (melted with butter)
3. **Whole eggs + yolks** (richness and structure)
4. **Short bake time** (sets outside, molten inside)
5. **Inverted onto plate** (presentation)
6. **Vanilla ice cream** (cold contrast to hot cake)
7. **Hot fudge drizzle** (extra chocolate intensity)

## Equipment
- 4 ramekins (6 oz/180ml each)
- Double boiler or microwave-safe bowl
- Mixing bowls (2)
- Whisk
- Electric mixer (hand or stand)
- Baking sheet
- Small offset spatula or knife

## Ingredients (makes 4 cakes)

**For Molten Cakes:**
- 6 oz (170g) semi-sweet or bittersweet chocolate, chopped
- 1/2 cup (113g) butter, cut into pieces
- 2 large eggs, room temperature
- 2 large egg yolks, room temperature
- 1/4 cup (50g) granulated sugar
- 2 tbsp (16g) all-purpose flour
- Pinch of salt
- 1/2 tsp (2.5ml) vanilla extract

**For Preparing Ramekins:**
- 2 tbsp (28g) softened butter (for greasing)
- 2 tbsp (16g) cocoa powder (for dusting)

**For Serving (Chili's Style):**
- 4 scoops vanilla ice cream
- 1/2 cup (120ml) hot fudge sauce, warmed
- Whipped cream (optional)
- Chocolate shavings or cocoa powder (garnish)

## Method

### Step 1: Prep Ramekins (5 minutes - DO THIS FIRST)

**Butter and Dust:**
1. Using fingers or pastry brush:
2. Coat inside of each ramekin THOROUGHLY with softened butter
3. Get every surface - bottom, sides, rim
4. Add 1 tbsp cocoa powder to first ramekin
5. Tilt and rotate to coat all surfaces
6. Tap excess cocoa into next ramekin
7. Repeat for all 4 ramekins
8. Place on baking sheet
9. Refrigerate until needed
10. 🚨 **THOROUGH COATING CRITICAL** - or cake won't release

### Step 2: Melt Chocolate and Butter

**Double Boiler Method:**
1. Fill saucepan with 1-2" water
2. Bring to simmer
3. Place bowl on top (shouldn't touch water)
4. Add 6 oz (170g) chopped chocolate
5. Add 1/2 cup (113g) butter pieces
6. Stir occasionally until melted and smooth
7. Remove from heat
8. Let cool 5 minutes (should be warm, not hot)

**OR Microwave Method:**
1. In microwave-safe bowl:
2. Add chocolate and butter
3. Microwave 30 seconds, stir
4. Repeat until melted (2-3 times total)
5. Stir until smooth
6. Let cool 5 minutes

### Step 3: Make Batter

**Beat Eggs and Sugar:**
1. In mixing bowl with electric mixer:
2. Add 2 whole eggs
3. Add 2 egg yolks
4. Add 1/4 cup (50g) sugar
5. Add pinch of salt
6. Beat on medium-high speed 3-5 minutes
7. Should be thick, pale, fluffy
8. Ribbon stage (falls in ribbons when lifted)

**Fold in Chocolate:**
1. Add 1/2 tsp vanilla to melted chocolate
2. Pour chocolate mixture into beaten eggs
3. Fold gently with spatula
4. Fold just until combined (don't overmix)
5. Add 2 tbsp (16g) flour
6. Fold gently until no streaks remain
7. Batter should be smooth, glossy, pourable

### Step 4: Fill Ramekins
1. Preheat oven to 425°F (218°C)
2. Remove ramekins from fridge
3. Divide batter evenly among 4 ramekins
4. Fill about 3/4 full
5. Smooth tops with spatula
6. Wipe any drips from rim (will burn)
7. 🚨 **CAN REFRIGERATE NOW** up to 4 hours (see make-ahead)

### Step 5: Bake (12-14 minutes - THE CRITICAL STEP)
1. Place baking sheet with ramekins in 425°F oven
2. Bake 12-14 minutes
3. **TIMING IS EVERYTHING**:
   - 12 min: Very molten center (may be too liquidy)
   - 13 min: Perfect molten center (IDEAL)
   - 14 min: Slightly molten (safe choice)
   - 15+ min: No molten center (overcooked)
4. Edges should be set and puffed
5. Centers should jiggle slightly when moved
6. Tops should be dry but centers soft
7. 🚨 **WATCH CLOSELY** - don't overbake

### Step 6: Serve Immediately (THE PRESENTATION)

**Invert Method (Chili's Style):**
1. Remove from oven
2. Let rest 1 minute (no longer)
3. Run thin knife around edge of each cake
4. Place serving plate upside down on top of ramekin
5. Using oven mitt, flip together
6. Lift ramekin off carefully
7. Cake should release (molten center intact)
8. If stuck, tap ramekin bottom gently

**Plate:**
1. Place inverted cake in center of plate
2. Add scoop of vanilla ice cream beside cake
3. Drizzle warm hot fudge over cake and ice cream
4. Optional: whipped cream, chocolate shavings, cocoa dust
5. **SERVE IMMEDIATELY** - within 2-3 minutes
6. Cut into cake with spoon - chocolate should flow out
7. 🚨 **TIMING CRITICAL** - molten center sets as it cools

- **Appearance**: Inverted dome shape, glossy
- **The Flow**: Center pours out when cut

## Timing

**Total Time: 30 minutes**
- Prep ramekins: 5 min
- Melt chocolate: 5 min
- Make batter: 10 min
- Bake: 12-14 min
- Invert and serve: 2 min

**Make-Ahead:**
- Fill ramekins, cover, refrigerate up to 4 hours
- Bake just before serving (add 1-2 min to bake time if cold)
- Or freeze unbaked up to 1 month
- Bake from frozen: 16-18 minutes at 425°F

## Troubleshooting

**No molten center:**
- Overbaked (most common issue)
- Reduce bake time by 1 minute next time
- Oven temp too low (check with oven thermometer)
- Batter was cold when baked

**Too liquid/raw center:**
- Underbaked
- Increase bake time by 1 minute
- Oven temp too high
- Ramekins too large (batter spread too thin)

**Cake stuck in ramekin:**
- Didn't butter and dust thoroughly
- Waited too long to invert (sets as it cools)
- Didn't run knife around edge first

**Cake collapsed:**
- Overmixed batter (deflated eggs)
- Opened oven door during baking
- Overbaked

**Dry cake:**
- Way overbaked
- Too much flour
- Old chocolate (didn't melt smoothly)

## Variations

### White Chocolate Molten Cake
- Use white chocolate instead of semi-sweet
- Same method
- Lighter, sweeter

### Peanut Butter Center
- Add 1 tbsp peanut butter to center before baking
- Press into batter
- Peanut butter molten center

### Salted Caramel Center
- Add 1 tbsp salted caramel to center
- Press into batter
- Sweet-salty molten center

### Espresso Molten Cake
- Add 1 tbsp instant espresso powder to chocolate
- Coffee-chocolate combo
- More intense

### Individual Lava Cakes with Ganache Center
- Place chocolate ganache truffle in center
- Ensures molten center
- Foolproof method

## Make-Ahead Strategies

**Day-Of:**
- Prep and fill ramekins up to 4 hours ahead
- Cover and refrigerate
- Bake just before serving (add 1-2 min)

**Freeze for Later:**
- Fill ramekins
- Freeze on baking sheet until solid
- Wrap each in plastic, then foil
- Freeze up to 1 month
- Bake from frozen: 16-18 min at 425°F
- No thawing needed

## Storage

**Unbaked:**
- Refrigerate filled ramekins up to 4 hours
- Or freeze up to 1 month

**Baked:**
- Not recommended to store
- Best fresh from oven
- Can refrigerate baked cakes, reheat 15 seconds in microwave (not the same)

## Pro Tips

**Restaurant Secrets:**
- Chili's bakes to order (made fresh for each table)
- Ramekins prepped in morning, refrigerated
- Baked 13 minutes exactly (tested in their ovens)
- Inverted at table for drama
- Ice cream placed just before serving (dramatic melt)
- Chocolate shavings scraped from chocolate bar

**For Best Results:**
- Use high-quality chocolate (Ghirardelli, Valrhona, Guittard)
- Room temp eggs beat to more volume
- Don't overmix after adding flour
- Butter and dust ramekins thoroughly
- Set timer - watch closely
- Serve immediately (within 2-3 minutes)
- Practice timing (every oven different)

**Timing Test:**
- First time making, bake one at 12 min, one at 13 min, one at 14 min
- Cut into each, see which timing you prefer
- Note the perfect time for your oven
- Use that timing going forward

**Chocolate Choice:**
- Semi-sweet (most balanced)
- Bittersweet (more intense, less sweet)
- Dark (70% cacao - very intense)
- Don't use milk chocolate (too sweet, wrong texture)
- Don't use chocolate chips (have stabilizers)
