# 🍁 Traditional Quebec Poutine (Authentic Recipe)

## Overview
The dish that put Quebec on the culinary map. Fresh-cut fries, squeaky cheese curds, and rich brown gravy. Invented in rural Quebec in the 1950s, now Canada's national dish. This recipe creates authentic poutine with the perfect ratio of fries, curds, and gravy.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **FRESH CHEESE CURDS MANDATORY**: Must squeak when bitten (1-2 days old max)
- **DOUBLE FRY FRIES**: Blanch then crisp (or they'll be soggy under gravy)
- **GRAVY MUST BE HOT**: Melts curds slightly (the magic moment)
- **ASSEMBLE IMMEDIATELY**: Gravy cools fast, fries get soggy
- **SERVE AT ONCE**: Doesn't wait or reheat

## The Quebec Secret
1. **Fresh cheese curds** (squeaky, room temp, never refrigerated blocks)
2. **Thick-cut fries** (substantial enough to hold gravy)
3. **Double-fried** (crispy exterior, fluffy inside)
4. **Brown gravy** (chicken or beef based, well-seasoned)
5. **Hot assembly** (gravy melts curds slightly)
6. **Eaten immediately** (doesn't keep)

## Equipment
- Deep fryer or large pot
- Oil thermometer
- Large knife
- Wire rack and baking sheet
- Large serving bowl or plate
- Saucepan (for gravy)

## Ingredients (serves 2-4)

**For Fries:**
- 4 large russet potatoes (2 lbs/900g)
- Oil for frying (peanut or vegetable, 8-10 cups/2-2.4L)
- Kosher salt

**For Gravy:**
- 3 tbsp (42g) butter
- 1/4 cup (30g) all-purpose flour
- 2 cups (480ml) beef or chicken broth
- 1 tsp (5ml) Worcestershire sauce
- 1/2 tsp (3g) kosher salt
- 1/4 tsp (0.5g) black pepper
- 1 tsp (5ml) ketchup (traditional Quebec secret)

**For Topping:**
- 2 cups (300g) fresh cheese curds, room temperature
- 🚨 **MUST BE FRESH** - old curds don't squeak

## Method

### Step 1: Cut Fries
1. Peel potatoes (or leave skin on - traditional both ways)
2. Cut into 1/2" thick fries
3. Soak in cold water 30 minutes (removes starch)
4. Drain and pat completely dry

### Step 2: First Fry - Blanch (5-6 minutes)
1. Heat oil to 325°F (163°C)
2. Working in batches, fry potatoes 5-6 minutes
3. Should be cooked through but pale (not brown)
4. Remove to wire rack
5. Let cool 10 minutes (or up to 2 hours)

### Step 3: Make Gravy
1. Melt butter in saucepan over medium heat
2. Add flour, whisk 2-3 minutes (brown roux)
3. Gradually add broth, whisking constantly
4. Add Worcestershire, salt, pepper, ketchup
5. Simmer 5-10 minutes until thick
6. Keep hot

### Step 4: Second Fry - Crisp (3-5 minutes)
1. Heat oil to 375°F (190°C)
2. Fry blanched potatoes in batches
3. Fry 3-5 minutes until deep golden and crispy
4. Remove, drain briefly
5. Season with salt immediately
6. Keep warm while finishing batches

### Step 5: Assemble (THE CRITICAL MOMENT)
1. Place hot crispy fries in large bowl or plate
2. Top with cheese curds (generously - don't be shy)
3. Pour hot gravy over everything
4. **Wait 30 seconds** (gravy melts curds slightly)
5. Serve immediately with fork
6. 🚨 **EAT WITHIN 5 MINUTES** - doesn't keep

## Perfect Poutine Indicators
- **Fries**: Crispy, golden, substantial
- **Curds**: Squeaky, slightly melted on edges
- **Gravy**: Hot, savory, coating everything
- **Sound**: Curds squeak when bitten
- **Texture**: Crispy fries, melty curds, rich gravy
- **Temperature**: Piping hot

## Timing
**Total Time: 1 hour**
- Cut and soak fries: 40 min
- Make gravy: 15 min
- First fry: 10 min
- Second fry: 10 min
- Assemble: 2 min

## Cheese Curd Sourcing
**Where to Find:**
- Cheese shops (ask for fresh curds)
- Farmers markets
- Some grocery stores (dairy section)
- **In Quebec**: Every corner store
- **Outside Quebec**: Harder to find, order online or substitute mozzarella (not ideal)

**Fresh Test:**
- Should squeak against teeth when bitten
- Room temperature, not refrigerated
- Made within 1-2 days
- Mild flavor, springy texture

## Variations

### Montreal Style
- Add Montreal steak spice to fries
- Darker gravy

### Galvaude (Traditional Quebec)
- Add chicken pieces
- Green peas
- Like chicken pot pie poutine

### Italian Poutine
- Add meat sauce (bolognese)
- Ground beef and Italian sausage

### La Banquise (24-hour Montreal spot)
- 30+ poutine varieties
- Most famous: La Elvis (peppers, mushrooms, bacon)

## Troubleshooting

**Soggy fries:**
- Didn't double fry
- Fries not crispy enough before assembly
- Too much gravy

**Curds don't melt:**
- Gravy not hot enough
- Curds were refrigerated (too cold)
- Need to wait 30 seconds after pouring gravy

**No squeak:**
- Curds not fresh enough
- Wrong cheese used
