# 🍁 Tim Hortons Classic Glazed Donuts (Copycat)

## Overview
The donut that made Tim Hortons a Canadian institution. Light, fluffy yeast donut with a perfectly sweet glaze. Not too heavy, not too sweet - just right. This copycat creates the exact texture and glaze that Canadians crave every morning.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **YEAST TEMPERATURE**: 105-115°F only - hotter kills yeast, cooler won't activate
- **PROOF TWICE**: First rise 1 hour, second rise 30 min (can't skip)
- **OIL TEMP CRITICAL**: 365-375°F - lower = greasy, higher = burnt
- **GLAZE WHILE WARM**: Dip donuts while still warm (glaze sets as they cool)
- **DON'T OVERFRY**: 60-90 seconds per side max or donuts get tough
- **CUTTER SIZE**: 3" cutter with 1" hole (classic donut proportions)

## The Tim Hortons Secret
1. **Yeast-raised** (not cake donuts - lighter texture)
2. **Potato flour** (keeps donuts soft for hours)
3. **Sweetened condensed milk** (in dough - secret moisture)
4. **Vanilla glaze** (simple, not too sweet)
5. **Fried at 365-375°F** (golden outside, fluffy inside)
6. **Always fresh** (made throughout the day)

## Equipment
- Stand mixer with dough hook (or by hand)
- Deep fryer or heavy pot
- Oil thermometer
- 3" round cutter
- 1" round cutter (for hole)
- Wire rack over baking sheet
- Rolling pin
- Clean kitchen towels

## Ingredients (makes 12 donuts + 12 holes)

**For Donuts:**
- 1 cup (240ml) whole milk, warmed to 110°F (43°C)
- 1/4 cup (50g) granulated sugar
- 1 packet (7g) active dry yeast (2 1/4 tsp)
- 2 large eggs, room temperature
- 1/4 cup (56g) butter, melted and cooled
- 2 tbsp (30ml) sweetened condensed milk
- 1 tsp (5ml) vanilla extract
- 3 1/2 cups (420g) all-purpose flour, plus more for dusting
- 2 tbsp (16g) potato flour or cornstarch
- 1 tsp (6g) kosher salt
- Oil for frying (vegetable or canola, about 8 cups/2L)

**For Glaze:**
- 3 cups (360g) powdered sugar
- 1/3 cup (80ml) whole milk
- 1 tsp (5ml) vanilla extract
- Pinch of salt

## Method

### Step 1: Activate Yeast (10 minutes)
1. Warm 1 cup (240ml) milk to 110°F (43°C)
2. Pour into stand mixer bowl
3. Add 1 tsp sugar from the 1/4 cup
4. Sprinkle 1 packet (7g) yeast over milk
5. Stir gently
6. Let sit 5-10 minutes until foamy
7. 🚨 **MUST BE FOAMY** - if not, yeast is dead, start over

### Step 2: Mix Dough
1. To foamy yeast mixture, add:
   - Remaining 1/4 cup (50g) sugar
   - 2 eggs
   - 1/4 cup (56g) melted butter (cooled)
   - 2 tbsp (30ml) sweetened condensed milk
   - 1 tsp (5ml) vanilla
2. Mix on low with paddle attachment until combined

**Add Dry Ingredients:**
1. In separate bowl, whisk together:
   - 3 1/2 cups (420g) flour
   - 2 tbsp (16g) potato flour
   - 1 tsp (6g) salt
2. Add to wet ingredients
3. Mix on low until shaggy dough forms

### Step 3: Knead Dough (8-10 minutes)
1. Switch to dough hook
2. Knead on medium-low speed 8-10 minutes
3. Dough should be:
   - Smooth and elastic
   - Slightly tacky but not sticky
   - Clears sides of bowl
4. Add flour 1 tbsp at a time if too sticky
5. **By Hand**: Knead on floured surface 10-12 min

### Step 4: First Rise (1 hour)
1. Form dough into ball
2. Place in greased bowl
3. Turn to coat with oil
4. Cover with damp towel
5. Let rise in warm place 1 hour
6. Should double in size
7. 🚨 **MUST DOUBLE** - this creates light texture

### Step 5: Roll and Cut Donuts
1. Punch down dough
2. Turn onto floured surface
3. Roll to 1/2" thick
4. Cut with 3" round cutter
5. Cut 1" hole from center with smaller cutter
6. Save holes (these are bonus donut holes!)
7. Place donuts and holes on floured baking sheet
8. Space 2" apart

### Step 6: Second Rise (30-45 minutes)
1. Cover donuts with damp towel
2. Let rise 30-45 minutes
3. Should puff up, nearly double
4. Touch gently - should spring back slowly
5. This rise is critical for fluffy texture
6. 🚨 **DON'T SKIP SECOND RISE**

### Step 7: Heat Oil
1. While donuts rise (last 15 min), heat oil
2. Pour oil into pot (3-4" deep, about 8 cups)
3. Heat to 365-375°F (185-190°C)
4. Use thermometer - temp is critical
5. Maintain temp between batches

### Step 8: Fry Donuts (2-3 minutes each)
1. Oil at 365-375°F
2. Gently slide 2-3 donuts into oil (don't crowd)
3. Fry 60-90 seconds first side
4. Should be golden brown
5. Flip carefully
6. Fry 60-90 seconds second side
7. Remove to wire rack
8. Let oil return to temp
9. Repeat with remaining donuts
10. Fry holes 30-45 seconds per side
11. 🚨 **WATCH TEMP** - adjust heat to maintain 365-375°F

### Step 9: Make Glaze
**While donuts cool slightly (still warm):**
1. In bowl, whisk together:
   - 3 cups (360g) powdered sugar
   - 1/3 cup (80ml) milk
   - 1 tsp (5ml) vanilla
   - Pinch salt
2. Should be thick but pourable
3. If too thick, add milk 1 tsp at a time
4. If too thin, add powdered sugar 2 tbsp at a time

### Step 10: Glaze Donuts
1. While donuts still warm (5-10 min after frying):
2. Dip top of donut in glaze
3. Let excess drip off
4. Place on wire rack glaze-side up
5. Glaze will set as donut cools
6. Glaze donut holes by tossing in bowl
7. 🚨 **GLAZE WHILE WARM** - glaze won't stick to cold donuts

### Step 11: Serve
1. Let glaze set 10-15 minutes
2. Serve at room temperature
3. Best within 4-6 hours of making
4. Store in airtight container up to 2 days

## Perfect Tim Hortons Donut Indicators
- **Texture**: Light, fluffy, airy inside
- **Color**: Golden brown exterior
- **Glaze**: Thin, even coating, slightly crispy when set
- **Weight**: Light (not dense or heavy)
- **Flavor**: Sweet but not cloying, slight vanilla
- **Shape**: Classic donut ring, even thickness

## Timing

**Total Time: 3 hours**
- Activate yeast: 10 min
- Mix dough: 10 min
- Knead: 10 min
- First rise: 1 hour
- Roll and cut: 15 min
- Second rise: 30-45 min
- Fry: 20-30 min (in batches)
- Glaze: 10 min
- **Active time: 75 min**

**Make-Ahead:**
- After first rise: Punch down, refrigerate overnight
- Next morning: Roll, cut, second rise (1 hour cold), fry
- Or freeze cut donuts after second rise
- Fry from frozen (add 30 seconds per side)

## Troubleshooting

**Dense, heavy donuts:**
- Didn't rise long enough (must double each time)
- Water too hot (killed yeast)
- Too much flour added
- Didn't knead enough

**Greasy donuts:**
- Oil temp too low (need 365-375°F)
- Fried too long
- Didn't drain on wire rack

**Burnt outside, raw inside:**
- Oil too hot
- Fried too long on first side
- Donuts rolled too thick

**Glaze won't stick:**
- Donuts too cool (must glaze warm)
- Glaze too thick (add milk)
- Donuts too greasy (didn't drain)

**Tough texture:**
- Overfried (should be 60-90 sec per side)
- Too much flour in dough
- Overmixed after adding flour

## Variations

### Chocolate Glazed
- Add 1/4 cup cocoa powder to glaze
- Reduce powdered sugar to 2 3/4 cups
- Thin with extra milk if needed

### Honey Dip
- Add 2 tbsp honey to glaze
- Reduce milk to 1/4 cup
- Classic Tim Hortons variety

### Maple Dip
- Replace vanilla with 1 tsp maple extract
- Add 2 tbsp maple syrup to glaze
- Most Canadian donut ever

### Old Fashioned Plain
- Use cake donut recipe instead
- Different texture (denser)
- Less sweet

## Storage and Reheating

**Room Temperature:**
- Store in airtight container up to 2 days
- Best day of making
- Glaze may soften in humid weather

**Refrigerator:**
- Not recommended (dries out faster)

**Freezing:**
- Freeze unglazed donuts up to 2 months
- Thaw at room temp
- Glaze after thawing
- Or freeze cut unbaked donuts, fry from frozen

**Reheating:**
- Microwave 10-15 seconds (softens donut)
- Don't overheat (makes tough)
- Can re-glaze if glaze dissolved
