# 🍁 Tim Hortons Timbits - 4 Varieties (Copycat)

## Overview
The iconic bite-sized donuts that define Canadian childhood. Originally made from donut holes, now their own beloved product. This recipe covers the 4 most popular varieties: Chocolate Glazed, Honey Dip, Old Fashioned Plain, and Sour Cream Glazed. Perfect for parties, road trips, or satisfying that Timbits craving.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **SMALL SIZE**: Use 1" scoop or cut small (they puff up)
- **DON'T OVERFRY**: 60-90 seconds total (they're tiny, cook fast)
- **OIL TEMP 365-375°F**: Critical for even cooking
- **GLAZE WHILE WARM**: All varieties need to be glazed hot
- **ROLL IN BATCHES**: Different coatings need different prep

## Ingredients (makes 40 Timbits - 10 of each variety)

**Base Dough (for all varieties):**
- 1 cup (240ml) whole milk, warmed to 110°F
- 1/4 cup (50g) granulated sugar
- 1 packet (7g) active dry yeast
- 2 large eggs
- 1/4 cup (56g) butter, melted
- 1 tsp (5ml) vanilla extract
- 3 1/2 cups (420g) all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp (6g) kosher salt
- Oil for frying (8 cups/2L)

**For Chocolate Glazed (10 Timbits):**
- 1 cup (120g) powdered sugar
- 3 tbsp (18g) cocoa powder
- 3-4 tbsp (45-60ml) milk
- 1/2 tsp vanilla

**For Honey Dip (10 Timbits):**
- 1 cup (120g) powdered sugar
- 2 tbsp (30ml) honey
- 2-3 tbsp (30-45ml) milk
- 1/2 tsp vanilla

**For Old Fashioned Plain (10 Timbits):**
- 1/2 cup (100g) granulated sugar
- 1 tsp (3g) cinnamon
- Use cake donut base instead (see variation)

**For Sour Cream Glazed (10 Timbits):**
- Sour cream dough variation (below)
- 1 cup (120g) powdered sugar
- 3 tbsp (45ml) milk
- 1/2 tsp vanilla

## Method

### Step 1: Make Base Yeast Dough
1. Activate yeast in warm milk with 1 tsp sugar (5-10 min until foamy)
2. Add remaining sugar, eggs, melted butter, vanilla
3. Mix in flour and salt
4. Knead 8-10 minutes until smooth and elastic
5. First rise: 1 hour until doubled
6. 🚨 **SAME AS DONUT DOUGH** - see Classic Glazed Donuts recipe

### Step 2: Shape Timbits
**Using Cookie Scoop:**
1. Punch down risen dough
2. Use 1" cookie scoop to portion dough
3. Roll each portion into smooth ball
4. Place on floured baking sheet, 2" apart
5. Should make about 40 balls

**OR Using Knife:**
1. Roll dough to 1/2" thick
2. Cut into 1" squares
3. Roll each square into ball

### Step 3: Second Rise (30 minutes)
1. Cover with damp towel
2. Let rise 30 minutes
3. Will puff up slightly
4. Don't let over-proof (gets too big)

### Step 4: Heat Oil
1. Heat oil to 365-375°F (185-190°C)
2. Maintain temp between batches
3. Oil should be 3-4" deep

### Step 5: Fry Timbits (60-90 seconds total)
1. Fry 8-10 Timbits at a time (don't crowd)
2. Drop gently into oil
3. Fry 30-45 seconds first side
4. Flip when golden brown
5. Fry 30-45 seconds second side
6. Total time: 60-90 seconds
7. Remove to wire rack
8. Let oil return to temp
9. Repeat with remaining Timbits
10. 🚨 **THEY COOK FAST** - watch closely!

### Step 6: Coat Each Variety

**Chocolate Glazed (10 Timbits):**
1. Make chocolate glaze: whisk powdered sugar, cocoa, milk, vanilla
2. While Timbits warm, dip in chocolate glaze
3. Let excess drip off
4. Place on rack, let set

**Honey Dip (10 Timbits):**
1. Make honey glaze: whisk powdered sugar, honey, milk, vanilla
2. Dip warm Timbits in honey glaze
3. Return to rack

**Old Fashioned Plain (10 Timbits):**
1. Mix sugar and cinnamon in bowl
2. Toss warm Timbits in cinnamon sugar
3. Shake off excess

**Sour Cream Glazed (10 Timbits):**
1. Make vanilla glaze: whisk powdered sugar, milk, vanilla
2. Dip warm Timbits
3. Place on rack

### Step 7: Serve
1. Let glazes set 10 minutes
2. Arrange in mixed box
3. Serve at room temperature
4. Best within 6 hours

## Perfect Timbits Indicators
- **Size**: Bite-sized, about 1.5" diameter after frying
- **Texture**: Light, fluffy, airy

- **Freshness**: Soft, never stale

## Sour Cream Dough Variation

**For Sour Cream Glazed Timbits:**
Replace in base dough:
- Use 1/2 cup (120ml) milk + 1/2 cup (120g) sour cream
- Add 1/4 tsp nutmeg
- Slightly denser, richer texture
- Classic variety

## Old Fashioned Plain (Cake Donut Version)

**Alternative Cake Donut Base:**
- 2 cups (240g) flour
- 1 cup (200g) sugar
- 2 tsp (8g) baking powder
- 1/2 tsp (3g) salt
- 1/2 tsp (1g) nutmeg
- 2 eggs
- 1/2 cup (120ml) milk
- 2 tbsp (28g) melted butter
- Mix wet and dry, scoop, fry same way
- Denser, more crumbly texture

## Troubleshooting

**Timbits too big:**
- Used too much dough per ball
- Use 1" scoop or smaller
- They puff up when fried

**Greasy:**
- Oil temp too low
- Fried too long
- Didn't drain properly

**Dense, not fluffy:**
- Didn't rise long enough
- Overfried
- Too much flour in dough

**Glaze falling off:**
- Timbits too cool when glazed
- Glaze too thin
- Timbits too greasy

## Storage

**Room Temperature:**
- Best within 6 hours
- Store in airtight container up to 2 days
- Will dry out

**Freezing:**
- Freeze unglazed up to 2 months
- Thaw, then glaze
- Or freeze fried and glazed, thaw at room temp
