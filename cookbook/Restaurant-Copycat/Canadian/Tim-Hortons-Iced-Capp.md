# 🍁 Tim Hortons Iced Capp (Copycat)

## Overview
The frozen coffee drink that defines Canadian summers. Creamy, sweet, coffee-flavored slush topped with whipped cream. Started as a summer special, now available year-round due to overwhelming demand. This copycat creates the exact smooth, slushy texture and sweet coffee flavor.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **STRONG COFFEE REQUIRED**: Brew double-strength or use espresso
- **MUST BE COLD**: Coffee must be completely chilled before blending
- **ICE RATIO CRITICAL**: Too much ice = watery, too little = not slushy
- **BLEND UNTIL SMOOTH**: No ice chunks (high-speed blender ideal)
- **SERVE IMMEDIATELY**: Melts quickly, separates if sits

## The Tim Hortons Secret
1. **Coffee base** (strong brewed coffee, not instant)
2. **Cream** (18% cream in Canada - half-and-half in US)
3. **Simple syrup** (pre-sweetened - blends smooth)
4. **Ice** (lots of it - creates slush)
5. **Whipped cream topping** (signature finish)
6. **Chocolate syrup drizzle** (optional but classic)

## Equipment
- Blender (high-speed ideal)
- Coffee maker or espresso machine
- Large glass or cup
- Straw

## Ingredients (makes 2 large Iced Capps)

**For Iced Capp:**
- 1 cup (240ml) strong brewed coffee, chilled completely
- 1/2 cup (120ml) half-and-half (or 18% cream)
- 3 tbsp (45ml) simple syrup (or 2 tbsp sugar)
- 3 cups (450g) ice cubes
- 1/2 tsp (2.5ml) vanilla extract (optional)

**For Topping:**
- Whipped cream
- Chocolate syrup (optional)

**For Simple Syrup** (make ahead):
- 1/2 cup (100g) sugar
- 1/2 cup (120ml) water
- Heat until dissolved, cool

## Method

### Step 1: Brew and Chill Coffee (Can do ahead)
1. Brew strong coffee (double strength)
2. OR brew 2 shots espresso, dilute with 1/2 cup water
3. Let cool to room temp
4. Refrigerate until completely cold (2+ hours)
5. OR quick chill: pour over ice, strain out ice
6. 🚨 **MUST BE COLD** - warm coffee melts ice too fast

### Step 2: Blend
1. Add to blender:
   - 1 cup (240ml) cold coffee
   - 1/2 cup (120ml) half-and-half
   - 3 tbsp (45ml) simple syrup
   - 3 cups (450g) ice
   - 1/2 tsp vanilla (if using)
2. Blend on high 30-60 seconds
3. Should be smooth, slushy consistency
4. No ice chunks should remain
5. Should be thick, pourable

### Step 3: Serve Immediately
1. Pour into large glass
2. Top with whipped cream
3. Drizzle with chocolate syrup (optional)
4. Add straw
5. Serve immediately
6. 🚨 **DRINK FAST** - separates and melts quickly

## Perfect Iced Capp Indicators
- **Texture**: Smooth slush, no ice chunks
- **Color**: Light brown, creamy
- **Consistency**: Thick, pourable, slushy
- **Taste**: Sweet coffee, creamy
- **Temperature**: Frozen, refreshing
- **Topping**: Whipped cream stays on top

## Variations

### Mocha Iced Capp
- Add 2 tbsp chocolate syrup to blender
- Extra chocolate drizzle on top

### Vanilla Iced Capp
- Increase vanilla to 1 tsp
- Add 1 tbsp vanilla syrup

### Caramel Iced Capp
- Add 2 tbsp caramel sauce to blender
- Drizzle caramel on top

### Chocolate Milk Base (Popular Canadian Version)
- Replace half-and-half with chocolate milk
- Reduce simple syrup to 2 tbsp
- Chocolate-coffee combo

## Troubleshooting

**Too watery:**
- Coffee wasn't cold enough
- Too much coffee, not enough ice
- Over-blended (ice melted)

**Too icy/chunky:**
- Not blended long enough
- Blender not powerful enough
- Ice cubes too large

**Not sweet enough:**
- Add more simple syrup (1 tbsp at a time)
- Use sweetened whipped cream

**Separates quickly:**
- Normal (ice melts)
- Drink immediately
- Stir before drinking
