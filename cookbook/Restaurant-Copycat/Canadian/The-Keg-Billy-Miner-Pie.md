# 🍁 The Keg Billy Miner Pie (Copycat)

## Overview
The dessert that made The Keg steakhouse famous for more than steaks. Coffee ice cream pie with Oreo crust, hot fudge, caramel sauce, and crushed peanuts. Named after a Canadian train robber. Rich, decadent, massive portion. One of Canada's most famous restaurant desserts.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **FREEZE SOLID**: Must freeze 4+ hours (overnight better)
- **HOT FUDGE HOT**: Contrast with frozen pie is key
- **OREO CRUST**: Crushed fine, butter binds
- **WHIPPED CREAM FRESH**: Not Cool Whip
- **SLICE FROZEN**: Let sit 5 min before eating

## Ingredients (serves 8-10)

**Oreo Crust:**
- 24 Oreo cookies, crushed fine
- 1/4 cup butter, melted

**Filling:**
- 1.5 quarts (1.4L) coffee ice cream, slightly softened

**Toppings:**
- 1 cup hot fudge sauce, warmed
- 1/2 cup caramel sauce, warmed
- 1 cup salted peanuts, roughly chopped
- Whipped cream

## Method

**Crust:**
1. Crush Oreos fine (food processor)
2. Mix with melted butter
3. Press into 9" pie plate
4. Freeze 15 min

**Assemble:**
1. Spread softened coffee ice cream into crust
2. Smooth top
3. Freeze solid (4+ hours, overnight ideal)

**Serve (The Keg Way):**
1. Slice frozen pie
2. Place slice on plate
3. Drizzle hot fudge over top
4. Drizzle caramel sauce
5. Sprinkle crushed peanuts
6. Top with whipped cream
7. Serve immediately

## Perfect Billy Miner Indicators:
- Pie frozen solid
- Sauces hot (creates melty effect)
- Peanuts crunchy
- Coffee ice cream flavor pronounced
- Massive portion (restaurant serves huge slices)

- Named after famous Canadian outlaw Billy Miner
- Signature Keg dessert since 1970s
- Massive portion (shareable but people don't)
- Coffee ice cream unexpected (most use vanilla)
