# 🍁 The Keg Garlic Cheese Bread (Copycat)

## Overview
The appetizer that made The Keg famous. Thick slices of bread loaded with garlic butter, covered in melted cheese, served bubbling hot. People go to The Keg just for this bread. Rich, garlicky, cheesy perfection that's impossible to stop eating.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **FRESH GARLIC MANDATORY**: Not powdered - flavor is everything
- **BUTTER MUST SOAK IN**: Let sit 10-15 minutes before baking
- **BROIL FOR FINISH**: Creates golden bubbly top
- **SERVE IMMEDIATELY**: Gets soggy if sits
- **USE GOOD BREAD**: Sourdough or French loaf (substantial bread needed)

## The Keg Secret
1. **Fresh garlic butter** (tons of fresh garlic)
2. **Three cheese blend** (mozzarella, cheddar, Parmesan)
3. **Thick bread slices** (1" thick - can soak up butter)
4. **Butter soaks through** (bread should be saturated)
5. **Broiled to finish** (golden bubbly cheese)
6. **Served in cast iron** (stays hot)

## Equipment
- Cast iron skillet or oven-safe dish
- Small saucepan
- Garlic press or microplane
- Pastry brush

## Ingredients (serves 4-6)

**For Garlic Butter:**
- 1/2 cup (113g) butter, melted
- 6 cloves garlic, minced fine (or pressed)
- 2 tbsp (8g) fresh parsley, minced
- 1/4 tsp (1.5g) kosher salt
- 1/4 tsp (0.5g) black pepper

**For Bread:**
- 1 sourdough or French loaf
- Cut into 1" thick slices (8-10 slices)

**For Cheese Topping:**
- 1 cup (113g) shredded mozzarella
- 1/2 cup (57g) shredded cheddar
- 1/4 cup (25g) grated Parmesan

## Method

### Step 1: Make Garlic Butter
1. Melt butter in small saucepan
2. Add minced garlic, parsley, salt, pepper
3. Simmer 1-2 minutes (garlic infuses butter)
4. Remove from heat

### Step 2: Prep Bread
1. Slice bread into 1" thick slices
2. Arrange in cast iron skillet or oven-safe dish
3. Slices should be upright, touching
4. Like a bread loaf standing up

### Step 3: Soak with Garlic Butter
1. Brush garlic butter generously on each slice
2. Get butter in all crevices
3. Pour remaining butter over top
4. Let sit 10-15 minutes (butter soaks in)

### Step 4: Add Cheese
1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C)
2. Mix mozzarella, cheddar, Parmesan
3. Sprinkle generously over bread
4. Cover all slices

### Step 5: Bake and Broil
1. Bake 375°F for 15-20 minutes
2. Cheese should melt completely
3. Switch to broil (high)
4. Broil 2-3 minutes
5. Watch closely - should turn golden and bubbly
6. Remove when cheese bubbling and slightly browned

### Step 6: Serve
1. Serve in cast iron (stays hot)
2. Pull apart slices
3. Serve with extra garlic butter for dipping
4. Eat immediately

## Perfect Keg Garlic Bread Indicators
- **Bread**: Saturated with garlic butter, soft inside
- **Cheese**: Golden, bubbly, melted throughout
- **Garlic**: Prominent but not raw
- **Texture**: Soft bread, gooey cheese
- **Temperature**: Piping hot
- **Presentation**: Served in hot skillet

- Prep: 10 min

## Variations

### Extra Garlic
- Double the garlic (8-10 cloves)
- For serious garlic lovers

### Herb-Crusted
- Add rosemary and thyme to butter
- Italian herb blend

### Four Cheese
- Add fontina or Gruyère
- Extra rich

## Troubleshooting

**Soggy bread:**
- Too much butter (use less next time)
- Bread too thin (need 1" slices)
- Didn't drain butter before baking

**Cheese not melted:**
- Oven not hot enough
- Didn't bake long enough
- Cheese too thick
