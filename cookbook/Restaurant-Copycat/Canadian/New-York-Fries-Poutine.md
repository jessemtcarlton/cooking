# 🍁 New York Fries Poutine (Copycat)

## Overview
The mall food court poutine that introduced millions to Quebec's greatest creation. Despite the name, it's 100% Canadian (started in Ontario 1984). Fresh-cut fries, hot gravy, cheese curds. The gateway poutine for most Canadians. No actual connection to New York - just sounded cool in the '80s.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **FRESH-CUT DAILY**: Hand-cut, skin-on fries (NYF signature)
- **REAL CHEESE CURDS**: Fresh, squeaky (not shredded cheese)
- **VEGETARIAN GRAVY**: NYF uses veggie gravy (different from beef)
- **CUT THICK**: 1/2" fries (NYF style - thick and hearty)
- **SERVE HOT**: Gravy must be piping hot

## The New York Fries Secret
- **Fresh-cut in store** (every location cuts daily)
- **Skin-on** (nutritious, adds texture)
- **Vegetarian gravy** (mushroom-based, not beef)
- **Real cheese curds** (sourced from Quebec)
- **Premium toppings** (bacon, green onions available)
- **Trans-fat free** (NYF was early adopter)

## Ingredients (serves 2-3)

**Fries:**
- 4 large russet potatoes (2 lbs/900g)
- Oil for frying
- Salt

**Vegetarian Gravy:**
- 3 cups (720ml) vegetable stock
- 1/2 cup (50g) mushrooms, finely chopped
- 3 tbsp (45g) butter
- 3 tbsp (24g) flour
- 2 tbsp (30ml) soy sauce
- 1 tbsp (9g) onion powder
- 1 tsp (3g) garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp dried thyme
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- Salt to taste

**Assembly:**
- 2 cups (300g) fresh cheese curds

## Method

### Fries (NYF Fresh-Cut Style):
1. Wash potatoes (leave skin on)
2. Cut into 1/2" thick fries
3. Soak in cold water 30 min
4. Dry completely
5. Fry at 350°F for 5-6 min (until golden)
6. Drain on paper towels
7. Salt generously

**Note**: NYF does single fry (not double) - simpler method, still crispy

### Vegetarian Gravy:
1. Sauté mushrooms in butter until soft
2. Add flour, cook 2 min (roux)
3. Gradually whisk in vegetable stock
4. Add soy sauce, onion powder, garlic, thyme, pepper
5. Simmer 10-12 min, stirring (should thicken)
6. Season with salt
7. Strain for smooth texture (optional - NYF gravy is smooth)
8. Keep hot

### Assemble:
1. Fill container with hot fries
2. Add cheese curds
3. Pour hot gravy over top
4. Serve immediately

## Perfect NYF Poutine Indicators
- **Fries**: Golden, crispy, skin-on visible
- **Curds**: Fresh, squeaky, melting slightly
- **Gravy**: Hot, smooth, rich (not too thick)
- **Portion**: Generous (mall food court sized)
- **Vegetarian**: Gravy has no meat

## Pro Tips
**The NYF Story:**
- Founded Ontario 1984 (Jay Gould, Hal Gould)
- Name chosen because "New York" sounded trendy
- 100% Canadian despite name
- 120+ locations (mostly mall food courts)
- Switched to trans-fat free oil early (2004)
- Vegetarian gravy option from start (inclusive)

**The Mall Food Court Classic:**
- Peak time: Friday night, Saturday shopping
- Signature smell fills food court
- Often first poutine for non-Quebecers
- Made poutine mainstream outside Quebec
- Kids' introduction to poutine

**NYF Menu Variations:**
- **Works Poutine**: Add bacon, green onions, sour cream
- **Butter Chicken Poutine**: Butter chicken sauce instead of gravy
- **Veggie Works**: Mushrooms, peppers, onions

**Why Vegetarian Gravy?**
- Inclusive (vegetarians can enjoy)
- Food court efficiency (one gravy for everyone)
- Actually delicious (mushroom umami)
- Different from Quebec-style beef gravy
- Made NYF unique

**Fresh-Cut Advantage:**
- Potatoes cut in-store daily
- Visible in open kitchens (customers see cutting)
- Irregular cuts (more texture, crispier bits)
- Fresher taste than frozen fries

The poutine that brought Quebec's treasure to every mall in Canada! Simple, delicious, nostalgic.
