# 🍁 A&W Canada Teen Burger (Copycat)

## Overview
The signature burger that's unique to A&W Canada (not available in US). Double patties, bacon, cheese, lettuce, tomato, pickles, onions, and the secret Teen Sauce. Different from US A&W - Canadian version is independently owned since 1972. This copycat creates the exact sweet-tangy sauce and proper assembly.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **TEEN SAUCE IS THE SECRET**: Sweet, tangy, special sauce (like Thousand Island but different)
- **TWO THIN PATTIES**: Not one thick patty (cooks fast, more crust)
- **FRESH GROUND BEEF**: 80/20 blend (need fat for flavor)
- **TOAST THE BUN**: On griddle in burger fat
- **ASSEMBLE HOT**: All components hot when assembled
- **PRESS PATTIES THIN**: 1/4" thick max (like smash burger)

## The A&W Canada Secret
1. **Teen Sauce** (sweet pickle relish, mayo, ketchup base)
2. **Two 2oz patties** (thin, crispy edges)
3. **Bacon** (crispy, 2-3 strips)
4. **Processed cheese** (American cheese, melts perfect)
5. **Fresh vegetables** (lettuce, tomato, onion, pickles)
6. **Sesame seed bun** (toasted)
7. **Specific assembly order** (sauce first, then toppings)

## Equipment
- Griddle or large cast iron skillet
- Spatula
- Small bowl (for sauce)

## Ingredients (makes 2 Teen Burgers)

**For Teen Sauce** (make ahead):
- 1/2 cup (120ml) mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp (30ml) ketchup
- 2 tbsp (30ml) sweet pickle relish
- 1 tsp (5ml) white vinegar
- 1/2 tsp (2.5g) sugar
- 1/4 tsp (0.75g) garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp (0.75g) onion powder
- Pinch of paprika

**For Burgers:**
- 8 oz (225g) ground beef (80/20)
- 4-6 strips bacon
- 4 slices American cheese
- 2 sesame seed buns
- Iceberg lettuce (2 leaves)
- 1 tomato, sliced
- 1/4 white onion, sliced thin
- Dill pickle slices
- Salt and pepper

## Method

### Step 1: Make Teen Sauce (Can do days ahead)
1. Mix all sauce ingredients in bowl
2. Whisk until smooth
3. Refrigerate 30 min (flavors meld)
4. Keeps 1 week refrigerated

### Step 2: Cook Bacon
1. Cook bacon crispy (oven or pan)
2. Drain on paper towels
3. Break slices in half (easier to fit on burger)
4. Keep warm

### Step 3: Form Patties
1. Divide beef into 4 portions (2 oz each)
2. Form into loose balls
3. Don't overwork meat
4. Season both sides with salt and pepper
5. Keep refrigerated until cooking

### Step 4: Cook Burgers
1. Heat griddle or skillet to medium-high
2. Place burger balls on griddle
3. Immediately smash flat with spatula
4. Press to 1/4" thick (thin!)
5. Cook 2-3 minutes first side
6. Should have crispy brown crust
7. Flip, add cheese slice to each patty
8. Cook 2 minutes second side
9. Cheese melts while cooking
10. Remove to plate

### Step 5: Toast Buns
1. While patties rest, split buns
2. Place cut-side down on griddle
3. Toast in burger fat 30-60 seconds
4. Should be golden brown

### Step 6: Assemble (A&W ORDER)
**Bottom Bun:**
1. Spread Teen Sauce generously
2. Add pickles
3. Add onion slices
4. Add first cheese-topped patty
5. Add 2-3 half-strips bacon
6. Add second cheese-topped patty
7. Add tomato slice
8. Add lettuce

**Top Bun:**
1. Spread Teen Sauce
2. Place on top
3. Press down gently
4. Cut in half if desired

### Step 7: Serve
1. Wrap in paper if desired (A&W style)
2. Serve immediately
3. With fries and root beer (classic pairing)

## Perfect Teen Burger Indicators
- **Patties**: Thin, crispy edges, juicy
- **Cheese**: Melted, covering patties
- **Bacon**: Crispy, not chewy
- **Sauce**: Generous, tangy-sweet
- **Bun**: Toasted, holds together
- **Assembly**: Layers visible when cut

## Variations

### Mama Burger (Single Patty)
- Use one 3oz patty instead of two
- Same toppings
- Smaller, less filling

### Papa Burger (Triple Patty)
- Three 2oz patties
- Extra cheese and bacon
- For serious appetites

### Teen Burger Poutine Combo
- Serve with poutine instead of fries
- Very Canadian
