# 🍁 A&W Canada Root Beer Float (Copycat)

## Overview
The classic ice cream float made with A&W's famous root beer. Creamy vanilla soft-serve meets bubbly, creamy root beer. A&W's root beer is made fresh in-store daily - that's what makes it special. This is nostalgia in a glass.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **COLD MUG**: Freeze mug 30+ min (essential)
- **ICE CREAM FIRST**: Then pour root beer slowly
- **POUR DOWN SIDE**: Prevents overflow foam
- **QUALITY ROOT BEER**: A&W brand or craft root beer
- **SERVE IMMEDIATELY**: Melts quickly

## Ingredients (per float)

- 1 frosty mug (freeze 30+ min)
- 2-3 scoops vanilla ice cream (or soft-serve)
- 12 oz A&W Root Beer (or quality craft root beer)
- Optional: whipped cream, cherry

## Method
1. Freeze mug until frosty (30+ min)
2. Add 2-3 scoops vanilla ice cream
3. Slowly pour root beer down side of mug
4. Pour gradually to prevent overflow
5. Top with whipped cream and cherry (optional)
6. Serve with straw and long spoon
7. Drink/eat immediately

## The A&W Difference:
- Root beer made fresh daily in-store
- Real cane sugar (not corn syrup)
- Proprietary recipe since 1919
- Served in frosted glass mug
- Soft-serve ice cream (not hard-packed)

## Perfect Float Indicators:
- Foamy head on top
- Root beer creamy (mixed with melted ice cream)
- Cold, frosty mug
- Vanilla and root beer balanced
