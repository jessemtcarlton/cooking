# 🍁 Boston Pizza Cactus Cut Potatoes (Copycat)

## Overview
The addictive spiral-cut fries that made Boston Pizza famous beyond pizza. Perfectly seasoned, crispy, served with signature BP dipping sauce. Spiral shape = maximum crispy surface area. A Canadian chain staple appetizer since the '90s. Warning: one order is never enough.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **SPIRAL CUTTER NEEDED**: Special tool creates the signature shape (or buy pre-cut)
- **SEASONING WHILE HOT**: Must toss immediately after frying
- **CRISPY = SUCCESS**: Spiral shape should be crunchy throughout
- **BP SAUCE ESSENTIAL**: Ranch-based dipping sauce is mandatory
- **DON'T CROWD FRYER**: Fry in batches (overcrowding = soggy)

## The Boston Pizza Secret
- **Spiral cut** (more surface area = crispier)
- **Seasoning blend** (garlic, onion, paprika, salt)
- **Consistent size** (uniform cooking)
- **BP dipping sauce** (creamy ranch-based)
- **Served hot** (in basket, stacked high)

## Ingredients (serves 3-4 as appetizer)

**Cactus Cut Potatoes:**
- 4 large russet potatoes
- Oil for frying (peanut or vegetable)
- 2 tsp (6g) seasoning salt
- 1 tsp (3g) garlic powder
- 1 tsp (3g) paprika
- 1/2 tsp (1.5g) onion powder
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
- Salt

**BP Dipping Sauce:**
- 1/2 cup (120ml) ranch dressing
- 2 tbsp (30ml) mayonnaise
- 1 tbsp (15ml) sour cream
- 1 tsp (5ml) hot sauce
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp dried dill

## Method

### Prepare Potatoes:
1. **Option A - Spiral Cutter**:
   - Use handheld spiral cutter or mandoline with spiral blade
   - Insert potato, twist to create continuous spiral
   - Cut into 4-5" sections

2. **Option B - Buy Pre-Cut**:
   - Look for "tornado potatoes" or "spiral fries" (frozen section)
   - McCain makes them in Canada

### Fry:
1. Heat oil to 350°F
2. Fry spirals in batches (don't crowd)
3. Cook 4-5 min until golden and crispy
4. Drain on paper towels

### Season:
1. Mix seasoning blend (garlic powder, paprika, onion powder, cayenne, seasoning salt)
2. Toss hot fries in seasoning immediately
3. Add extra salt if needed

### Make BP Sauce:
1. Mix all sauce ingredients
2. Refrigerate 30 min (flavors blend)
3. Serve cold

### Serve:
1. Pile cactus cuts in basket or on plate
2. Serve with BP sauce on side
3. Eat immediately while hot

## Perfect Cactus Cut Indicators
- **Shape**: Spiral visible, uniform cuts
- **Texture**: Crispy throughout, not limp
- **Seasoning**: Well-coated, flavorful
- **Color**: Golden brown, not pale
- **Sauce**: Creamy, tangy, cool contrast

## Pro Tips
**The Boston Pizza Story:**
- Founded Edmonton 1964 (Gus Agioritis)
- Started as "Boston Pizza and Spaghetti House"
- Named "Boston" because it sounded sophisticated
- 100% Canadian despite American-sounding name
- 380+ locations (Canada's #1 casual dining chain)
- Cactus Cuts introduced 1990s (instant hit)

**Why "Cactus Cut"?**
- Marketing name (sounds cooler than "spiral fries")
- Resembles cactus arms branching out
- Trademarked by Boston Pizza
- Became signature appetizer

**Spiral Cutting at Home:**
**Tools Available:**
- Handheld spiral cutter ($10-15)
- Mandoline with spiral blade
- Electric spiralizer
- Or buy frozen "tornado potatoes"

**If You Can't Spiral Cut:**
- Make thick waffle fries instead
- Use same seasoning blend
- Still delicious, just different shape

**Boston Pizza Menu Classics:**
- Cactus Cuts (this recipe)
- Boston's Starter (sampler platter)
- Spicy Perogy Pizza (Canadian fusion)
- The Meateor (loaded meat pizza)

**Serving Like BP:**
- Serve in red plastic basket lined with paper
- Pile high (generous portion)
- Sauce in small bowl on side
- Hot, fresh from fryer

**Seasoning Variations:**
- **Cajun Style**: Add more cayenne, black pepper
- **Garlic Parmesan**: Parmesan + garlic powder + parsley
- **BBQ**: Toss with BBQ seasoning instead
- **Buffalo**: Toss with buffalo sauce + ranch
