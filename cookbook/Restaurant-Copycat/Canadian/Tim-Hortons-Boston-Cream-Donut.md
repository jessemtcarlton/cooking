# 🍁 Tim Hortons Boston Cream Donut (Copycat)

## Overview
Fluffy yeast donut filled with vanilla custard, topped with chocolate glaze. Tim Hortons' version of the classic Boston cream. Light, creamy filling with rich chocolate topping. One of the most popular filled donuts.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **FILL AFTER FRYING**: Never before (custard burns)
- **COOL BEFORE FILLING**: Warm donuts melt custard
- **CHOCOLATE GLAZE THIN**: Should drip down sides
- **FILL GENEROUSLY**: Custard should be substantial

## Ingredients (makes 12)

**Donuts**: Use Classic Glazed recipe, don't cut holes
**Custard Filling:**
- 2 cups milk
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1/4 cup cornstarch
- 4 egg yolks
- 2 tbsp butter
- 2 tsp vanilla

**Chocolate Topping:**
- 1 1/2 cups powdered sugar
- 1/4 cup cocoa
- 3-4 tbsp milk
- 1 tsp vanilla

## Method
1. Make donuts (whole rounds, no holes)
2. Fry at 365°F, 90 seconds per side
3. Cool completely

**Make Custard:**
1. Whisk milk, sugar, cornstarch, yolks
2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly
3. Thicken 5-7 min
4. Remove, add butter and vanilla
5. Cool with plastic wrap on surface

**Fill & Top:**
1. Poke hole in side of each donut
2. Pipe custard into center (generous amount)
3. Make chocolate glaze
4. Dip tops in chocolate
5. Let set
