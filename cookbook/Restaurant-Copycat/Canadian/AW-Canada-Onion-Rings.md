# 🍁 A&W Canada Onion Rings (Copycat)

## Overview
Thick-cut onion rings with crispy battered coating. A&W Canada's are famous for being substantial - thick onion slices with crunchy batter. Served hot and fresh, perfect with burgers or on their own.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **THICK ONION SLICES**: 1/2" thick (thinner fall apart)
- **SEPARATE RINGS GENTLY**: Don't break onions
- **DOUBLE DREDGE**: Flour → batter → flour (extra crispy)
- **OIL TEMP 375°F**: Prevents greasiness
- **FRY IN BATCHES**: Don't crowd

## Ingredients (serves 4)

**Onions:**
- 2 large white or sweet onions
- Cut into 1/2" thick rings
- Separate into individual rings

**Batter:**
- 1 1/2 cups flour (divided)
- 1 cup buttermilk
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- Oil for frying

## Method
1. Slice onions 1/2" thick, separate rings
2. Setup: Bowl 1 (1/2 cup flour), Bowl 2 (batter: mix buttermilk, egg, 1 cup flour, baking powder, spices), Bowl 3 (dry flour for final coat)
3. Dredge each ring: flour → batter → flour
4. Rest on rack 10 min (coating sets)
5. Heat oil to 375°F
6. Fry 2-3 minutes until golden
7. Drain on wire rack
8. Season with salt immediately
9. Serve hot
