# 🍁 Swiss Chalet Chalet Sauce (Copycat)

## Overview
The legendary dipping sauce that made Swiss Chalet famous. Savory, slightly tangy, chicken-based gravy that Canadians dip everything in. The most requested Canadian restaurant recipe ever. Sweet, savory, with hints of herbs and a unique flavor that's impossible to describe but instantly recognizable.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **CHICKEN BROTH BASE**: Must use good quality chicken broth (not water)
- **ONION POWDER, NOT FRESH**: Texture must be smooth (fresh onions won't work)
- **WHISK CONSTANTLY**: Prevents lumps when adding flour
- **LOW SIMMER ONLY**: High heat breaks the emulsion
- **SEASON AT END**: Taste and adjust (every broth has different salt levels)
- **STRAIN IF LUMPY**: Must be silky smooth

## Equipment
- Medium saucepan
- Whisk
- Measuring cups and spoons
- Fine mesh strainer (if needed)

## Ingredients (makes 2 cups - enough for 6-8 servings)

**For Chalet Sauce:**
- 2 cups (480ml) good quality chicken broth
- 2 tbsp (28g) butter
- 2 tbsp (16g) all-purpose flour
- 1 tbsp (9g) onion powder
- 1 tsp (3g) garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp (1g) dried oregano
- 1/4 tsp (0.5g) dried sage
- 1/4 tsp (0.5g) dried thyme
- 1/4 tsp (0.5g) paprika
- 1 tsp (4g) granulated sugar
- 1/2 tsp (3g) kosher salt (adjust to taste)
- 1/4 tsp (0.5g) black pepper
- 1 tbsp (15ml) white vinegar
- Dash of Worcestershire sauce (optional)

## Method

### Step 1: Make Roux
1. In medium saucepan over medium heat:
2. Melt 2 tbsp (28g) butter
3. Add 2 tbsp (16g) flour
4. Whisk constantly 1-2 minutes
5. Should smell nutty, turn blonde
6. Don't let brown (will darken sauce too much)

### Step 2: Add Spices to Roux
1. To roux, add:
   - 1 tbsp onion powder
   - 1 tsp garlic powder
   - 1/2 tsp oregano
   - 1/4 tsp sage
   - 1/4 tsp thyme
   - 1/4 tsp paprika
2. Whisk together 30 seconds
3. Spices will bloom in fat

### Step 3: Add Broth Gradually
1. Slowly pour in 2 cups chicken broth
2. Whisk constantly to prevent lumps
3. Start with 1/2 cup, whisk until smooth
4. Add remaining broth gradually
5. Continue whisking

### Step 4: Season and Simmer
1. Add:
   - 1 tsp sugar
   - 1/2 tsp salt
   - 1/4 tsp black pepper
   - 1 tbsp white vinegar
   - Dash Worcestershire (if using)
2. Bring to gentle simmer
3. Reduce heat to low
4. Simmer 5-10 minutes, whisking occasionally
5. Should thicken to gravy consistency
6. Coats back of spoon

### Step 5: Taste and Adjust
1. Taste sauce
2. Adjust:
   - More salt if needed
   - More vinegar for tang
   - More sugar for sweetness
   - More onion powder for depth
3. Should be savory, slightly sweet, tangy
4. If lumpy, strain through fine mesh strainer

### Step 6: Serve Warm
1. Serve immediately while hot
2. Pour over rotisserie chicken
3. Or serve in dipping cup
4. Reheat gently if needed

## Variations

### Extra Tangy
- Increase vinegar to 1 1/2 tbsp
- Add squeeze of lemon

### Herb-Forward
- Double the herbs
- Add 1/4 tsp rosemary

### Richer Version
- Add 2 tbsp heavy cream at end
- Stir in 1 tbsp butter after simmering

### Gluten-Free
- Replace flour with cornstarch
- Use 1 1/2 tbsp cornstarch
- Mix with cold broth before adding

## Troubleshooting

**Lumpy sauce:**
- Didn't whisk constantly
- Added broth too fast
- Strain through fine mesh strainer
- Whisk vigorously

**Too thin:**
- Didn't simmer long enough
- Not enough flour
- Simmer longer to reduce
- Or mix 1 tbsp flour with cold broth, whisk in

**Too thick:**
- Too much flour
- Simmered too long
- Thin with chicken broth

**Too salty:**
- Broth was already salty
- Reduce added salt next time
- Add water to dilute

**Not enough flavor:**
- Weak chicken broth
- Not enough onion powder
- Taste and add more spices

## Storage

**Refrigerator:**
- Store in airtight container up to 5 days
- Reheat gently on stovetop
- Whisk smooth, may need to thin with broth

**Freezer:**
- Freeze up to 3 months
- Thaw in fridge overnight
- Reheat gently, whisk smooth
