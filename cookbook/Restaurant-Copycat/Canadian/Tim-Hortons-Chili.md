# 🍁 Tim Hortons Chili (Copycat)

## Overview
Surprisingly good fast-food chili. Bean and beef based, well-seasoned, hearty. A Tim Hortons staple for lunch - pairs perfectly with donuts for breakfast! Rich, tomato-based, with chunks of beef and beans.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **SIMMER LOW AND SLOW**: 30+ minutes (flavors develop)
- **DRAIN BEEF**: Remove excess grease
- **SEASON IN LAYERS**: Add spices gradually
- **THICK CONSISTENCY**: Should be spoonable, not soupy

## Ingredients (serves 6-8)

- 1 lb (450g) ground beef
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 can (28oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1 can (14oz) kidney beans, drained
- 1 can (14oz) pinto beans, drained
- 1 cup beef broth
- 2 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tbsp cumin
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp oregano
- 1/2 tsp cayenne
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Method
1. Brown ground beef in large pot
2. Drain excess fat
3. Add onion and garlic, cook 5 min
4. Add all spices, toast 1 min
5. Add tomatoes, beans, broth
6. Simmer 30-45 minutes, stirring occasionally
7. Add brown sugar last 10 min
8. Season with salt and pepper
9. Serve with crackers or bread

## Toppings (Tim Hortons Style):
- Shredded cheddar cheese
- Sour cream
- Green onions
- Crackers on side
