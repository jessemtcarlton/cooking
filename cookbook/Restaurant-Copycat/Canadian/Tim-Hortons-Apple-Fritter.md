# 🍁 Tim Hortons Apple Fritter (Copycat)

## Overview
Chunks of fresh apple in sweet yeast dough, fried golden and glazed. More apple than donut - that's what makes it special. Crispy outside, soft inside, with cinnamon-sugar apple pieces throughout. The most substantial donut in the lineup.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **DICE APPLES SMALL**: 1/4" cubes (cook evenly)
- **PAT APPLES DRY**: Excess moisture makes dough soggy
- **IRREGULAR SHAPE**: Hand-formed (not uniform like other donuts)
- **LONGER FRY TIME**: 3-4 min per side (thicker than regular donuts)
- **GLAZE WHILE WARM**: Absorbs better

## Ingredients (makes 8 fritters)

**Dough:**
- 1 cup milk, warm
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1 packet yeast
- 2 eggs
- 1/4 cup butter, melted
- 3 1/2 cups flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 cups diced apples (Granny Smith)
- 2 tbsp sugar + 1 tsp cinnamon (for apples)

**Glaze:**
- 2 cups powdered sugar
- 1/4 cup milk
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon

## Method
1. Make yeast dough (same as glazed donuts)
2. Toss diced apples with cinnamon-sugar
3. Pat apples dry with paper towels
4. Fold apples into risen dough
5. Divide into 8 portions, shape irregularly (rustic look)
6. Second rise 30 min
7. Fry 350°F, 3-4 minutes per side
8. Glaze while warm
