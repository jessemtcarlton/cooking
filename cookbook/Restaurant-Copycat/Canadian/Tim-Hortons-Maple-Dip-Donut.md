# 🍁 Tim Hortons Maple Dip Donut (Copycat)

## Overview
The most Canadian donut possible. Classic yeast donut topped with sweet maple glaze. Every Canadian's favorite - the perfect combination of maple syrup flavor and fluffy donut. This is Tim Hortons' ode to Canadian identity.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **REAL MAPLE EXTRACT**: Not maple-flavored syrup (different taste)
- **PURE MAPLE SYRUP OPTIONAL**: Adds authentic flavor (but pricey)
- **GLAZE WHILE WARM**: Maple glaze sets faster than regular
- **DOUBLE RISE**: Both rises mandatory for light texture
- **OIL TEMP 365-375°F**: Too low = greasy, too high = burnt

## Ingredients (makes 12 donuts)

**Donut Dough** (same as Classic Glazed):
- Follow Tim Hortons Classic Glazed Donuts recipe
- Make dough, first rise, cut, second rise, fry

**Maple Glaze:**
- 2 cups (240g) powdered sugar
- 3 tbsp (45ml) whole milk
- 2 tbsp (30ml) pure maple syrup
- 1 tsp (5ml) maple extract
- Pinch of salt

## Method

### Make Donuts:
1. Follow Classic Glazed Donuts recipe through frying
2. Fry at 365-375°F, 60-90 seconds per side
3. Drain on wire rack

### Maple Glaze:
1. Whisk powdered sugar, milk, maple syrup, maple extract, salt
2. Should be thick but pourable
3. Add milk if too thick (1 tsp at a time)
4. Glaze should coat spoon

### Glaze Donuts:
1. While donuts still warm:
2. Dip top half in maple glaze
3. Let excess drip off
4. Place on rack glaze-side up
5. Glaze sets in 10-15 minutes

## Perfect Maple Dip Indicators
- **Glaze**: Maple-colored (light brown), smooth, sets crispy
- **Flavor**: Sweet maple (not fake), balanced
- **Texture**: Fluffy donut, crispy glaze coating
- **Appearance**: Even glaze coverage, glossy when fresh
