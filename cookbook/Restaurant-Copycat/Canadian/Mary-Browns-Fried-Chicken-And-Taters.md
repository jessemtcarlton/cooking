# 🍁 Mary Brown's Fried Chicken & Taters (Copycat)

## Overview
Atlantic Canada's favorite fried chicken chain. Crispy, well-seasoned chicken with signature taters (seasoned potato wedges). Founded in Newfoundland, now across Canada. Known for extra-crispy coating and perfect seasoning blend.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **DOUBLE DREDGE**: Buttermilk → flour → buttermilk → flour (ultra crispy)
- **SEASONING IN FLOUR**: Every layer seasoned (flavor throughout)
- **OIL TEMP 350°F**: Critical for crispy, non-greasy chicken
- **REST BEFORE FRYING**: 20 min for coating to set
- **TATERS SEPARATE**: Different seasoning than chicken

## The Mary Brown's Secret
- **Buttermilk brine** (2-4 hours, tenderizes)
- **Heavy seasoning** (garlic, onion, paprika, pepper)
- **Double coating** (thick, crunchy crust)
- **Pressure fried** (restaurant uses pressure fryers)
- **Big Mary sauce** (signature dipping sauce - ranch-based)
- **Taters** (wedges, not fries, heavily seasoned)

## Ingredients (serves 4)

**For Chicken:**
- 8 pieces chicken (legs, thighs, breasts)
- 2 cups (480ml) buttermilk
- 2 cups (240g) all-purpose flour
- 2 tbsp (18g) garlic powder
- 2 tbsp (12g) paprika
- 1 tbsp (9g) onion powder
- 1 tbsp (18g) salt
- 1 tsp (2g) black pepper
- 1 tsp (2g) cayenne
- Oil for frying

**For Big Mary Sauce:**
- 1 cup (240ml) ranch dressing
- 2 tbsp (30ml) hot sauce
- 1 tbsp (15ml) pickle juice
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder

**For Taters:**
- 4 large russet potatoes
- 2 tbsp (30ml) oil
- 2 tsp (6g) seasoning salt
- 1 tsp (3g) paprika
- 1 tsp (3g) garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp (1g) black pepper

## Method

### Chicken:
1. Brine chicken in buttermilk 2-4 hours
2. Mix flour and all spices
3. Dredge chicken: flour → buttermilk → flour
4. Rest 20 minutes
5. Fry at 350°F:
   - Wings/drums: 12-14 min
   - Thighs: 14-16 min
   - Breasts: 16-18 min
6. Internal temp 165°F

### Taters:
1. Cut potatoes into wedges
2. Toss with oil and seasonings
3. Bake 425°F for 35-40 minutes
4. Flip halfway through
5. Should be crispy outside, fluffy inside

### Big Mary Sauce:
1. Mix all ingredients
2. Refrigerate 30 min
3. Serve cold
