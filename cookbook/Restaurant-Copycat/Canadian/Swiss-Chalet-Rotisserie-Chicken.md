# 🍁 Swiss Chalet Rotisserie Chicken (Copycat)

## Overview
The rotisserie chicken that made Swiss Chalet Canada's go-to spot since 1954. Herb-seasoned, slow-roasted until the skin is golden and crackling. Served with legendary Chalet sauce, fries, and a roll. This copycat creates that signature seasoning and juicy result at home.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **DRY BRINE OVERNIGHT**: Salt and seasonings penetrate meat
- **AIR-DRY SKIN**: Uncovered in fridge 4+ hours (crispy skin)
- **ROTISSERIE OR SPATCHCOCK**: Even cooking (rotisserie ideal, spatchcock works)
- **INTERNAL 165°F**: Breast temp (thighs can go to 175°F)
- **REST 15 MINUTES**: Juices redistribute

## The Swiss Chalet Secret
- **Rotisserie cooking** (constant rotation, self-basting)
- **Herb seasoning blend** (garlic, paprika, herbs)
- **Slow roasted** (not high heat - stays moist)
- **Chalet sauce** (served with every chicken)
- **Quarter chicken portions** (leg/breast combo most popular)

## Ingredients (serves 4)

**For Chicken:**
- 1 whole chicken (3-4 lbs/1.4-1.8kg)
- 2 tbsp (36g) kosher salt
- 1 tbsp (9g) garlic powder
- 1 tbsp (6g) paprika
- 2 tsp (4g) dried thyme
- 2 tsp (4g) dried oregano
- 1 tsp (2g) black pepper
- 1 tsp (2g) onion powder
- 1/2 tsp (1g) cayenne
- 2 tbsp (30ml) vegetable oil

**For Serving:**
- Swiss Chalet Sauce (see separate recipe)
- Fries
- Dinner roll

## Method

### Dry Brine (Night Before):
1. Pat chicken completely dry
2. Mix all spices
3. Rub all over chicken (inside and out)
4. Place on rack over tray
5. Refrigerate uncovered 12-24 hours
6. Skin dries out (becomes crispy when roasted)

### Rotisserie Method (Best):
1. Remove chicken from fridge 1 hour before
2. Truss chicken (tie legs)
3. Place on rotisserie spit
4. Roast 350°F for 1-1.5 hours
5. Internal temp 165°F (breast)
6. Skin should be deep golden, crispy

### Oven Method (Alternative):
1. Spatchcock chicken (remove backbone)
2. Press flat on baking sheet
3. Roast 425°F for 45-55 minutes
4. Skin-side up throughout
5. Finish under broiler 3-5 min for extra crispy skin

### Serve:
1. Rest 15 minutes
2. Quarter chicken (2 legs, 2 breasts)
3. Serve with Chalet sauce, fries, roll

## Perfect Chicken Indicators
- **Skin**: Golden, crispy, not burnt
- **Meat**: Juicy, pulls from bone easily
- **Temp**: 165°F breast, 175°F thigh
- **Flavor**: Herby, garlicky, well-seasoned
