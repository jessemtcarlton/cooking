# 🍁 Swiss Chalet Dinner Roll (Copycat)

## Overview
The soft, slightly sweet dinner roll served with every Swiss Chalet meal. Brushed with butter, served warm, perfect for soaking up Chalet sauce. Simple but essential part of the Swiss Chalet experience.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **MILK AND BUTTER DOUGH**: Creates soft texture
- **TWO RISES**: Essential for light rolls
- **BRUSH WITH BUTTER**: While hot from oven
- **SERVE WARM**: Best within 30 minutes

## Ingredients (makes 12 rolls)

- 1 cup milk, warm
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1 packet yeast
- 1/4 cup butter, melted
- 1 egg
- 3 1/2 cups flour
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp butter, melted (for brushing)

## Method
1. Activate yeast in warm milk with sugar
2. Add melted butter, egg
3. Mix in flour and salt
4. Knead 8-10 minutes
5. First rise 1 hour (double in size)
6. Divide into 12 portions, shape into balls
7. Place in greased 9x13 pan (touching)
8. Second rise 30-45 minutes
9. Bake 375°F for 18-22 minutes
10. Brush with melted butter immediately
11. Serve warm

## Perfect Roll Indicators:
- Golden brown top
- Soft, pillowy texture
- Slightly sweet
- Pull-apart (touching while baking creates soft sides)
