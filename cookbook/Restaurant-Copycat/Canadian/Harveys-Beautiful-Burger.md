# 🍁 Harvey's "Beautiful Burger" (Copycat)

## Overview
The burger where you customize everything. Famous slogan: "Harvey's makes your hamburger a beautiful thing." Fresh toppings bar where you watch them dress your burger exactly how you want. Flame-grilled patty, toasted sesame bun, unlimited toppings. Canadian fast food institution since 1959.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **FLAME-GRILLED**: Charcoal or gas grill (not griddle - need char)
- **FRESH GROUND BEEF**: 80/20 blend, never frozen
- **TOAST THE BUN**: On grill (essential)
- **TOPPINGS BAR**: Set up all toppings (customization is the point)
- **ASSEMBLE FRESH**: Each burger custom-made

## The Harvey's Secret
- **Flame-grilled** (char marks, smoky flavor)
- **Fresh beef** (never frozen, ground daily)
- **Toppings bar** (customer chooses everything)
- **Pickles two ways** (dill slices or relish)
- **Harvey's sauce** (mayo-based, slightly tangy)
- **Fresh vegetables** (cut daily)
- **Customization** (the whole point of Harvey's)

## Ingredients (makes 4 burgers)

**Burgers:**
- 1.25 lbs (565g) ground beef (80/20)
- 4 sesame seed buns
- Salt and pepper

**Harvey's Sauce:**
- 1/2 cup (120ml) mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp (30ml) ketchup
- 1 tbsp (15ml) sweet pickle relish
- 1 tsp (5ml) white vinegar
- 1/2 tsp (2.5g) sugar
- 1/4 tsp garlic powder

**Toppings Bar (Set Up All):**
- Lettuce (shredded iceberg)
- Tomatoes (sliced)
- Onions (sliced, raw or grilled)
- Pickles (dill slices)
- Pickle relish
- Ketchup
- Mustard (yellow)
- Mayo
- Harvey's sauce
- Cheese (processed American)
- Bacon (optional)
- Hot peppers (banana peppers)

## Method

### Make Harvey's Sauce:
1. Mix all sauce ingredients
2. Refrigerate 30 min
3. Keeps 1 week

### Grill Burgers:
1. Form beef into 4 patties (5 oz each)
2. Make slight indent in center
3. Season with salt and pepper
4. Grill over high heat:
   - 4 min first side
   - Flip once
   - 3-4 min second side
5. Add cheese last minute if using
6. Internal temp 160°F (well-done is Harvey's style)

### Toast Buns:
1. Slice buns
2. Place cut-side down on grill
3. Toast 30-60 seconds
4. Should have grill marks

### Assemble (Customer's Choice):
**Harvey's Style:**
- Bottom bun
- Patty (with or without cheese)
- Customer chooses all toppings at bar
- Top bun

**Classic Harvey's Order:**
- Lettuce, tomato, onion, pickles
- Ketchup, mustard, Harvey's sauce
- That's the "works"
