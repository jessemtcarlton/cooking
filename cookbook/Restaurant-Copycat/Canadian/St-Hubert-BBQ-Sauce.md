# 🍁 St-Hubert BBQ Sauce (Copycat)

## Overview
Quebec's legendary BBQ sauce from the famous rotisserie chicken chain. Sweet, tangy, with hints of smoke and spice. Thinner than American BBQ sauce, more vinegar-forward. Perfect on chicken, ribs, or as a dipping sauce. This is the sauce that defines Quebec BBQ.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **SIMMER, DON'T BOIL**: High heat makes bitter
- **STRAIN FOR SMOOTH**: No chunks (unlike American BBQ sauce)
- **BALANCE SWEET/TANG**: Taste and adjust vinegar/sugar ratio
- **THIN CONSISTENCY**: Should be pourable, not thick like ketchup

## The St-Hubert Secret
- **Vinegar base** (more tangy than American BBQ)
- **Tomato paste** (not ketchup - richer flavor)
- **Brown sugar** (caramel notes)
- **Worcestershire sauce** (umami depth)
- **Spices** (mustard powder, garlic, onion)
- **Smooth texture** (no chunks or seeds)

## Ingredients (makes 2 cups)
- 1 cup (240ml) white vinegar
- 1/2 cup (120ml) water
- 1/2 cup (113g) tomato paste
- 1/2 cup (100g) brown sugar, packed
- 2 tbsp (30ml) Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tbsp (9g) onion powder
- 1 tbsp (9g) garlic powder
- 1 tsp (3g) dry mustard powder
- 1 tsp (3g) paprika
- 1/2 tsp (3g) kosher salt
- 1/4 tsp (0.5g) black pepper
- 1/4 tsp (0.5g) cayenne pepper

## Method
1. Whisk all ingredients in saucepan
2. Bring to simmer over medium heat
3. Reduce heat to low
4. Simmer 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally
5. Should thicken slightly
6. Strain through fine mesh (optional, for smooth texture)
7. Cool and store

**Pro Tip**: Makes ahead - flavors improve after 24 hours refrigerated.

**Cost**: $2 for 2 cups vs $5-6 for store-bought St-Hubert bottle

## Perfect For
- Rotisserie chicken (classic)
- Pork ribs
- Grilled chicken
- Dipping fries
- Pizza crust (Quebec style)

This is THE Quebec BBQ sauce. Tangier than American BBQ, perfect for chicken!
