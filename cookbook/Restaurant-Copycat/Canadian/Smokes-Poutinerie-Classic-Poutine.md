## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **FRESH CURDS MANDATORY**: 1-2 days old, room temperature (must squeak)
- **DOUBLE FRY**: Blanch 325°F then crisp 375°F (perfect texture)
- **GRAVY PIPING HOT**: Must melt curds slightly (not swimming, not cold)
- **ASSEMBLE FAST**: Serve immediately (fries get soggy)
- **GENEROUS PORTIONS**: Smoke's is known for massive servings

## Ingredients (serves 2-3 as main, 4 as side)

**Fries:**
- 4 large russet potatoes (2 lbs/900g)
- Oil for frying (peanut or canola)
- Salt

**Gravy:**
- 3 cups (720ml) beef stock
- 3 tbsp (45g) butter
- 3 tbsp (24g) flour
- 1 tbsp (15ml) soy sauce
- 1 tsp (5ml) Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tsp (3g) onion powder
- 1/2 tsp (1.5g) garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- Salt to taste

**Assembly:**
- 2 cups (300g) fresh cheese curds, room temperature

## Method

### Fries (Double Fry):
1. Cut potatoes into 1/2" thick fries (leave skin on)
2. Soak in cold water 30 min (removes starch)
3. Dry completely
4. **First fry** (blanch): 325°F for 4-5 min (pale, soft)
5. Drain, cool 10 min
6. **Second fry** (crisp): 375°F for 3-4 min (golden, crispy)
7. Drain, salt immediately

### Gravy:
1. Melt butter in saucepan
2. Whisk in flour, cook 2 min (roux)
3. Gradually add beef stock, whisking constantly
4. Add soy sauce, Worcestershire, spices
5. Simmer 10 min, stirring (should coat spoon)
6. Season with salt and pepper
7. Keep hot

### Assemble (The Smoke's Way):
1. Fill bowl/container with hot fries
2. Scatter cheese curds over top
3. Pour piping hot gravy over everything
4. Curds should start melting (but still have texture)
5. Serve immediately with fork

## Perfect Poutine Indicators
- **Fries**: Golden, crispy outside, fluffy inside
- **Curds**: Squeaky, slightly melted from gravy
- **Gravy**: Hot, thick, coats everything
- **Portion**: Generous (almost too much)
- **Temperature**: Hot throughout

## Pro Tips
**The Smoke's Experience:**
- Founded Toronto 2008 (Adam Smollett)
- 150+ locations Canada-wide
- 40+ poutine varieties
- Signature varieties: Traditional, Pulled Pork, Bacon Cheeseburger
- "Smokes" refers to slow-smoked meats

**Poutine Assembly Science:**
- Hot fries + hot gravy + room temp curds = perfect melt
- Cold curds from fridge = won't melt properly
- Gravy too cool = fries get soggy without melting curds
- Must eat immediately (doesn't hold)

**Popular Smoke's Variations:**
Add to Classic for loaded poutines:
- **Pulled Pork**: Add BBQ pulled pork, bacon, green onions
- **Bacon Cheeseburger**: Ground beef, bacon, pickles, onions
- **Veggie**: Mushrooms, peppers, onions, tomatoes

**Make-Ahead Tips:**
- Blanch fries ahead, store in fridge (crisp before serving)
- Make gravy ahead, reheat before serving
- Curds must be fresh (lose squeak after 3-4 days)
