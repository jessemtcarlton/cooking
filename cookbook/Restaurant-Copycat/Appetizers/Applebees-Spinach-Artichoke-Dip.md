# 🌟 Applebee's Spinach & Artichoke Dip (Copycat)

## Overview
Hot, creamy, cheesy spinach and artichoke dip served bubbling with tortilla chips. This is THE spinach artichoke dip that set the standard for all others. Creamy, gooey, perfectly seasoned, loaded with cheese. This copycat recipe creates the exact flavor and texture.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **SQUEEZE SPINACH DRY**: Excess water = watery dip (critical step)
- **SQUEEZE ARTICHOKES DRY**: Same - must be dry
- **CREAM CHEESE MUST BE SOFTENED**: Room temp for 1-2 hours or won't blend smooth
- **SHRED CHEESE YOURSELF**: Pre-shredded has anti-caking agents (won't melt smooth)
- **BAKE UNCOVERED**: Want golden bubbly top, not steamed
- **SERVE HOT**: Dip gets thick when it cools

## The Applebee's Secret
1. **Cream cheese base** (makes it thick and creamy)
2. **Three cheese blend** (Parmesan, mozzarella, provolone)
3. **Frozen spinach** (chopped, not leaf - better texture)
4. **Canned artichokes** (marinated, not plain - more flavor)
5. **Garlic and onion** (aromatics build flavor)
6. **Baked until bubbly** (golden top, hot center)
7. **Served in cast iron** (stays hot longer)

## Equipment
- Cast iron skillet (10") or oven-safe baking dish
- Large mixing bowl
- Saucepan
- Wooden spoon
- Grater (for cheese)
- Strainer or colander
- Clean kitchen towel (for squeezing)

## Ingredients (serves 6-8)

**For Dip:**
- 1 package (10 oz/283g) frozen chopped spinach, thawed
- 1 can (14 oz/400g) artichoke hearts, drained
- 8 oz (225g) cream cheese, softened to room temp
- 1/4 cup (60ml) sour cream
- 1/4 cup (60ml) mayonnaise
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/4 cup (40g) white onion, diced fine
- 1 cup (100g) shredded mozzarella cheese
- 1/2 cup (50g) shredded provolone cheese
- 1/2 cup (50g) grated Parmesan cheese, plus more for topping
- 1/4 tsp (1g) red pepper flakes (optional)
- 1/2 tsp (3g) kosher salt
- 1/4 tsp (0.5g) black pepper

**For Serving:**
- Tortilla chips (restaurant-style, thick)
- Or toasted baguette slices
- Or pita chips

## Method

### Step 1: Prep Vegetables (THE CRITICAL STEP)

**Spinach:**
1. Thaw frozen spinach completely
2. Place in strainer
3. Press out as much liquid as possible
4. Transfer to clean kitchen towel
5. Twist towel and squeeze HARD
6. Continue squeezing until almost dry
7. Should reduce to about 1/2 cup packed spinach
8. 🚨 **MUST BE DRY** - this is critical or dip will be watery

**Artichokes:**
1. Drain canned artichokes
2. Press in strainer to remove liquid
3. Wrap in kitchen towel
4. Squeeze dry
5. Chop into small pieces (1/4" dice)
6. 🚨 **SQUEEZE VERY DRY** - they hold a lot of liquid

### Step 2: Sauté Aromatics (5 minutes)
1. In saucepan over medium heat, add 1 tbsp butter or oil
2. Add diced onion
3. Sauté 3-4 minutes until softened
4. Add minced garlic
5. Sauté 1 minute more (just until fragrant)
6. Remove from heat

### Step 3: Mix Dip Base
1. In large mixing bowl, combine:
   - 8 oz (225g) softened cream cheese
   - 1/4 cup (60ml) sour cream
   - 1/4 cup (60ml) mayonnaise
2. Beat with spoon or mixer until smooth and creamy
3. Should be no lumps
4. 🚨 **CREAM CHEESE MUST BE ROOM TEMP** - or will be lumpy

### Step 4: Add Everything
1. To cream cheese mixture, add:
   - Squeezed-dry spinach
   - Chopped squeezed-dry artichokes
   - Sautéed onions and garlic
   - 1 cup (100g) shredded mozzarella
   - 1/2 cup (50g) shredded provolone
   - 1/2 cup (50g) grated Parmesan
   - 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes (if using)
   - 1/2 tsp (3g) salt
   - 1/4 tsp (0.5g) black pepper
2. Mix thoroughly until well combined
3. Taste and adjust seasoning

### Step 5: Transfer to Baking Dish
1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C)
2. Grease cast iron skillet or baking dish
3. Spread dip evenly in dish
4. Smooth top with spatula
5. Sprinkle extra Parmesan on top (2-3 tbsp)
6. Optional: drizzle with olive oil

### Step 6: Bake (25-30 minutes)
1. Bake at 375°F (190°C) for 25-30 minutes
2. **Check at 25 minutes**:
   - Bubbling at edges
   - Golden brown spots on top
   - Hot throughout (use spoon to check center)
3. If not golden, broil 2-3 minutes
4. Should be bubbly and lightly browned

### Step 7: Serve Immediately
1. Remove from oven (very hot!)
2. Let cool 5 minutes (too hot to eat right away)
3. Serve in the dish it was baked in (stays hot)
4. Surround with tortilla chips
5. Provide spoons for scooping
6. 🚨 **SERVE HOT** - gets thick when cool

**Total Time: 45 minutes**
- Prep vegetables: 10 min
- Sauté aromatics: 5 min
- Mix dip: 5 min
- Bake: 25-30 min

**Make-Ahead:**
- Mix dip completely, refrigerate up to 1 day

- Bake just before serving

## Troubleshooting

**Watery dip:**
- Didn't squeeze spinach dry enough (most common issue)
- Didn't squeeze artichokes dry
- Used too much sour cream or mayo
- Didn't bake long enough

**Lumpy texture:**
- Cream cheese wasn't softened (must be room temp)
- Didn't mix thoroughly
- Used low-fat cream cheese (doesn't blend as smooth)

**Bland flavor:**
- Not enough salt (taste and adjust)
- Garlic not fresh (use fresh, not powdered)
- Not enough Parmesan (Parm adds savory depth)
- Forgot to sauté garlic and onions

**Too thick:**
- Not enough sour cream or mayo
- Add 2 tbsp milk or cream
- Mix and bake

**Oily/greasy:**
- Used pre-shredded cheese (has oil coating)
- Too much cheese
- Baked too long at too high temp

## Variations

### Extra Creamy
- Add 1/4 cup (60ml) heavy cream
- Use full-fat everything

### Spicy Spinach Artichoke Dip
- Add 1 diced jalapeño
- Increase red pepper flakes to 1/2 tsp
- Add dash of hot sauce

### Bacon Spinach Artichoke Dip
- Top with 4 strips cooked crumbled bacon
- Add before or after baking

### Crab Spinach Artichoke Dip
- Add 1/2 cup lump crab meat
- Mix in before baking
- Premium upgrade

### Lighter Version
- Use low-fat cream cheese and sour cream
- Reduce mayo to 2 tbsp
- Still tasty, less calories

## Storage and Reheating

**Refrigerator:**
- Store covered up to 3 days
- Dip thickens when cold (this is normal)

**Reheating:**
- Microwave in 30-second intervals, stirring
- Or reheat covered in 350°F oven for 15-20 minutes
- Add splash of milk if too thick
- Stir well before serving

**Freezing:**
- Can freeze up to 2 months
- Thaw in fridge overnight
- Texture may change slightly (creamier)
- Reheat thoroughly before serving

## Pro Tips

**Restaurant Secrets:**
- Applebee's uses frozen spinach (easier, consistent)
- Marinated artichokes (more flavor than plain)
- Made in batches, portioned, baked to order
- Served in cast iron (keeps hot 20+ minutes)
- Always garnished with Parmesan
- Chips served on the side (not on top - stay crispy)

**For Best Results:**
- Squeeze spinach and artichokes VERY dry (most important step)
- Room temp cream cheese (must be soft)
- Shred cheese yourself (pre-shredded doesn't melt smooth)
- Fresh garlic (not jarred or powdered)
- Bake until bubbling and golden
- Serve in cast iron or oven-safe dish (stays hot)
- Don't skip the Parmesan topping

**Slow Cooker Method:**
- Mix everything except top Parmesan
- Cook on LOW 2-3 hours
- Stir occasionally
- Top with Parmesan last 30 min
- Perfect for parties (stays warm)
