# 🌟 Outback Steakhouse Bloomin' Onion (Copycat)

## Overview
The most iconic restaurant appetizer in America. A colossal onion, cut to bloom like a flower, battered, fried until crispy, and served with tangy Bloom Sauce. This copycat recipe creates the exact experience for 1/4 the restaurant price.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **OIL TEMPERATURE CRITICAL**: 350-375°F - lower = greasy, higher = burnt
- **LARGE ONION REQUIRED**: 4" diameter minimum or won't bloom properly
- **CUTTING IS TRICKY**: Cut too deep = falls apart, too shallow = won't bloom
- **OIL DEPTH**: Need 3-4" deep oil (3-4 quarts) - deep pot or fryer required
- **WILL SPATTER**: Oil + wet batter = splattering - use splatter screen
- **DRAIN THOROUGHLY**: Paper towels critical or batter stays soggy

## The Outback Secret
1. **Colossal sweet onion** (Vidalia or sweet yellow)
2. **Specialized cutting** (16-20 petals, connected at root)
3. **Seasoned flour coating** (cayenne, paprika, herbs)
4. **Buttermilk batter** (tangy, helps coating stick)
5. **Bloom sauce** (mayo-based with horseradish and spices)
6. **Deep fry twice** (once to cook, once to crisp)

## Equipment
- Large heavy pot or deep fryer (need 5-6 quart capacity)
- Oil thermometer or deep-fry thermometer
- Sharp chef's knife
- Spider strainer or slotted spoon
- Paper towels (lots)
- Wire rack over baking sheet
- Splatter screen (optional but recommended)

## Ingredients

**For Bloomin' Onion:**
- 1 colossal sweet onion (Vidalia, Maui, or sweet yellow, 4"+ diameter)
- Oil for frying (vegetable or peanut, 3-4 quarts)

**For Seasoned Flour:**
- 2 cups (240g) all-purpose flour
- 2 tsp (6g) cayenne pepper (adjust for heat)
- 2 tsp (4g) paprika
- 1 tsp (3g) garlic powder
- 1 tsp (3g) onion powder
- 1/2 tsp (1g) dried thyme
- 1/2 tsp (1g) dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp (3g) black pepper
- 1/2 tsp (3g) kosher salt

**For Batter:**
- 1 1/2 cups (360ml) buttermilk
- 2 eggs
- 1 cup (120g) all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp (6g) kosher salt

**For Bloom Sauce:**
- 1/2 cup (120ml) mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp (30ml) ketchup
- 2 tbsp (30ml) horseradish cream
- 1/4 tsp (0.5g) cayenne pepper
- 1/4 tsp (0.75g) paprika
- 1/4 tsp (0.75g) garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp (0.75g) dried oregano
- 1/8 tsp black pepper
- Pinch of salt

## Method

### Step 1: Make Bloom Sauce (Can Make Day Ahead)
1. Mix all sauce ingredients in bowl:
   - 1/2 cup (120ml) mayo
   - 2 tbsp (30ml) ketchup
   - 2 tbsp (30ml) horseradish
   - All spices
2. Whisk until smooth
3. Refrigerate at least 30 minutes (flavors meld)
4. Can make up to 3 days ahead

### Step 2: Cut the Onion (THE CRITICAL STEP)

**Preparation:**
1. Peel onion completely
2. Cut 3/4" off pointed top (NOT root end)
3. Leave root end intact (holds onion together)

**The Bloom Cut:**
1. Place onion cut-side DOWN (root up)
2. Make first cut 1/2" from root:
   - Cut straight down
   - Stop 1/2" from cutting board (don't cut through root)
3. Rotate 90°, make another cut (creates quarters)
4. Make cuts between each section (creates 8 sections)
5. Make cuts between each section again (creates 16 sections)
6. 🚨 **CRITICAL**: All cuts stop 1/2" from root (root holds it together)

**Opening the Bloom:**
1. Turn onion over (cut-side up)
2. Gently spread petals apart
3. Work from center outward
4. Should look like blooming flower
5. Don't force - be gentle or petals break

**Soak:**
1. Place cut onion in ice water
2. Soak 30 minutes to 2 hours
3. Petals will open more, become crisp
4. This is the "blooming" step

### Step 3: Prepare Coatings

**Seasoned Flour:**
1. Mix all seasoned flour ingredients in large bowl
2. Set aside

**Batter:**
1. Whisk together:
   - 1 1/2 cups (360ml) buttermilk
   - 2 eggs
   - 1 cup (120g) flour
   - 1 tsp (6g) salt
2. Should be thick but pourable (like pancake batter)

### Step 4: Heat Oil
1. Add oil to large pot (3-4" deep, about 3-4 quarts)
2. Heat to 375°F (190°C)
3. Use thermometer - temperature is critical
4. While oil heats, coat onion

### Step 5: Coat the Onion (Multi-Step Process)

1. Remove onion from ice water
2. Drain thoroughly, pat with paper towels
3. Place cut-side up in large bowl

**First Coating (Seasoned Flour):**
1. Sprinkle seasoned flour generously over onion
2. Work flour between petals (get in all crevices)
3. Turn upside down, tap to remove excess
4. Set aside

**Batter Coating:**
1. Pour buttermilk batter over onion (upside down in bowl)
2. Make sure batter gets between all petals
3. Lift out, let excess drip off

**Second Flour Coating:**
1. Sprinkle more seasoned flour over battered onion
2. Work into all petals
3. Get flour between every petal
4. Shake off excess
5. Should be completely coated, no onion showing

### Step 6: First Fry (5-6 minutes)
1. Oil should be 375°F (190°C)
2. Carefully lower onion into oil cut-side UP
3. 🚨 **WILL SPATTER** - use splatter screen or stand back
4. Fry 5-6 minutes
5. Oil will bubble vigorously
6. Onion will turn light golden
7. Carefully flip using two spider strainers
8. Fry cut-side DOWN 2-3 minutes more
9. Should be light golden, not dark brown

### Step 7: Drain and Cool (3 minutes)
1. Carefully remove from oil (use spider strainer and slotted spoon)
2. Let drain over pot 30 seconds
3. Place on wire rack over baking sheet
4. Let cool 3 minutes
5. Oil temp will recover to 375°F (190°C)

### Step 8: Second Fry (2-3 minutes - THE CRISPY STEP)
1. Oil should be back to 375°F (190°C)
2. Carefully lower onion back into oil (cut-side UP)
3. Fry 2-3 minutes
4. Petals will crisp up, turn deep golden
5. 🚨 **WATCH CLOSELY** - can burn quickly
6. When deep golden and crispy, remove

### Step 9: Final Drain and Serve
1. Remove from oil carefully
2. Drain over pot
3. Place on paper towel-lined plate
4. Blot top gently with paper towels
5. Let drain 2 minutes
6. Transfer to serving plate
7. Serve immediately with Bloom Sauce in center

## Perfect Bloomin' Onion Indicators
- **Appearance**: Flower shape, deep golden brown
- **Texture**: Crispy exterior, tender onion inside
- **Size**: Opens to 6-8" diameter
- **Petals**: Separated, crispy, not soggy
- **Coating**: Adherent, crispy, evenly browned
- **Sauce**: Tangy, creamy, spicy kick

## Restaurant Serving Style
- Serve on large plate (needs space)
- Bloom Sauce in small bowl in center
- Garnish with parsley (optional)
- Serve immediately while hot and crispy
- Provide small plates for sharing
- Petals pull off easily for dipping

## Timing

**Total Active Time: 45 minutes**
- Cut onion: 10 min
- Soak: 30 min (passive)
- Make sauce: 5 min
- Coat onion: 10 min
- Heat oil: 15 min
- Fry: 10 min total
- Drain and serve: 5 min

## Troubleshooting

**Onion fell apart:**
- Cut too deep (must stop 1/2" from root)
- Root wasn't intact
- Too rough when spreading petals

**Coating fell off:**
- Onion too wet (must pat dry)
- Not enough flour between petals
- Oil too hot (coating burned before sticking)
- Batter too thin

**Soggy, not crispy:**
- Oil too cold (need 375°F)
- Didn't drain well enough
- Skipped second fry
- Served too late (best within 5 min of frying)

**Burned coating:**
- Oil too hot (over 385°F)
- Fried too long
- Dark spices burn easily (watch closely)

**Won't bloom properly:**
- Onion too small (need 4"+ diameter)
- Not enough cuts (need 16-20 petals)
- Didn't soak in ice water long enough

## Variations

### Spicy Bloomin' Onion
- Increase cayenne to 1 tbsp in flour
- Add hot sauce to batter
- Add extra cayenne to Bloom Sauce

### Extra Crispy
- Double-dip: Flour → Batter → Flour → Batter → Flour
- Results in thicker, crunchier coating

### Different Sauces
- Ranch dressing
- Sriracha mayo
- Chipotle aioli
- Comeback sauce

## Storage and Reheating

**Best Fresh:**
- Bloomin' Onion is best within 5 minutes of frying
- Quality degrades quickly

**If Must Store:**
- Refrigerate up to 1 day
- Reheat at 375°F in oven 10 minutes (won't be as crispy)

**Bloom Sauce:**
- Refrigerate up to 1 week
- Bring to room temp before serving

## Safety Tips

**Hot Oil Safety:**
- Never leave hot oil unattended
- Keep kids and pets away
- Have fire extinguisher nearby (NOT water - grease fire)
- Don't overfill pot (oil expands when onion added)
- Use long-handled tools
- Splatter screen recommended
- Let oil cool completely before disposing

**Disposal:**
- Let oil cool completely (hours)
- Strain and reuse (up to 3 times) or
- Pour into container, dispose in trash (NOT sink)

## Pro Tips

**Restaurant Secrets:**
- Outback uses special onion cutters (automated)
- Onions are pre-cut and soaked
- They fry at precisely 375°F
- Fresh oil daily

**For Best Results:**
