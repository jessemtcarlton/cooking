# 🌟 The Cheesecake Factory Avocado Egg Rolls (Copycat)

## Overview
Crispy egg roll wrappers filled with creamy avocado, sun-dried tomatoes, red onions, and cilantro. Served with sweet tamarind-cashew dipping sauce. These are THE most popular appetizer at Cheesecake Factory - unique, addictive, impossible to replicate anywhere else. Until now.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **AVOCADOS MUST BE RIPE**: But not mushy - should be firm enough to dice
- **DON'T OVERFILL**: Too much filling = egg rolls burst
- **SEAL EDGES WITH EGG WASH**: Or they'll open during frying
- **OIL TEMP CRITICAL**: 350-375°F - lower = soggy, higher = burnt
- **FREEZE 30 MINUTES BEFORE FRYING**: Prevents avocado from getting mushy
- **MAKE SAUCE AHEAD**: Needs to chill (flavors develop)

## The Cheesecake Factory Secret
1. **Ripe avocado chunks** (not mashed - want texture)
2. **Sun-dried tomatoes** (concentrated sweet-tart flavor)
3. **Red onion** (sharp bite)
4. **Cilantro** (fresh, bright)
5. **Egg roll wrappers** (crispy when fried)

## Equipment
- Large mixing bowl
- Sharp knife and cutting board
- Small bowl (for egg wash)
- Pastry brush
- Deep fryer or heavy pot
- Oil thermometer
- Spider strainer or slotted spoon
- Paper towels and wire rack
- Blender (for sauce)

## Ingredients (makes 8 egg rolls, serves 4)

**For Egg Rolls:**
- 3 ripe avocados
- 1/4 cup (35g) sun-dried tomatoes (oil-packed), diced fine
- 1/4 cup (40g) red onion, diced fine
- 2 tbsp (8g) fresh cilantro, chopped
- 1/2 tsp (3g) kosher salt
- 1/4 tsp (0.5g) black pepper
- Juice of 1/2 lime
- 8 egg roll wrappers (square, 7-8" size)
- 1 egg, beaten (for sealing)
- Oil for frying (vegetable or canola, about 6 cups/1.4L)

**For Tamarind-Cashew Dipping Sauce:**
- 1/4 cup (35g) roasted cashews
- 1/4 cup (60ml) tamarind paste or concentrate
- 1/4 cup (60ml) honey
- 2 tbsp (30ml) white vinegar or rice vinegar
- 2 tbsp (30ml) balsamic vinegar
- 1 tsp (5ml) soy sauce
- 1/2 cup (120ml) water (adjust for consistency)
- 1/4 tsp (0.75g) garlic powder
- Pinch of cayenne pepper
- 1 tsp (5ml) sesame oil (optional)

## Method

### Step 1: Make Dipping Sauce (Can make days ahead)
1. Add to blender:
   - 1/4 cup (35g) cashews
   - 1/4 cup (60ml) tamarind paste
   - 1/4 cup (60ml) honey
   - 2 tbsp (30ml) white vinegar
   - 2 tbsp (30ml) balsamic vinegar
   - 1 tsp (5ml) soy sauce
   - 1/2 cup (120ml) water
   - 1/4 tsp garlic powder
   - Pinch cayenne
   - 1 tsp sesame oil (if using)
2. Blend on high 1-2 minutes until completely smooth
3. Should be pourable but thick (like honey)
4. If too thick, add water 1 tbsp at a time
5. If too thin, add more cashews
6. Refrigerate at least 1 hour (flavors develop)
7. Can make up to 1 week ahead
8. 🚨 **SAUCE MUST BE SMOOTH** - no chunks

### Step 2: Prep Filling (15 minutes)
1. Cut 3 avocados in half, remove pits
2. Dice into 1/4"-1/2" cubes (not too small)
3. Place in bowl
4. Add:
   - 1/4 cup (35g) diced sun-dried tomatoes
   - 1/4 cup (40g) diced red onion (very fine)
   - 2 tbsp (8g) chopped cilantro
   - 1/2 tsp (3g) salt
   - 1/4 tsp (0.5g) black pepper
   - Juice of 1/2 lime
5. Fold gently to combine (don't mash avocado)
6. Should be chunky, not mushy
7. 🚨 **GENTLE FOLDING** - want distinct avocado chunks

### Step 3: Roll Egg Rolls (THE TRICKY PART)

**Prep Station:**
1. Beat 1 egg in small bowl
2. Have pastry brush ready
3. Clear workspace
4. Have clean plate or tray ready

**Rolling Technique:**
1. Lay egg roll wrapper on work surface (diamond shape, corner facing you)
2. Place 2-3 tbsp filling in center (lower third)
3. Don't overfill (egg roll will burst)
4. Fold bottom corner over filling (snug but not tight)
5. Fold in left and right corners (like envelope)
6. Roll up tightly from bottom
7. Brush top corner with beaten egg
8. Press to seal
9. Place seam-side down on plate
10. Repeat with remaining wrappers and filling
11. 🚨 **SEAL WELL** - egg wash is critical

### Step 4: Freeze (30 minutes - IMPORTANT)
1. Place rolled egg rolls on parchment-lined tray
2. Make sure they're not touching
3. Freeze 30 minutes (or up to 2 hours)
4. This firms up avocado, prevents mushiness
5. Can freeze longer (see make-ahead)
6. 🚨 **DON'T SKIP FREEZING** - avocado will get mushy if fried fresh

### Step 5: Heat Oil
1. Heat oil to 350-375°F (177-190°C)
2. Need 2-3" deep oil (about 6 cups)
3. Use thermometer - temp is critical
4. While oil heats, remove egg rolls from freezer

### Step 6: Fry Egg Rolls (3-4 minutes per batch)
1. Carefully lower 2-3 frozen egg rolls into oil
2. Don't crowd (temp will drop)
3. Fry 3-4 minutes, turning occasionally
4. Should be:
   - Deep golden brown
   - Crispy and bubbling
   - Evenly colored all over
5. Remove with spider strainer
6. Drain on wire rack (not paper towels)
7. Let oil return to temp before next batch
8. 🚨 **WATCH CLOSELY** - brown quickly

### Step 7: Serve Immediately
1. Let cool 3-4 minutes (filling is very hot)
2. Cut each egg roll in half diagonally
3. Arrange on plate (standing up shows cross-section)
4. Drizzle sauce on plate
5. Serve with sauce in bowl for dipping
6. Garnish with cilantro (optional)
7. 🚨 **SERVE HOT** - best within 15 minutes

## Troubleshooting

**Egg rolls burst during frying:**
- Overfilled (use 2-3 tbsp filling max)
- Not sealed well (need egg wash on edge)
- Oil too hot (wrapper burned before sealed)
- Didn't freeze (avocado expanded when hot)

**Soggy wrapper:**
- Oil temp too low (need 350-375°F)
- Crowded the pot (temp dropped)
- Drained on paper towels only (need wire rack)
- Served too late (coating absorbs moisture)

**Mushy avocado:**
- Avocado too ripe (should be firm)
- Didn't freeze before frying
- Fried too long (overheated filling)

**Wrapper not crispy:**
- Oil temp too low
- Not fried long enough
- Egg rolls touching each other during frying

**Sauce too thick:**
- Not enough water
- Add water 1 tbsp at a time
- Blend again

**Sauce too thin:**
- Too much water
- Simmer to reduce (or add more cashews)

## Variations

### Spicy Version
- Add 1 diced jalapeño to filling
- Add sriracha to dipping sauce

### Extra Creamy
- Add 2 oz cream cheese to filling
- Mix with avocado

### Chicken Avocado Egg Rolls
- Add 1/2 cup diced cooked chicken to filling
- More substantial

### Baked Version (Healthier)
- Brush egg rolls with oil
- Bake at 425°F for 15-20 minutes
- Flip halfway through
- Less crispy but still good

## Air Fryer Method

**Lower Fat Option:**
1. Roll egg rolls as directed
2. Freeze 30 minutes
3. Brush with oil
4. Air fry at 375°F for 10-12 minutes
5. Flip halfway through
6. Spray with more oil after flipping
7. Should be golden and crispy

## Storage

**Before Frying:**
- Freeze rolled egg rolls up to 1 month
- Store in freezer bag
- Fry from frozen (add 1 minute to cook time)

**After Frying:**
- Not ideal to store
- Best fresh
- If must store: refrigerate 1 day, reheat in oven

**Sauce:**
- Refrigerate up to 1 week
- Stir before serving
- Can thin with water if needed

## Pro Tips

**Restaurant Secrets:**
- Cheesecake Factory makes these fresh daily
- Egg rolls are assembled in morning, fried to order
- Filling is very simple (no spices except salt, pepper, lime)
- Sauce is the secret (sweet, tangy, nutty complexity)
- Freezing step is mandatory (prevents mushy avocado)
- Served immediately (don't hold in warmer)

**For Best Results:**
- Use ripe but firm avocados (should be slightly firm to touch)
- Dice avocado (don't mash) - want chunks
- Sun-dried tomatoes in oil (not dry-packed) - more flavor
- Seal edges well with egg wash
- Freeze 30 min minimum (1-2 hours is fine)
- Don't overfill (2-3 tbsp filling max)
- Fry at 350-375°F (use thermometer)
- Serve immediately (coating gets soggy)

**Tamarind Note:**
- Find in Asian grocery stores
- Or substitute: 2 tbsp molasses + 2 tbsp lime juice (not exact but close)
- Tamarind paste vs concentrate: Both work, concentrate is thicker
