# 🌟 TGI Friday's Mozzarella Sticks (Copycat)

## Overview
The gold standard of mozzarella sticks. Crispy, seasoned coating with gooey melted cheese inside. Served with marinara sauce. These are better than the restaurant version - crispy exterior that stays crispy, cheese that stretches but doesn't explode out.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **FREEZE BEFORE FRYING**: Must freeze 2+ hours or cheese will leak out
- **OIL TEMPERATURE CRITICAL**: 350-365°F - lower = soggy, higher = burnt outside/cold inside
- **DOUBLE BREAD**: Flour → egg → breadcrumbs → egg → breadcrumbs (double coat)
- **USE STRING CHEESE**: Real mozzarella sticks (not fresh mozzarella)
- **FRY IN BATCHES**: Don't crowd or oil temp drops
- **WATCH CLOSELY**: 1-2 minutes max - any longer and cheese explodes

## The TGI Friday's Secret
1. **String cheese cut in half** (perfect size, even melting)
2. **Italian seasoning in breading** (signature flavor)
3. **Double breading** (thick, crispy coating)
4. **Freeze before frying** (prevents cheese leakage)
5. **Quick fry** (1-2 minutes only)
6. **Marinara for dipping** (classic pairing)

## Equipment
- Deep fryer or heavy pot (3-4 quart capacity)
- Oil thermometer
- 3 shallow bowls (breading station)
- Baking sheet
- Parchment paper
- Spider strainer or slotted spoon
- Paper towels
- Wire rack

## Ingredients (serves 4-6, makes 24 sticks)

**For Mozzarella Sticks:**
- 12 mozzarella string cheese sticks
- 1 cup (120g) all-purpose flour
- 3 large eggs
- 2 tbsp (30ml) milk
- 2 cups (200g) Italian-seasoned breadcrumbs (or make your own below)
- 1/2 tsp (3g) kosher salt
- 1/4 tsp (0.5g) black pepper
- 1/4 tsp (0.75g) garlic powder
- Oil for frying (vegetable or canola, about 4 cups/1L)

**For Homemade Italian Breadcrumbs (if not using store-bought):**
- 2 cups (200g) plain breadcrumbs
- 2 tsp (4g) Italian seasoning
- 1 tsp (3g) garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp (3g) salt
- 1/2 tsp (2g) paprika

**For Marinara Sauce:**
- 1 cup (240ml) marinara sauce (store-bought or homemade)
- Heat before serving

## Method

### Step 1: Prep Cheese (5 minutes)
1. Unwrap 12 string cheese sticks
2. Cut each in half crosswise
3. Creates 24 pieces (perfect size)
4. Return to fridge until needed
5. 🚨 **KEEP COLD** - warm cheese = disaster

### Step 2: Set Up Breading Station

**Bowl 1 - Flour:**
- 1 cup (120g) all-purpose flour
- 1/2 tsp (3g) salt
- 1/4 tsp (0.5g) black pepper

**Bowl 2 - Egg Wash:**
- 3 eggs
- 2 tbsp (30ml) milk
- Whisk together until combined

**Bowl 3 - Breadcrumbs:**
- 2 cups (200g) Italian-seasoned breadcrumbs
- 1/4 tsp (0.75g) garlic powder
- Mix together

### Step 3: Double Bread the Cheese (THE CRITICAL STEP)

**First Coating:**
1. Take cold cheese stick
2. Roll in flour (coat completely)
3. Dip in egg wash (coat completely)
4. Roll in breadcrumbs (press to adhere)
5. Place on parchment-lined baking sheet

**Second Coating (Makes Them AMAZING):**
1. Dip breaded stick back in egg wash
2. Roll in breadcrumbs again
3. Press breadcrumbs firmly to adhere
4. Place on baking sheet
5. Repeat for all 24 pieces
6. 🚨 **DOUBLE COAT ALL STICKS** - this prevents leaks

### Step 4: Freeze (2-4 hours - MANDATORY)
1. Place baking sheet in freezer
2. Freeze at least 2 hours (overnight is fine)
3. Can freeze up to 1 month (then fry from frozen)
4. 🚨 **DON'T SKIP FREEZING** - cheese will explode if not frozen
5. Must be completely frozen solid

### Step 5: Heat Oil
1. Add oil to pot (3-4" deep, about 4 cups)
2. Heat to 350°F (177°C)
3. Use thermometer - temp is critical
4. While oil heats, remove frozen sticks from freezer
5. Keep frozen until ready to fry

### Step 6: Fry (1-2 minutes per batch)
1. Oil at 350-365°F (177-185°C)
2. Carefully add 4-6 frozen sticks (don't crowd)
3. Fry 1-2 minutes
4. **WATCH CLOSELY**:
   - Golden brown coating
   - Slightly puffed
   - If frying too long, cheese will leak
5. Remove with spider strainer
6. Drain on wire rack or paper towels
7. Let oil return to temp (30 seconds)
8. Repeat with remaining sticks
9. 🚨 **FRY TIME IS SHORT** - 1-2 min only!

### Step 7: Serve Immediately
1. Let cool 2-3 minutes (cheese is molten)
2. Serve hot with warm marinara sauce
3. Should be crispy outside, gooey stretchy inside
4. 🚨 **BEST WITHIN 10 MINUTES** - coating gets soggy over time

## Perfect Mozzarella Stick Indicators
- **Exterior**: Golden brown, crispy, crunchy
- **Interior**: Melted, gooey, stretchy cheese
- **Coating**: Thick, seasoned, adhered (not falling off)
- **No Leaks**: Cheese stays inside (double breading + freezing)
- **Temperature**: Hot, but not mouth-burning
- **Stretch**: Cheese pulls when bitten

**Total Active Time: 30 minutes (plus 2+ hours freezing)**
- Prep cheese: 5 min

- Freeze: 2+ hours (passive)

- Perfect for parties

## Troubleshooting

**Cheese leaked out:**
- Didn't freeze long enough (need 2+ hours)
- Oil too hot (coating burned before cheese melted)
- Fried too long (should be 1-2 min only)
- Single breading (need double coat)

**Coating fell off:**
- Didn't press breadcrumbs firmly
- Skipped double breading
- Oil not hot enough (coating absorbed oil)

**Soggy coating:**
- Oil temp too low (need 350-365°F)
- Crowded the pan (dropped oil temp)
- Not enough oil (sticks touching bottom)
- Didn't drain on wire rack

**Burnt outside, cold inside:**
- Oil too hot (over 375°F)
- Started with room temp cheese (must be frozen)

**Not crispy:**
- Oil temp too low
- Didn't drain properly
- Served too late (coating absorbs moisture)

## Storage and Reheating

**Before Frying:**
- Freeze breaded sticks up to 1 month
- Store in freezer bags
- Fry from frozen when needed

**After Frying:**
- Best fresh (within 10 minutes)
- Refrigerate leftovers up to 2 days
- Reheat at 400°F in oven 8-10 minutes
- Or air fryer 375°F for 5-6 minutes
- Don't microwave (coating gets soggy)

## Pro Tips

**Restaurant Secrets:**
- TGI Friday's uses pre-breaded frozen sticks (they don't bread in-house)
- Fried at exactly 350°F
- Timer set for 90 seconds
- Served immediately
- Marinara is warmed (not cold)

**For Best Results:**
- String cheese works better than block mozzarella (less moisture)
- Double breading is mandatory (prevents leaks)
- Freeze completely (overnight is ideal)
- Don't overcrowd fryer (4-6 sticks per batch)
- Drain on wire rack, not paper towels only
- Serve immediately (coating gets soggy within 30 min)
- Oil temp is critical (use thermometer)

**Freezer Stash Strategy:**
- Make large batch (48+ sticks)
- Freeze in bags (12 per bag)
- Fry directly from frozen when craving hits
- Perfect party appetizer

## Air Fryer Method

**If You Don't Want to Deep Fry:**
1. Bread and freeze as directed
2. Preheat air fryer to 390°F (199°C)
3. Spray frozen sticks with cooking spray
4. Air fry 6-8 minutes
5. Flip halfway through
6. Watch closely (cooking time varies by air fryer)
7. Less greasy, still crispy
