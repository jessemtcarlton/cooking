# 🌟 Chili's Texas Cheese Fries (Copycat)

## Overview
Loaded steak fries topped with melted cheese, crispy bacon, green onions, and served with ranch dressing. These fully-loaded fries are a Chili's signature - massive portion, perfectly crispy fries, tons of toppings. This copycat creates the exact experience.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **DOUBLE FRY METHOD**: Fry twice for crispy exterior, fluffy interior
- **CHEESE SAUCE, NOT SHREDDED**: Liquid cheese sauce coats better, stays gooey
- **BACON MUST BE CRISPY**: Chewy bacon ruins the texture
- **LAYER THE TOPPINGS**: Cheese first (so it melts), then bacon and onions
- **SERVE IMMEDIATELY**: Fries get soggy fast once topped

## The Chili's Secret
1. **Thick steak fries** (not thin fries - need substance)
2. **Double fry method** (blanch then crisp)
3. **Liquid cheese sauce** (smooth, melted, coats fries)
4. **Crispy bacon bits** (chopped fine, maximum coverage)
5. **Fresh green onions** (bright flavor, color contrast)
6. **Ranch for dipping** (cooling contrast)
7. **Massive portion** (meant for sharing)

## Equipment
- Deep fryer or heavy pot (5-6 quart capacity)
- Oil thermometer
- Spider strainer or slotted spoon
- Large oven-safe platter or baking sheet
- Paper towels
- Cutting board and knife

## Ingredients (serves 4-6 as appetizer)

**For Fries:**
- 3 lbs (1.4kg) russet potatoes (about 4-5 large)
- Oil for frying (vegetable or peanut, about 8 cups/2L)
- Kosher salt

**For Cheese Sauce:**
- 2 tbsp (28g) butter
- 2 tbsp (16g) all-purpose flour
- 1 cup (240ml) whole milk
- 2 cups (225g) shredded cheddar cheese (sharp)
- 1/4 tsp (0.75g) garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp (0.75g) onion powder
- Pinch of cayenne pepper
- Salt to taste

**For Toppings:**
- 8 slices bacon, cooked crispy and chopped
- 4 green onions, sliced thin (white and green parts)
- Ranch dressing for serving (1 cup/240ml)

## Method

### Step 1: Prep Potatoes (10 minutes)
1. Wash and scrub 3 lbs (1.4kg) russet potatoes
2. Can peel or leave skin on (Chili's leaves it on)
3. Cut into steak fries:
   - Cut potato in half lengthwise
   - Cut each half into 4-5 wedges
   - About 1/2" thick
4. Rinse cut fries in cold water (removes excess starch)
5. Pat completely dry with paper towels
6. 🚨 **MUST BE DRY** - wet fries = oil splatter

### Step 2: Cook Bacon (10 minutes)
1. While fries dry, cook 8 slices bacon
2. Bake at 400°F for 15-20 min OR
3. Pan fry until very crispy
4. Drain on paper towels
5. Chop into small pieces (fine bits)
6. Set aside
7. 🚨 **MUST BE CRISPY** - chewy bacon = soggy topping

### Step 3: Make Cheese Sauce (10 minutes)

**Make the Roux:**
1. In saucepan, melt 2 tbsp (28g) butter over medium heat
2. Add 2 tbsp (16g) flour
3. Whisk constantly for 1-2 minutes (blonde roux)
4. Should smell nutty, no raw flour smell

**Add Milk:**
1. Slowly add 1 cup (240ml) milk, whisking constantly
2. Whisk until smooth, no lumps
3. Simmer 2-3 minutes until thickened
4. Should coat back of spoon

**Add Cheese:**
1. Remove from heat
2. Add 2 cups (225g) shredded sharp cheddar
3. Add 1/4 tsp garlic powder, 1/4 tsp onion powder
4. Add pinch cayenne pepper
5. Stir until cheese melted and smooth
6. Taste, add salt if needed
7. Keep warm on lowest heat
8. If too thick, thin with splash of milk
9. If too thin, simmer to reduce

### Step 4: First Fry - Blanching (6-8 minutes)
1. Heat oil to 325°F (163°C)
2. Working in batches, add dry fries
3. Fry 5-7 minutes (no browning, just cooking through)
4. Fries should be limp, pale, cooked but not crispy
5. Remove to paper towel-lined tray
6. Let cool 10 minutes (or up to 2 hours)
7. Can refrigerate at this point (see make-ahead)
8. 🚨 **FIRST FRY = COOKING, NOT CRISPING**

### Step 5: Second Fry - Crisping (3-5 minutes)
1. Heat oil to 375°F (190°C)
2. Working in batches, add blanched fries
3. Fry 3-5 minutes until deep golden and crispy
4. Should be crackling, bubbling
5. Remove to wire rack (not paper towels)
6. Season immediately with kosher salt
7. Keep warm while finishing batches
8. 🚨 **HIGH TEMP FOR CRISPY EXTERIOR**

### Step 6: Assemble (THE LOADED STEP)
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (204°C)
2. Pile all crispy fries on large oven-safe platter
3. **LAYER 1**: Drizzle cheese sauce all over (use about 2/3)
4. Place in oven 2-3 minutes (cheese melts, gets gooey)
5. **LAYER 2**: Remove from oven, sprinkle crispy bacon all over
6. **LAYER 3**: Sprinkle sliced green onions
7. Drizzle remaining cheese sauce on top
8. 🚨 **SERVE IMMEDIATELY** - clock is ticking

### Step 7: Serve
1. Serve on the platter (family style)
2. Ranch dressing in bowl alongside
3. Provide small plates
4. Dig in while hot and crispy
5. Best within 10 minutes of assembly

## Timing

**Total Time: 45 minutes**
- Prep potatoes: 10 min
- Cook bacon: 10 min
- Make cheese sauce: 10 min
- First fry (blanch): 8 min
- Cool: 10 min
- Second fry: 5 min
- Assemble and broil: 5 min

**Make-Ahead:**
- Blanch fries (first fry) up to 2 hours ahead
- Refrigerate until ready
- Second fry and assemble when ready to serve
- Cook bacon ahead, reheat to crisp
- Make cheese sauce ahead, reheat gently

## Troubleshooting

**Soggy fries:**
- Oil temp too low (need 375°F for second fry)
- Fries not dried before frying
- Topped too early (should serve immediately)
- Single fry method (need to double fry)

**Cheese sauce grainy:**
- Overheated after adding cheese (remove from heat first)
- Added cheese to boiling liquid (should be off heat)
- Used pre-shredded cheese (has anti-caking agents)

**Cheese sauce too thick:**
- Not enough milk
- Cooked too long
- Add more milk 1 tbsp at a time

**Fries not crispy:**
- Oil temp too low for second fry
- Crowded the pot (dropped temp)
- Not dried before frying
- Topped while fries still too hot from fryer

**Bacon chewy:**
- Didn't cook long enough
- Need crispy, almost burnt edges
- Should shatter when chopped

## Air Fryer Method

**If You Don't Want to Deep Fry:**
1. Cut potatoes as directed
2. Toss with 2 tbsp oil, salt
3. Air fry at 375°F for 20-25 minutes
4. Shake basket every 5 minutes
5. Should be golden and crispy
6. Top as directed
7. Less crispy than double-fried but still good

## Oven Method

**Baked Option:**
1. Cut potatoes as directed
2. Toss with 3 tbsp oil, salt
3. Spread on baking sheet (single layer)
4. Bake at 450°F for 30-35 minutes
5. Flip halfway through
6. Should be golden and crispy
7. Top as directed

## Storage and Reheating

**Not Ideal for Storage:**
- Best fresh
- Fries get soggy when reheated
- Toppings don't reheat well

**If Must Store:**
- Store fries separate from toppings
- Refrigerate up to 2 days
- Reheat fries in 400°F oven 10 minutes
- Re-top and serve

## Pro Tips

**Restaurant Secrets:**
- Chili's uses frozen pre-cut fries (you can too - saves time)
- Cheese sauce is made in batches, kept warm
- Bacon is pre-cooked, reheated when needed
- Fries are fried to order (takes 3-4 min)
- Assembled under heat lamp (melts cheese)
- Massive portions (part of value proposition)

**For Best Results:**
- Double fry method is mandatory (blanch then crisp)
- Keep first-fry batches warm in low oven
- Cheese sauce should be pourable but thick
- Bacon should be crispy, almost burnt
- Fresh green onions (not dried)
- Serve immediately (this is critical)
- Use russet potatoes (high starch, fluffy interior)

**Frozen Fries Shortcut:**
- Buy frozen steak fries
- Follow package directions
- Or double fry from frozen (325°F then 375°F)
- Saves time, still great results
