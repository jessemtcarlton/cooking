# 🌟 Buffalo Wild Wings Boneless Wings (Copycat)

## Overview
Crispy breaded chicken pieces tossed in your choice of signature sauces. These aren't traditional wings - they're chunks of chicken breast, breaded and fried until ultra-crispy, then tossed in sauce. This copycat recipe creates the exact crispy texture and includes recipes for BWW's most popular sauces.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **BUTTERMILK BRINE MANDATORY**: 2-4 hours minimum (tenderizes, adds flavor)
- **DOUBLE BREAD**: Flour → buttermilk → flour again (ultra-crispy coating)
- **OIL TEMP CRITICAL**: 350°F exactly - lower = greasy, higher = burnt
- **TOSS IN SAUCE AFTER FRYING**: Never before or coating gets soggy
- **SAUCE SHOULD BE WARM**: Cold sauce makes coating soggy
- **SERVE IMMEDIATELY**: Coating stays crispy 15-20 min max

## The Buffalo Wild Wings Secret
1. **Bite-sized chicken breast** (not wings - more tender)
2. **Buttermilk brine** (tenderizes, seasons)
3. **Seasoned flour coating** (garlic powder, paprika, cayenne)
4. **Double dredge** (creates ultra-thick crispy coating)
5. **Fried at 350°F** (perfect crisp without burning)
6. **Tossed in sauce** (coats completely, stays crispy)
7. **21 signature sauces** (we'll make top 5)

## Equipment
- Large bowl (for brining)
- Deep fryer or heavy pot (5-6 quart)
- Oil thermometer
- 3 shallow bowls (breading station)
- Spider strainer or slotted spoon
- Large bowl (for tossing with sauce)
- Paper towels and wire rack

## Ingredients (serves 4-6, makes about 30 pieces)

**For Boneless Wings:**
- 2 lbs (900g) boneless skinless chicken breasts
- 2 cups (480ml) buttermilk
- 1 tsp (6g) kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp (2g) black pepper
- 1/2 tsp (1.5g) garlic powder
- Oil for frying (vegetable or peanut, about 8 cups/2L)

**For Breading:**
- 2 cups (240g) all-purpose flour
- 1 tbsp (9g) garlic powder
- 1 tbsp (6g) paprika
- 2 tsp (6g) onion powder
- 1 tsp (6g) kosher salt
- 1 tsp (2g) black pepper
- 1 tsp (3g) cayenne pepper (adjust for heat)
- 1/2 tsp (1.5g) dried oregano

**For Buttermilk Dredge:**
- 1 cup (240ml) buttermilk
- 1 egg

## Method

### Step 1: Brine Chicken (2-4 hours ahead)
1. Cut 2 lbs (900g) chicken breast into bite-sized pieces:
   - About 1.5" chunks
   - Should be similar size for even cooking
2. In large bowl, combine:
   - 2 cups (480ml) buttermilk
   - 1 tsp (6g) salt
   - 1/2 tsp (2g) black pepper
   - 1/2 tsp (1.5g) garlic powder
3. Add chicken pieces
4. Stir to coat all pieces
5. Cover and refrigerate 2-4 hours (overnight is fine)
6. 🚨 **DON'T SKIP BRINE** - this is what makes them tender

### Step 2: Make Sauce (While chicken brines)
**Choose one or make multiple sauces:**

**For Buffalo Sauce:**
1. Melt 1/2 cup (113g) butter
2. Add 1 cup (240ml) Frank's RedHot
3. Add 1 tbsp (15ml) vinegar, garlic powder, cayenne
4. Whisk together, keep warm

**For Other Sauces:**
- Follow recipes above
- Keep warm (not hot)
- Make ahead and reheat before tossing

### Step 3: Set Up Breading Station

**Bowl 1 - Seasoned Flour:**
- 2 cups (240g) flour
- 1 tbsp (9g) garlic powder
- 1 tbsp (6g) paprika
- 2 tsp (6g) onion powder
- 1 tsp (6g) salt
- 1 tsp (2g) black pepper
- 1 tsp (3g) cayenne
- 1/2 tsp (1.5g) oregano
- Whisk together

**Bowl 2 - Buttermilk Dredge:**
- 1 cup (240ml) buttermilk
- 1 egg
- Whisk together

**Bowl 3 - More Seasoned Flour:**
- Same as Bowl 1 (or just refill as needed)

### Step 4: Bread Chicken (THE DOUBLE DREDGE)
1. Remove chicken from brine (discard brine)
2. Let excess buttermilk drip off
3. For each piece:
   - **First**: Dredge in seasoned flour (coat completely)
   - **Second**: Dip in buttermilk-egg mixture (coat completely)
   - **Third**: Dredge in seasoned flour again (press to adhere)
   - Place on wire rack
4. Let rest 10 minutes (coating sets)
5. 🚨 **DOUBLE FLOUR COAT** - this creates the crispy texture

### Step 5: Heat Oil
1. Heat oil to 350°F (177°C) in deep fryer or pot
2. Oil should be 3-4" deep
3. Use thermometer - temp is critical
4. While oil heats, finish breading chicken

### Step 6: Fry Chicken (6-8 minutes per batch)
1. Working in batches (don't crowd), add chicken
2. Fry 6-8 minutes
3. Should be:
   - Deep golden brown
   - Crispy coating
   - Internal temp 165°F (74°C)
4. Remove with spider strainer
5. Drain on wire rack (not paper towels)
6. Let oil return to 350°F before next batch
7. Keep cooked pieces warm in 200°F oven
8. 🚨 **DON'T CROWD** - fry 8-10 pieces at a time

### Step 7: Toss in Sauce (IMMEDIATELY BEFORE SERVING)
1. Heat sauce to warm (not boiling)
2. Place fried chicken in large bowl
3. Pour warm sauce over chicken
4. Toss gently to coat all pieces
5. Should be glossy, completely coated
6. Serve immediately
7. 🚨 **TOSS JUST BEFORE SERVING** - don't let sit or coating gets soggy

### Step 8: Serve
1. Transfer to serving plate or basket
2. Celery sticks alongside (traditional)
3. Carrot sticks
4. Ranch or blue cheese dressing for dipping
5. Extra sauce on side
6. Wet naps (it's messy!)

## Timing

**Total Time: 3 hours (most is passive brining)**
- Brine chicken: 2-4 hours (passive)
- Make sauce: 10 min
- Bread chicken: 15 min
- Heat oil: 10 min
- Fry: 20 min (in batches)
- Toss and serve: 5 min

**Make-Ahead:**
- Brine chicken overnight
- Bread chicken 1 hour ahead, refrigerate
- Make sauces days ahead, refrigerate, reheat
- Fry just before serving

## Troubleshooting

**Coating fell off:**
- Didn't let rest after breading (need 10 min)
- Oil temp too low (coating absorbed oil)
- Didn't press flour firmly
- Skipped double dredge

**Soggy coating:**
- Tossed in sauce too early (must be just before serving)
- Sauce too watery (should be thick)
- Oil temp too low
- Didn't drain on wire rack

**Dry chicken:**
- Didn't brine (mandatory step)
- Overcooked (should be 165°F, no higher)
- Chicken pieces too small

**Not crispy:**
- Single breading (need double dredge)
- Oil temp too low (need 350°F)
- Crowded the fryer
- Didn't let coating rest before frying

**Sauce not sticking:**
- Sauce too thin (reduce it)
- Chicken not hot enough (sauce won't adhere)
- Tossed too gently (need to coat thoroughly)

## Sauce Heat Levels (Buffalo Wild Wings Style)

**Mild:**
- Reduce cayenne to pinch in Buffalo sauce
- Perfect for kids

**Medium:**
- Recipe as written above
- Most popular

**Hot:**
- Double cayenne in Buffalo sauce
- Add 1 tbsp sriracha

**Blazin':**
- Add 2 tbsp cayenne
- Add 2 tbsp ghost pepper sauce
- WARNING: Very hot

## Storage and Reheating

**Before Frying:**
- Bread and refrigerate up to 4 hours
- Bring to room temp 15 min before frying

**After Frying (No Sauce):**
- Refrigerate up to 2 days
- Reheat in 400°F oven 10-12 minutes
- Will crisp up again
- Toss in sauce after reheating

**After Saucing:**
- Not ideal to store
- Coating gets soggy
- Best fresh

## Air Fryer Method

**Lower Fat Option:**
1. Bread chicken as directed
2. Spray with cooking spray
3. Air fry at 400°F for 12-15 minutes
4. Flip halfway through
5. Spray again after flipping
6. Toss in sauce and serve
7. Less greasy, still crispy
