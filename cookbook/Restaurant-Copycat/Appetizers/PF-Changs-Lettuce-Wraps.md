# 🌟 P.F. Chang's Chicken Lettuce Wraps (Copycat)

## Overview
One of the most iconic appetizers in casual dining. Minced chicken with water chestnuts, mushrooms, and Asian aromatics served in crisp lettuce cups. Fresh, flavorful, and interactive eating experience.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **MINCE, DON'T GRIND**: Hand-minced chicken has better texture than ground
- **HIGH HEAT ESSENTIAL**: Wok or skillet must be screaming hot for proper sear
- **WATER CHESTNUTS CRITICAL**: Fresh crunch is signature texture
- **CRISPY RICE NOODLES**: Must be fried (not baked) for authentic crunch
- **ICEBERG LETTUCE ONLY**: Butter lettuce tears, iceberg holds filling

## The P.F. Chang's Secret
1. **Minced chicken thighs** (not breast - more flavor, stays moist)
2. **Water chestnuts** (signature crunch)
3. **Hoisin-based sauce** (sweet-savory balance)
4. **Crispy rice sticks** (textural contrast)
5. **Iceberg lettuce cups** (sturdy, holds filling)
6. **Serve in lettuce** (interactive, hands-on eating)

## Equipment
- Wok or large skillet
- Sharp knife and cutting board
- Small pot (for frying rice noodles)
- Slotted spoon
- Mixing bowls

## Ingredients (serves 4-6 as appetizer)

**For Chicken Filling:**
- 1 lb (450g) boneless skinless chicken thighs
- 2 tbsp (30ml) vegetable oil
- 1 can (8 oz) water chestnuts, drained and finely diced
- 8 oz (225g) mushrooms, finely diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp (15g) fresh ginger, minced
- 3 green onions, chopped (whites and greens separated)
- 1/4 cup (35g) diced red bell pepper

**For Sauce:**
- 3 tbsp (45ml) hoisin sauce
- 2 tbsp (30ml) soy sauce
- 1 tbsp (15ml) rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp (15ml) Asian sesame oil
- 1 tsp (5g) sugar
- 1/2 tsp (2ml) sriracha or chili garlic sauce

**For Serving:**
- 1 head iceberg lettuce (leaves separated into cups)
- 2 oz rice stick noodles (vermicelli)
- Oil for frying noodles
- Optional: Additional hoisin sauce for drizzling

## Method

### Step 1: Prep Ingredients (Mise en Place)
1. **Mince chicken**:
   - Cut thighs into small dice
   - Then mince with knife (don't use food processor)
   - Should be texture of coarse ground meat
   - 🚨 Hand-mincing = better texture than pre-ground

2. **Prep vegetables**:
   - Dice water chestnuts finely (1/4" pieces)
   - Dice mushrooms finely
   - Mince garlic and ginger
   - Chop green onions (separate whites from greens)
   - Dice red bell pepper

3. **Make sauce**:
   - Whisk all sauce ingredients in small bowl
   - Set aside

### Step 2: Fry Crispy Rice Noodles
1. Heat 2" oil in small pot to 375°F (190°C)
2. Break rice noodles into small pieces
3. Drop small handful into hot oil
4. 🚨 **WILL PUFF INSTANTLY** - be ready
5. Fry 5-10 seconds until puffed and white
6. Remove with slotted spoon to paper towels
7. Repeat with remaining noodles
8. Set aside

### Step 3: Prepare Lettuce Cups
1. Remove core from iceberg lettuce
2. Carefully separate leaves
3. Select cup-shaped leaves (6-8 large cups)
4. Rinse, pat dry
5. Refrigerate until serving (keeps crisp)

### Step 4: Cook Chicken Filling (High Heat)
1. Heat wok or large skillet over HIGH heat
2. Add 2 tbsp (30ml) oil
3. When oil shimmers and smokes slightly, add chicken
4. Break up with spatula, cook 3-4 minutes
5. Should brown and get some crispy bits
6. Remove chicken to bowl

### Step 5: Cook Vegetables
1. Same pan, still on HIGH heat
2. Add mushrooms
3. Cook 2-3 minutes until liquid evaporates
4. Add water chestnuts
5. Cook 1 minute
6. Add garlic, ginger, green onion whites
7. Cook 30 seconds until fragrant
8. 🚨 **Don't burn garlic** - watch closely

### Step 6: Combine and Sauce
1. Return chicken to pan
2. Add sauce mixture
3. Add red bell pepper
4. Stir-fry 1-2 minutes
5. Sauce should coat everything
6. Add green onion greens
7. Toss to combine
8. Remove from heat

### Step 7: Serve
1. Transfer filling to serving bowl
2. Arrange lettuce cups on platter
3. Top filling with crispy rice noodles
4. Serve immediately with:
   - Lettuce cups on the side
   - Extra hoisin sauce for drizzling
   - Guests fill their own lettuce cups

## How to Eat (P.F. Chang's Style)
1. Take one lettuce cup
2. Spoon 2-3 tbsp filling into center
3. Top with crispy rice noodles
4. Optional: Drizzle with extra hoisin
5. Fold lettuce around filling (like a taco)
6. Eat with hands in 2-3 bites

## Perfect Lettuce Wrap Indicators
- **Filling**: Savory, slightly sweet, balanced flavors
- **Texture**: Crunchy water chestnuts, tender chicken, crispy noodles
- **Temperature**: Hot filling, cold crisp lettuce (contrast)
- **Moisture**: Not too wet (lettuce should hold up)
- **Aroma**: Garlic, ginger, hoisin, sesame

## Variations

### Vegetarian
- Replace chicken with extra-firm tofu, crumbled
- Or use minced mushrooms only

### Shrimp
- Use 1 lb minced shrimp instead of chicken
- Cook only 2-3 minutes (cooks faster)

### Spicy
- Increase sriracha to 1-2 tsp
- Add red pepper flakes

## Storage
- Refrigerate filling up to 3 days
- Reheat in skillet
- Fry rice noodles fresh when serving
- Lettuce cups don't store well

## Pro Tips
- Chicken thighs >>> breasts (more flavor, moisture)
- Hand-mince for best texture (food processor makes mushy)
- High heat is critical (develops flavor)
- Water chestnuts must be crunchy (fresh or canned whole, diced)
- Iceberg lettuce is structurally superior to butter lettuce
- Make crispy noodles ahead, store airtight
