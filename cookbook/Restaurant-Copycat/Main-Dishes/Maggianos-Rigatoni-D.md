# 🌟 Maggiano's Rigatoni "D" (Copycat)

## Overview
The secret menu item that Maggiano's regulars order - rigatoni tossed with Italian sausage, mushrooms, red peppers, and spicy tomato cream sauce. Rich, creamy, with a kick of heat. This dish isn't on the regular menu, but it's legendary among those who know. This copycat creates the exact creamy-spicy sauce.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **ITALIAN SAUSAGE CASINGS**: Remove from casings before cooking (crumble as you cook)
- **CREAM ADDED LAST**: Add to tomato sauce, don't boil or it may break
- **RED PEPPER FLAKES**: Start with less, add more (everyone's heat tolerance differs)
- **PASTA AL DENTE**: Cook 1 minute less than package (finishes in sauce)
- **RESERVE PASTA WATER**: Critical for sauce consistency
- **PARMIGIANO-REGGIANO**: Real Parmesan, freshly grated (not green can)

## Equipment
- Large pot (for pasta)
- Large deep skillet or sauté pan (12")
- Wooden spoon
- Colander
- Grater (for Parmesan)
- Measuring cups

## Ingredients (serves 4-6)

**For Sauce:**
- 1 lb (450g) spicy Italian sausage (remove from casings)
- 2 tbsp (30ml) olive oil
- 8 oz (225g) mushrooms (cremini or white button), sliced
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 cup (75g) roasted red peppers (jarred is fine), sliced
- 1 can (28 oz/794g) crushed tomatoes (San Marzano if possible)
- 1/2 tsp (3g) kosher salt (plus more to taste)
- 1/2 tsp (1g) black pepper
- 1 tsp (2g) red pepper flakes (adjust for heat preference)
- 1 tsp (2g) dried oregano
- 1 tsp (2g) dried basil
- 1 cup (240ml) heavy cream
- 1/2 cup (50g) freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano, plus more for serving

**For Pasta:**
- 1 lb (450g) rigatoni pasta
- 1 tbsp (18g) salt (for pasta water)
- 1/2 cup (120ml) reserved pasta water

**For Serving:**
- Fresh basil leaves, torn
- Extra Parmigiano-Reggiano, grated
- Red pepper flakes (for those who want extra heat)
- Crusty Italian bread

## Method

### Step 1: Boil Pasta Water
1. Fill large pot with water
2. Add 1 tbsp (18g) salt
3. Bring to rolling boil
4. Don't add pasta yet (add when sauce almost done)

### Step 2: Brown Sausage (10 minutes)
1. In large deep skillet over MEDIUM-HIGH heat:
2. Add 1 tbsp (15ml) olive oil
3. Add 1 lb (450g) Italian sausage (removed from casings)
4. Break up with wooden spoon as it cooks
5. Cook 8-10 minutes until browned
6. Should be crumbly, no longer pink
7. Remove sausage with slotted spoon
8. Leave fat in pan (flavor)
9. Set sausage aside

### Step 3: Sauté Vegetables (8-10 minutes)
1. In same pan with sausage fat:
2. Add 1 tbsp (15ml) olive oil if pan is dry
3. Add diced onion
4. Sauté 3-4 minutes until softened
5. Add sliced mushrooms
6. Sauté 4-5 minutes until mushrooms release liquid and brown
7. Add minced garlic
8. Sauté 1 minute until fragrant
9. Add sliced roasted red peppers
10. Stir to combine
11. 🚨 **DON'T BURN GARLIC** - add last, cook briefly

### Step 4: Build Tomato Sauce (15 minutes)
1. Return sausage to pan
2. Add:
   - 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
   - 1/2 tsp (3g) salt
   - 1/2 tsp (1g) black pepper
   - 1 tsp (2g) red pepper flakes (adjust to taste)
   - 1 tsp (2g) dried oregano
   - 1 tsp (2g) dried basil
3. Stir to combine
4. Bring to simmer
5. Reduce heat to MEDIUM-LOW
6. Simmer 10-15 minutes
7. Sauce should thicken slightly
8. Stir occasionally

**When Sauce is Simmering, Start Pasta:**
1. Add 1 lb (450g) rigatoni to boiling water
2. Cook 1 minute LESS than package directions (usually 10-11 min)
3. Before draining, reserve 1/2 cup (120ml) pasta water
4. Drain pasta (don't rinse)

### Step 5: Add Cream (THE "D" STEP)
1. Reduce heat to LOW
2. Stir in 1 cup (240ml) heavy cream
3. Stir until fully incorporated
4. Simmer gently 2-3 minutes (don't boil)
5. Should be creamy, pink-orange color
6. Stir in 1/2 cup (50g) grated Parmigiano-Reggiano
7. Stir until cheese melted
8. Taste and adjust:
   - More salt if needed
   - More red pepper flakes for heat
   - More Parmesan for richness
9. 🚨 **LOW HEAT** - high heat can break cream sauce

### Step 6: Combine Pasta and Sauce
1. Add drained rigatoni directly to sauce
2. Toss to coat completely
3. If sauce too thick, add pasta water 2 tbsp at a time
4. Sauce should cling to pasta and coat ridges
5. Toss for 1-2 minutes (pasta finishes cooking)
6. Should be creamy, every piece coated

### Step 7: Serve
1. Transfer to serving bowls or plates
2. Top with torn fresh basil
3. Grate extra Parmigiano-Reggiano on top
4. Sprinkle red pepper flakes for those who want heat
5. Serve immediately with crusty bread
6. 🚨 **SERVE HOT** - cream sauce thickens as it cools

- Sauté vegetables: 10 min
- Simmer sauce: 15 min

- Add cream when reheating
- Cook pasta just before serving

## Troubleshooting

**Too spicy:**
- Reduce red pepper flakes to 1/2 tsp
- Add more cream
- Serve with bread to cool palate
- Can't remove heat once added (start conservative)

**Not spicy enough:**
- Add more red pepper flakes
- Use extra spicy sausage
- Add cayenne pepper
- Pass red pepper flakes at table

**Sauce too thick:**
- Add pasta water 2 tbsp at a time
- Or add more cream
- Stir well to combine

**Sauce too thin:**
- Simmer longer before adding cream
- Reduce cream amount
- Add more Parmesan (thickens and adds flavor)

**Cream sauce broke (oily):**
- Heat too high after adding cream
- Reduce heat, whisk vigorously
- Add splash of pasta water, whisk
- Prevention: LOW heat after cream added

**Bland flavor:**
- Not enough salt (cream dulls flavors)
- Taste and add more salt
- Add more Parmesan
- Use spicy sausage (not mild)

## Variations

### Spicier "D"
- Increase red pepper flakes to 2 tsp
- Use extra spicy Italian sausage
- Add diced jalapeños

### Lighter Version
- Use half-and-half instead of heavy cream
- Reduce cream to 1/2 cup
- Still creamy but less rich

### Add Spinach
- Add 4 cups fresh spinach at end
- Wilts into sauce
- Adds color and nutrition

### Chicken Instead of Sausage
- Use 1 lb diced chicken breast or thighs
- Season with Italian seasoning
- Brown before adding vegetables

### Vodka Sauce Style
- Add 1/4 cup vodka after vegetables sauté
- Let alcohol cook off 2 minutes
- Then add tomatoes
- More complex flavor

## Storage and Reheating

**Refrigerator:**
- Store in airtight container up to 3 days
- Sauce will thicken (normal)

**Reheating:**
- Stovetop: Heat in skillet with splash of cream or pasta water
- Stir constantly over low heat
- Microwave: Heat in 1-minute intervals, stirring
- Add liquid to thin if needed

**Freezing:**
- Not ideal (cream sauces can separate)
- If must freeze: freeze sauce only (no pasta)
- Thaw in fridge, reheat gently, add to fresh pasta

**For Best Results:**

**Heat Level Adjustment:**
- **Mild**: 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes, use mild sausage
- **Medium**: 1 tsp red pepper flakes, spicy sausage (as written)
- **Hot**: 2 tsp red pepper flakes, extra spicy sausage, add cayenne
- **Molto Fuoco** (very fire): Double red pepper, add fresh chili peppers
