# 🌟 Chick-fil-A Chicken Sandwich (Copycat)

## Overview
America's #1 chicken sandwich. Pressure-fried pickle-brined chicken breast on a buttered toasted bun with pickles. Simple but executed perfectly. This copycat replicates the exact texture, flavor, and experience.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **PICKLE JUICE BRINE MANDATORY**: 2-4 hours minimum (overnight best)
- **PRESSURE FRYING IDEAL**: Regular frying works but pressure fryer = authentic texture
- **PEANUT OIL**: Chick-fil-A uses 100% refined peanut oil (signature flavor)
- **BUTTER THE BUN**: Must butter and toast - non-negotiable
- **BREAST THICKNESS**: Pound to even 1/2" or won't cook evenly

## The Chick-fil-A Secret
1. **Pickle juice brine** (tenderizes, seasons, adds tang)
2. **Pressure frying** (cooks fast, stays juicy, ultra-crispy)
3. **Peanut oil** (signature flavor, high smoke point)
4. **Milk wash** (helps breading adhere)
5. **Seasoned breading** (flour with spices)
6. **Buttered toasted bun** (soft, slightly sweet, buttery)

## Equipment
- Pressure fryer (or regular deep pot/fryer)
- Meat mallet
- Mixing bowls
- Instant-read thermometer
- Wire rack

## Ingredients (serves 4)

**For Brining:**
- 4 boneless skinless chicken breasts (6-8 oz each)
- 1 cup (240ml) dill pickle juice (from jar of pickles)
- 1 cup (240ml) water
- 2 tbsp (30g) sugar

**For Breading:**
- 1.5 cups (180g) all-purpose flour
- 2 tbsp (16g) powdered sugar
- 1 tsp (6g) kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp (2g) black pepper
- 1/2 tsp (2g) paprika
- 1/4 tsp (1g) garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp (1g) onion powder
- 1/8 tsp cayenne pepper (optional)

**For Milk Wash:**
- 1 cup (240ml) whole milk
- 1 egg

**For Frying:**
- Peanut oil for frying (about 8 cups)

**For Assembly:**
- 4 soft hamburger buns (potato buns ideal)
- 2 tbsp (28g) butter, softened
- Dill pickle chips (2-3 per sandwich)

## Method

### Step 1: Brine Chicken (2-24 hours ahead)
1. Pound chicken breasts to even 1/2" thickness
   - Place between plastic wrap
   - Pound with flat side of mallet
   - Even thickness = even cooking
2. Mix brine:
   - 1 cup (240ml) pickle juice
   - 1 cup (240ml) water
   - 2 tbsp (30g) sugar
3. Submerge chicken in brine
4. Refrigerate:
   - Minimum: 2 hours
   - Ideal: 4 hours
   - Maximum: 24 hours (overnight)
5. 🚨 **Brine is critical** - tenderizes and seasons

### Step 2: Prep Breading Station
1. **Bowl 1 - Milk Wash:**
   - 1 cup (240ml) milk
   - 1 egg
   - Whisk together

2. **Bowl 2 - Seasoned Flour:**
   - 1.5 cups (180g) flour
   - 2 tbsp (16g) powdered sugar
   - All spices
   - Whisk to combine

### Step 3: Heat Oil
**If Using Pressure Fryer:**
- Heat peanut oil to 375°F (190°C)
- Follow pressure fryer instructions

**If Using Regular Pot/Fryer:**
- Heat 3-4" peanut oil to 350°F (177°C)
- Use thermometer - temp critical

### Step 4: Bread Chicken
1. Remove chicken from brine
2. Pat dry with paper towels
3. For each piece:
   - Dip in milk wash (coat completely)
   - Let excess drip off
   - Dredge in seasoned flour
   - Press flour into chicken
   - Shake off excess
   - Place on wire rack
4. Let breaded chicken rest 5 minutes (coating adheres)

### Step 5: Fry Chicken

**Pressure Frying (Authentic Method):**
- 3-4 minutes at 375°F under pressure
- Follow equipment instructions
- Results in ultra-juicy, crispy exterior

**Regular Frying:**
- Fry at 350°F (177°C) for 6-8 minutes
- Flip halfway through
- Golden brown, crispy
- Internal temp 165°F (74°C)
- Remove to wire rack
- 🚨 Don't crowd - fry in batches

### Step 6: Toast Buns
1. While chicken rests, butter buns
2. Spread softened butter on cut sides
3. Toast in skillet or on griddle
4. Medium heat, 1-2 minutes
5. Should be golden and warm

### Step 7: Assemble Sandwiches
**Chick-fil-A Assembly:**
1. Bottom bun
2. 2-3 pickle chips
3. Hot fried chicken breast
4. Top bun
5. That's it - simplicity is key

**Serve immediately** while chicken is hot and crispy

## Perfect Chick-fil-A Sandwich Indicators
- **Coating**: Golden, crispy, adheres to chicken
- **Chicken**: Juicy (165°F), tender, slight tang from brine
- **Bun**: Soft, buttery, toasted
- **Pickles**: Crisp, tangy
- **Overall**: Simple, perfectly executed

## Variations

### Deluxe
- Add lettuce, tomato
- Add Chick-fil-A sauce (recipe below)

### Chick-fil-A Sauce (Copycat)
Mix together:
- 1/2 cup (120ml) mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp (30ml) honey
- 1 tbsp (15ml) yellow mustard
- 2 tsp (10ml) lemon juice
- 2 tsp (10ml) BBQ sauce
- 1/2 tsp (2ml) liquid smoke (optional)

## Storage
- Best fresh (within 30 min of frying)
- Refrigerate leftovers up to 2 days
- Reheat in 400°F oven 10 min (crisps coating)
- Don't microwave (coating gets soggy)

## Pro Tips

**Restaurant Secrets:**
- Chick-fil-A uses pressure fryers (3-4 min cook time)
- 100% refined peanut oil (allergen-free, signature flavor)
- Pickle juice brine is the secret weapon
- Buns are specially made (soft potato-style)
- Simple assembly (pickles only) lets chicken shine
- Closed Sundays (gives staff rest)

**For Best Results:**
- Brine overnight (best flavor/tenderness)
- Peanut oil mandatory (canola is acceptable substitute)
- Even thickness critical (pound to 1/2")
- Don't skip buttering and toasting bun
- Serve immediately (coating stays crispy 30 min max)
- If no pressure fryer, regular frying works (just not quite as juicy)

**Pressure Fryer vs Regular:**
- Pressure: 3-4 min, ultra-juicy, professional
- Regular: 6-8 min, still great, accessible

## Why This Recipe Works

**Science:**
- Pickle juice (acid) breaks down proteins (tender chicken)
- Salt in brine seasons throughout
- Milk + egg creates protein bond (coating adheres)
- Powdered sugar in breading aids browning
- Pressure frying cooks fast under steam (juicy)
- Peanut oil has neutral-nutty flavor, high smoke point

**Simplicity:**
- Only pickles on sandwich (chicken is the star)
- Perfect execution of simple ingredients
- Quality over complexity

This is why people wait in line at Chick-fil-A. Simple, perfectly executed chicken sandwich. Make these at home and you'll have them whenever you want (even on Sundays!).
