# 🌟 In-N-Out Animal Style Burger (Copycat)

## Overview
The legendary West Coast burger with caramelized onions, extra spread, pickles, and grilled mustard. "Animal Style" is In-N-Out's secret menu item - a regular burger transformed into something magical. Crispy beef patty, gooey cheese, tangy spread, sweet caramelized onions. This is burger perfection.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **THIN PATTIES**: 1/4 lb max, pressed thin (cooks fast, crispy edges)
- **DON'T PRESS WHILE COOKING**: Press at start only, then hands off
- **MUSTARD ON GRIDDLE**: Smear mustard on raw patty, place mustard-side down (grilled mustard)
- **AMERICAN CHEESE ONLY**: Real American cheese, not cheddar (melts perfectly)
- **CARAMELIZE ONIONS LOW AND SLOW**: 20-30 min, deep golden (the secret)
- **TOAST BUNS ON GRIDDLE**: Use burger fat to toast (adds flavor)

## The In-N-Out Secret
1. **Fresh beef** (never frozen, ground daily)
2. **Smashed thin** (1/4" thick on griddle)
3. **Grilled mustard** (mustard on patty, grilled into meat)
4. **Caramelized onions** (cooked low and slow until golden)
5. **Thousand Island spread** (mayo-based, sweet, tangy)
6. **American cheese** (melts smooth and gooey)
7. **Butter-toasted buns** (grilled in beef fat)
8. **Fresh vegetables** (crisp lettuce, tomato, pickle)

## Equipment
- Griddle or large cast iron skillet
- Metal spatula (sturdy)
- Small saucepan (for onions)
- Burger press or heavy spatula (for smashing)
- Sharp knife

## Ingredients (makes 4 burgers)

**For Patties:**
- 1 lb (450g) ground beef (80/20 blend - need fat for flavor)
- Salt and black pepper
- 8 tsp (40ml) yellow mustard (2 tsp per patty)

**For Spread:**
- 1/2 cup (120ml) mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp (30ml) ketchup
- 2 tbsp (30ml) sweet pickle relish
- 1 tsp (5ml) white vinegar
- 1/2 tsp (2.5g) sugar
- Pinch of salt

**For Caramelized Onions:**
- 2 large yellow onions, sliced thin
- 2 tbsp (28g) butter
- Pinch of salt
- Pinch of sugar

**For Assembly:**
- 4 hamburger buns (squishy white buns, not artisan)
- 4 slices American cheese (real deli American, not Kraft singles if possible)
- 4 leaves iceberg lettuce
- 1 large tomato, sliced (4 slices, 1/4" thick)
- 8-12 dill pickle slices
- Extra spread for buns

## Method

### Step 1: Make the Spread (Can make days ahead)
1. In bowl, mix:
   - 1/2 cup (120ml) mayo
   - 2 tbsp (30ml) ketchup
   - 2 tbsp (30ml) sweet pickle relish
   - 1 tsp (5ml) white vinegar
   - 1/2 tsp (2.5g) sugar
   - Pinch salt
2. Whisk until smooth
3. Taste and adjust (should be tangy, slightly sweet)
4. Refrigerate until needed (up to 1 week)

### Step 2: Caramelize Onions (20-30 minutes)

**The Animal Style Secret:**
1. Slice 2 large onions into thin half-moons
2. In saucepan over MEDIUM-LOW heat:
3. Melt 2 tbsp (28g) butter
4. Add sliced onions
5. Add pinch of salt and sugar
6. Cook 20-30 minutes, stirring occasionally
7. Should be:
   - Deep golden brown (almost mahogany)
   - Very soft and jammy
   - Sweet and caramelized
8. Don't rush (low and slow is key)
9. 🚨 **PATIENCE** - this is what makes Animal Style special

### Step 3: Prep Burger Components

**Portion Beef:**
1. Divide 1 lb (450g) beef into 4 equal portions (4 oz/113g each)
2. Form into loose balls (don't compress)
3. Keep refrigerated until cooking

**Prep Vegetables:**
1. Wash and dry lettuce leaves
2. Slice tomato into 4 thick slices
3. Have pickle slices ready

### Step 4: Cook Burgers (THE IN-N-OUT WAY)

**Heat Griddle:**
1. Heat griddle or cast iron to MEDIUM-HIGH
2. Should be very hot but not smoking

**Cook Each Patty:**
1. Place beef ball on hot griddle
2. Immediately SMASH flat with spatula or press
3. Press to 1/4" thick (thin patty)
4. Season top with salt and pepper
5. Spread 2 tsp yellow mustard on top of raw patty
6. Cook 2 minutes (don't touch, don't press)
7. **FLIP** (mustard side now on griddle)
8. Season cooked side with salt and pepper
9. Immediately place American cheese on top
10. Cook 2 more minutes
11. Cheese melts, mustard caramelizes on griddle
12. Remove to plate
13. 🚨 **ONLY PRESS AT START** - then hands off

### Step 5: Toast Buns
1. While last patties cook, slice buns
2. Place buns cut-side down on griddle
3. Toast in burger fat 30-60 seconds
4. Should be golden and slightly crispy
5. Remove when golden

### Step 6: Assemble Animal Style (THE RIGHT ORDER)

**Bottom Bun:**
1. Spread generous amount of spread on bottom bun
2. Add 2-3 pickle slices

**First Layer:**
1. Add cheese-topped patty
2. Top patty with generous heap of caramelized onions
3. 🚨 **LOTS OF ONIONS** - don't be shy

**Second Layer:**
1. Add tomato slice
2. Add lettuce leaf

**Top Bun:**
1. Spread generous amount of spread on top bun
2. Place on top
3. Press down gently

**Repeat for All 4 Burgers**

### Step 7: Serve Immediately
1. Serve hot
2. Wrap in paper if desired (In-N-Out style)
3. With fries or onion rings
4. 🚨 **BEST EATEN IMMEDIATELY** - onions and spread make bun soggy over time

## Perfect Animal Style Burger Indicators
- **Patty**: Thin, crispy edges, juicy center
- **Cheese**: Melted, gooey, covering patty
- **Onions**: Deep golden, sweet, jammy (not raw or pale)
- **Spread**: Generous, tangy, coating both buns
- **Mustard**: Grilled into patty (caramelized flavor)
- **Bun**: Soft, lightly toasted, holds together
- **Messy**: Should be messy to eat (that's the point)

## Troubleshooting

**Burgers too thick:**
- Didn't smash thin enough (should be 1/4" max)
- Used too much meat (4 oz per patty max)
- Pressed too gently (really smash it)

**Dry burgers:**
- Used lean beef (need 80/20 for moisture)
- Overcooked (should be 4 min total)
- Pressed while cooking (squeezes out juice)

**Pale onions:**
- Heat too high (burned instead of caramelized)
- Didn't cook long enough (need 20-30 min)
- Rushed the process (must be low and slow)

**Soggy bun:**
- Too much spread
- Didn't toast bun
- Let burger sit too long before eating

**Mustard flavor weak:**
- Didn't use enough mustard (2 tsp per patty)
- Flipped too early (need to grill mustard side)
- Didn't press mustard onto griddle

## Storage and Reheating

**Not Ideal:**
- Burgers don't store well (best fresh)
- Spread stores 1 week refrigerated
- Caramelized onions store 3 days refrigerated

**If Must Store:**
- Store components separately
- Refrigerate patties up to 2 days
- Reheat patties in skillet
- Assemble fresh

## Pro Tips

**Restaurant Secrets:**
- In-N-Out grinds beef fresh daily (never frozen)
- Burgers cooked to order (nothing pre-made)
- Onions caramelized in batches throughout day
- Spread made fresh daily
- Vegetables delivered fresh daily
- Mustard grilled directly on griddle (signature move)
- Buns toasted in beef fat (adds flavor)

**For Best Results:**
- Use 80/20 ground beef (need fat for flavor)
- Smash patties thin (1/4" max)
- Don't press after initial smash
- Caramelize onions low and slow (20-30 min)
- Use real deli American cheese
- Toast buns in beef fat on griddle
- Generous spread on both buns
- Serve immediately (don't let sit)

**Griddle vs Pan:**
- Griddle is ideal (flat, even heat)
- Cast iron skillet works great
- Need high heat for crispy edges
- Don't use grill (need flat surface to smash)
