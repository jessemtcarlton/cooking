# 🌟 Olive Garden Chicken Alfredo (Copycat)

## Overview
Creamy, rich Alfredo sauce coating fettuccine pasta, topped with tender grilled chicken. This is the dish that made Olive Garden famous - simple, comforting, indulgent. This copycat creates the exact creamy, garlicky sauce that people crave.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **BUTTER AND CREAM ONLY**: No flour roux - authentic Alfredo is butter, cream, Parmesan
- **PARMIGIANO-REGGIANO MANDATORY**: Real imported Parmesan, freshly grated (not green can)
- **LOW HEAT FOR SAUCE**: High heat = broken sauce (oil separates)
- **PASTA WATER IS GOLD**: Reserve 1 cup - it binds the sauce
- **TOSS, DON'T POUR**: Mix pasta with sauce in pan, don't pour sauce on top
- **SERVE IMMEDIATELY**: Alfredo sauce thickens as it cools

## The Olive Garden Secret
1. **Heavy cream base** (no milk, no flour)
2. **Fresh Parmigiano-Reggiano** (aged, nutty, melts smooth)
3. **Butter** (adds richness, helps emulsify)
4. **Fresh garlic** (not powder - aromatic depth)
5. **Pasta water** (starchy water binds sauce to pasta)
6. **Grilled chicken** (seasoned, sliced on bias)
7. **Served immediately** (sauce is best fresh)

## Equipment
- Large pot (for pasta)
- Large skillet or sauté pan (for sauce)
- Grill pan or regular skillet (for chicken)
- Whisk
- Tongs
- Grater (for Parmesan)
- Meat thermometer

## Ingredients (serves 4)

**For Chicken:**
- 2 large chicken breasts (about 1.5 lbs/680g)
- 2 tbsp (30ml) olive oil
- 1 tsp (3g) Italian seasoning
- 1/2 tsp (3g) kosher salt
- 1/4 tsp (0.5g) black pepper
- 1/2 tsp (1.5g) garlic powder

**For Alfredo Sauce:**
- 4 tbsp (56g) butter
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 cups (480ml) heavy cream (NOT half-and-half)
- 1 1/2 cups (150g) freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano
- 1/2 tsp (3g) kosher salt
- 1/4 tsp (0.5g) black pepper
- 1/4 tsp (0.5g) nutmeg (optional, traditional)
- 1/2 cup (120ml) reserved pasta water

**For Pasta:**
- 1 lb (450g) fettuccine pasta
- 1 tbsp (18g) salt (for pasta water)

**For Serving:**
- Fresh parsley, chopped
- Extra Parmigiano-Reggiano, grated
- Black pepper

## Method

### Step 1: Marinate and Cook Chicken (20 minutes)

**Marinate:**
1. Pound chicken breasts to even thickness (about 3/4")
2. In bowl, combine:
   - 2 tbsp (30ml) olive oil
   - 1 tsp Italian seasoning
   - 1/2 tsp salt
   - 1/4 tsp black pepper
   - 1/2 tsp garlic powder
3. Coat chicken completely
4. Let sit 15 minutes (or up to 4 hours refrigerated)

**Grill:**
1. Heat grill pan or skillet over medium-high
2. Add chicken breasts
3. Cook 6-7 minutes first side (don't move)
4. Flip, cook 6-7 minutes second side
5. Internal temp should reach 165°F (74°C)
6. Remove to cutting board
7. Let rest 5 minutes
8. Slice on bias into 1/2" strips
9. Keep warm
10. 🚨 **DON'T OVERCOOK** - dry chicken ruins the dish

### Step 2: Boil Pasta (While chicken cooks)
1. Bring large pot of water to boil
2. Add 1 tbsp (18g) salt (water should taste like sea)
3. Add 1 lb (450g) fettuccine
4. Cook according to package (usually 10-12 minutes)
5. **Cook 1 minute LESS than package says** (will finish in sauce)
6. Before draining, reserve 1 cup (240ml) pasta water
7. Drain pasta (don't rinse)
8. 🚨 **RESERVE PASTA WATER** - critical for sauce

### Step 3: Make Alfredo Sauce (10 minutes - THE CRITICAL STEP)

**Sauté Garlic:**
1. In large skillet over MEDIUM-LOW heat:
2. Melt 4 tbsp (56g) butter
3. Add 4 cloves minced garlic
4. Sauté 1-2 minutes (just until fragrant, not brown)
5. 🚨 **DON'T BURN GARLIC** - bitter flavor

**Add Cream:**
1. Pour in 2 cups (480ml) heavy cream
2. Whisk to combine with butter and garlic
3. Bring to gentle simmer (small bubbles at edge)
4. Simmer 5 minutes, whisking occasionally
5. Should reduce slightly and thicken
6. 🚨 **LOW HEAT** - don't boil hard or cream may break

**Add Cheese:**
1. Reduce heat to LOW
2. Add 1 1/2 cups (150g) grated Parmigiano-Reggiano
3. Add gradually, handful at a time
4. Whisk constantly as cheese melts
5. Wait for each addition to melt before adding more
6. Add 1/2 tsp salt, 1/4 tsp pepper, 1/4 tsp nutmeg
7. Sauce should be smooth, creamy, coat spoon
8. If too thick, add pasta water 2 tbsp at a time
9. 🚨 **LOW HEAT FOR CHEESE** - high heat = grainy sauce

### Step 4: Combine Pasta and Sauce (THE RIGHT WAY)
1. Add drained fettuccine directly to sauce pan
2. Toss with tongs to coat completely
3. Add pasta water 1/4 cup at a time if needed
4. Sauce should cling to pasta (not pool at bottom)
5. Toss for 1-2 minutes (pasta finishes cooking)
6. Should be creamy, silky, coating every strand
7. 🚨 **TOSS IN PAN** - don't pour sauce on top

### Step 5: Plate and Serve
1. Use tongs to twirl pasta into serving bowls
2. Top with sliced grilled chicken
3. Spoon extra sauce over chicken
4. Garnish with fresh parsley
5. Grate extra Parmesan on top
6. Crack black pepper over top
7. Serve immediately
8. 🚨 **SERVE HOT** - sauce thickens as it cools

## Perfect Alfredo Indicators
- **Sauce**: Silky, creamy, coats pasta (not watery or broken)
- **Color**: Ivory white, glossy
- **Consistency**: Thick enough to cling, thin enough to flow
- **Flavor**: Rich, buttery, cheesy, garlicky (not bland)
- **Pasta**: Al dente, completely coated
- **Chicken**: Tender, juicy, well-seasoned

## Restaurant Serving Style

- Pasta twirled in center
- Chicken sliced on top
- Parsley garnish
- Extra Parmesan and pepper mill at table
- Olive Garden breadsticks alongside
- Salad before meal

## Timing

**Total Time: 30 minutes**
- Marinate chicken: 15 min (can do ahead)
- Cook chicken: 12-14 min
- Boil pasta: 10-12 min (concurrent with chicken)
- Make sauce: 10 min
- Combine and serve: 5 min

**Can Do Ahead:**
- Marinate chicken up to 4 hours
- Grill chicken up to 2 hours ahead, slice when ready
- Grate Parmesan ahead
- Make sauce just before serving (doesn't hold well)

## Troubleshooting

**Sauce broke (oil separated):**
- Heat too high (must be low)
- Added cheese too fast
- Used pre-grated Parmesan (has anti-caking agents)
- Fix: Add 2 tbsp pasta water, whisk vigorously off heat

**Sauce too thick:**
- Add pasta water 2 tbsp at a time
- Whisk to combine
- Should be pourable

**Sauce too thin:**
- Simmer longer to reduce cream
- Add more Parmesan
- Let sit 2 minutes (thickens as cools)

**Bland flavor:**
- Not enough salt (Parmesan is salty but need more)
- Used cheap Parmesan (MUST use Parmigiano-Reggiano)
- Not enough garlic
- Taste and adjust

**Grainy sauce:**
- Heat too high when adding cheese
- Used pre-grated Parmesan
- Added cheese too fast
- Boiled instead of simmered

**Dry chicken:**
- Overcooked (should be 165°F, no higher)
- Chicken too thick (pound to even thickness)
- Didn't let rest before slicing

## Variations

### Shrimp Alfredo
- Substitute 1 lb (450g) large shrimp for chicken
- Sauté in butter 2-3 min per side
- Add to pasta

### Cajun Chicken Alfredo
- Season chicken with Cajun seasoning instead of Italian
- Add 1 tsp Cajun seasoning to sauce
- Spicy kick

### Broccoli Chicken Alfredo
- Add 2 cups steamed broccoli florets
- Toss with pasta and sauce
- Classic addition

### Vegetable Alfredo
- Skip chicken
- Add sautéed mushrooms, broccoli, bell peppers
- Vegetarian option

### Lighter Alfredo
- Use half heavy cream, half whole milk
- Reduce butter to 2 tbsp
- Still creamy but less rich

## Storage and Reheating

**Refrigerator:**
- Store pasta and chicken together up to 3 days
- Sauce will thicken (this is normal)

**Reheating:**
- Add to skillet with 1/4 cup cream or milk
- Heat gently over low heat
- Stir constantly
- Add pasta water or cream to thin
- Microwave works but sauce may break

**Freezing:**
- Not recommended (cream sauce breaks when frozen)
- Chicken can be frozen separately

## Pro Tips

**Restaurant Secrets:**
- Olive Garden uses heavy cream (never milk)
- Fresh Parmigiano-Reggiano delivered weekly
- Sauce made to order (not pre-made)
- Pasta cooked fresh for each order
- Chicken grilled on actual grill (char marks)
- Always served immediately (never held)

**For Best Results:**
- Use real Parmigiano-Reggiano (not domestic Parmesan)
- Grate cheese yourself (pre-grated won't melt smooth)
- Heavy cream only (not half-and-half or milk)
- Low heat for sauce (patient cooking = smooth sauce)
- Reserve pasta water (it's magic for sauce consistency)
- Toss pasta in sauce pan (not plate assembly)
- Serve immediately (sauce doesn't hold)

**Pasta Water Science:**
- Starchy water helps sauce cling to pasta
- Acts as emulsifier (keeps fat and water together)
- Adjust sauce consistency without diluting flavor
- Add gradually - can always add more, can't take away
