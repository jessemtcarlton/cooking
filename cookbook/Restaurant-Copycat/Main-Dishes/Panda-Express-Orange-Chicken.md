# 🌟 Panda Express Orange Chicken (Copycat)

## Overview
The most popular dish at Panda Express. Crispy battered chicken in sweet-tangy orange sauce. This copycat recipe replicates the exact texture (crispy yet tender) and flavor (sweet, citrusy, slightly spicy).

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **DOUBLE-COAT CHICKEN**: Batter twice for maximum crispiness
- **OIL TEMP 350°F**: Too low = soggy, too high = burnt coating/raw inside
- **SAUCE AFTER FRYING**: Toss in sauce just before serving or coating gets soggy
- **ORANGE ZEST ESSENTIAL**: Fresh orange zest = authentic flavor
- **CORNSTARCH SLURRY**: Must add at end to thicken sauce properly

## The Panda Express Secret
1. **Egg-based batter** (creates crispy shell that holds sauce)
2. **Double-coating technique** (extra crispy exterior)
3. **Fresh orange zest** (bright citrus flavor, not just juice)
4. **Sweet-spicy balance** (sugar + vinegar + red pepper)
5. **Cornstarch slurry** (glossy thick sauce that coats)
6. **Toss just before serving** (keeps coating crispy)

## Equipment
- Deep heavy pot or fryer
- Oil thermometer
- Whisk and mixing bowls
- Spider strainer or slotted spoon
- Wire rack over baking sheet
- Wok or large skillet (for sauce)

## Ingredients (serves 4)

**For Chicken:**
- 1.5 lbs (680g) boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into 1" pieces
- 2 eggs
- 1/4 cup (60ml) water
- 1.5 cups (180g) cornstarch, divided
- 1/4 cup (30g) all-purpose flour
- 1/2 tsp (3g) salt
- 1/4 tsp (1g) white pepper
- Vegetable oil for frying (about 6 cups)

**For Orange Sauce:**
- 1/2 cup (120ml) orange juice (fresh squeezed best)
- Zest of 1 orange
- 1/2 cup (100g) sugar
- 1/3 cup (80ml) rice vinegar (or white vinegar)
- 2 tbsp (30ml) soy sauce
- 1 tbsp (15ml) sesame oil
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp (15g) fresh ginger, minced
- 1/2 tsp (2g) red pepper flakes (adjust to taste)
- 2 tbsp (16g) cornstarch + 2 tbsp (30ml) water (slurry)

**For Garnish:**
- 2 green onions, sliced
- 1 tsp sesame seeds (optional)
- Orange slices (optional)

## Method

### Step 1: Prep Chicken
1. Cut chicken thighs into 1" bite-sized pieces
2. Pat dry with paper towels
3. Season with salt and white pepper
4. Set aside

### Step 2: Make Batter
1. In bowl, whisk together:
   - 2 eggs
   - 1/4 cup (60ml) water
2. In separate bowl, mix:
   - 1 cup (120g) cornstarch
   - 1/4 cup (30g) flour
3. Set aside remaining 1/2 cup (60g) cornstarch in third bowl

### Step 3: Heat Oil
1. Heat 3-4" oil in heavy pot to 350°F (177°C)
2. Use thermometer - temp is critical
3. While oil heats, coat chicken

### Step 4: Double-Coat Chicken (THE SIGNATURE TEXTURE)
1. Take piece of chicken
2. Dip in egg mixture (coat completely)
3. Roll in cornstarch-flour mixture (coat well)
4. Dip AGAIN in egg mixture
5. Roll AGAIN in plain cornstarch (in third bowl)
6. 🚨 **DOUBLE COATING = EXTRA CRISPY**
7. Place on plate
8. Repeat with all chicken pieces

### Step 5: Fry Chicken (2 batches)
1. Oil should be 350°F (177°C)
2. Carefully add half the chicken pieces
3. Don't crowd - maintain oil temp
4. Fry 4-5 minutes until golden brown and crispy
5. Internal temp should reach 165°F (74°C)
6. Remove to wire rack
7. Let oil return to 350°F
8. Fry second batch
9. 🚨 **Keep first batch warm in 200°F oven while frying second**

### Step 6: Make Orange Sauce (While Chicken Fries)
1. Heat wok or large skillet over MEDIUM-HIGH
2. Add 1 tbsp oil
3. Add garlic and ginger
4. Stir-fry 30 seconds (don't burn)
5. Add:
   - Orange juice
   - Orange zest
   - Sugar
   - Rice vinegar
   - Soy sauce
   - Sesame oil
   - Red pepper flakes
6. Bring to simmer
7. Cook 2-3 minutes

### Step 7: Thicken Sauce
1. Make cornstarch slurry:
   - 2 tbsp cornstarch + 2 tbsp water
   - Mix until smooth
2. Pour into simmering sauce while stirring
3. Sauce will thicken immediately
4. Cook 1 minute until glossy
5. Should coat back of spoon
6. Remove from heat

### Step 8: Toss and Serve (IMMEDIATELY)
1. Add all fried chicken to sauce
2. Toss gently to coat
3. Every piece should be glossy with sauce
4. Transfer to serving platter
5. Garnish with:
   - Green onions
   - Sesame seeds
   - Orange slices
6. 🚨 **Serve IMMEDIATELY** - coating stays crispy only 5-10 min after saucing

## Perfect Orange Chicken Indicators
- **Coating**: Crispy, golden, stays crunchy even with sauce
- **Chicken**: Tender, juicy inside (165°F internal)
- **Sauce**: Glossy, thick, coats every piece
- **Flavor**: Sweet-tangy-spicy balance
- **Texture**: Crispy outside, tender inside
- **Aroma**: Orange, garlic, ginger

## Variations

### Spicier Orange Chicken
- Increase red pepper flakes to 1-2 tsp
- Add 1 tsp chili garlic sauce to sauce

### Healthier Baked Version
- Bake coated chicken at 425°F for 20 min
- Not as crispy but less oil

### Extra Crispy
- Triple coat (egg-flour-egg-cornstarch-egg-cornstarch)
- Fry twice (fry, rest 5 min, fry again 2 min)

## Storage

**Best Fresh:**
- Orange chicken best within 15 min of saucing

**If Must Store:**
- Store chicken and sauce SEPARATELY
- Refrigerate up to 3 days
- Reheat chicken in 400°F oven 10 min (crisps up)
- Reheat sauce, then toss together

**Don't Store Together:**
- Coating gets soggy overnight

## Pro Tips

**Restaurant Secrets:**
- Panda Express fries chicken in batches all day
- Sauce is made in large batches, kept warm
- Chicken is sauced to order (keeps crispy)
- They use chicken thighs (not breast - more flavor)
- Double coating is mandatory

**For Best Results:**
- Use chicken thighs (breast gets dry)
- Fresh orange zest makes huge difference
- Maintain oil temp (use thermometer)
- Don't sauce until ready to serve
- Double coating is non-negotiable
- High heat for sauce (proper gloss)

**Crispy Coating Science:**
- Egg = protein binder
- Cornstarch = ultra crispy (crisper than flour)
- Double coat = thicker shell
- 350°F = perfect crisp without burning

## Make Ahead
1. Cut and season chicken (day before)
2. Make sauce (day before, refrigerate)
3. Coat chicken (1-2 hours before, refrigerate)
4. Fry just before serving
5. Reheat sauce, toss together
