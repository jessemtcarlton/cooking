# 🌟 KFC Original Recipe Fried Chicken (Copycat)

## Overview
The most famous fried chicken in the world. Crispy, herb-and-spice coated chicken with 11 secret herbs and spices. Pressure-fried for extra juiciness. This copycat recipe comes as close as possible to the Colonel's original recipe - crispy coating, juicy meat, perfectly seasoned.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **PRESSURE COOKER IDEAL**: KFC uses pressure fryers (home version: pressure cooker or regular fry)
- **BRINE IS MANDATORY**: 2-4 hours minimum (makes chicken juicy)
- **DOUBLE BREAD**: Flour → buttermilk → flour again (thick crispy coating)
- **LET COATING REST**: 20-30 min before frying (coating adheres better)
- **OIL TEMP CRITICAL**: 325-350°F for regular frying, 365°F for pressure
- **DON'T CROWD**: Fry 4-5 pieces at a time max
- **INTERNAL TEMP 165°F**: Use thermometer - no guessing

## The KFC Secret
1. **11 herbs and spices** (secret blend decoded over decades)
2. **Buttermilk brine** (tenderizes, adds flavor)
3. **Pressure frying** (seals in moisture, faster cooking)
4. **MSG** (controversial but authentic - use if comfortable)
5. **Double dredge** (thick, crunchy coating)
6. **Resting time** (coating sets before frying)
7. **Specific cut sizes** (even cooking)

## Ingredients (serves 6-8, makes 12 pieces)

**For Chicken:**
- 1 whole chicken (3-4 lbs/1.4-1.8kg), cut into 12 pieces:
  - 2 drumsticks
  - 2 thighs
  - 2 wings
  - 2 breasts (each cut in half)
- Or buy pre-cut chicken pieces

**For Brine:**
- 4 cups (960ml) buttermilk
- 2 tbsp (36g) kosher salt
- 1 tbsp (6g) black pepper
- 1 tbsp (9g) garlic powder
- 1 tbsp (6g) paprika

**For "11 Herbs and Spices" Coating:**
- 2 cups (240g) all-purpose flour
- 2 tsp (6g) paprika
- 1 tsp (2g) black pepper
- 1 tsp (2g) white pepper (important for authentic flavor)
- 1 tsp (3g) garlic powder
- 1 tsp (3g) onion powder
- 1 tsp (2g) dried oregano
- 1 tsp (2g) dried basil
- 1 tsp (2g) dried thyme
- 1 tsp (2g) dried marjoram
- 1 tsp (1g) celery salt
- 1 tsp (3g) mustard powder
- 2 tsp (2g) ground ginger
- 1/2 tsp (1g) cayenne pepper (adjust for heat)
- 1 tbsp (18g) kosher salt
- 1 tsp (5g) MSG (optional but authentic - omit if preferred)

**For Dredging:**
- 2 cups (480ml) buttermilk
- 1 egg

**For Frying:**
- Oil for frying (vegetable or shortening, about 8-10 cups/2-2.4L)

## Method

### Step 1: Brine Chicken (2-4 hours ahead)

**Prepare Brine:**
1. In large bowl or container, combine:
   - 4 cups (960ml) buttermilk
   - 2 tbsp (36g) salt
   - 1 tbsp (6g) black pepper
   - 1 tbsp (9g) garlic powder
   - 1 tbsp (6g) paprika
2. Whisk to combine

**Brine:**
1. Add chicken pieces to brine
2. Make sure all pieces submerged
3. Cover and refrigerate 2-4 hours (overnight is fine)
4. 🚨 **DON'T SKIP BRINE** - this is what makes it juicy

### Step 2: Make Herb and Spice Coating

**Mix "11 Herbs and Spices":**
1. In large bowl, combine:
   - 2 cups (240g) flour
   - 2 tsp paprika
   - 1 tsp black pepper
   - 1 tsp white pepper
   - 1 tsp garlic powder
   - 1 tsp onion powder
   - 1 tsp oregano
   - 1 tsp basil
   - 1 tsp thyme
   - 1 tsp marjoram
   - 1 tsp celery salt
   - 1 tsp mustard powder
   - 2 tsp ginger
   - 1/2 tsp cayenne
   - 1 tbsp salt
   - 1 tsp MSG (if using)
2. Whisk thoroughly to distribute spices
3. Divide into 2 shallow bowls (for double dredging)

**Buttermilk Wash:**
1. In another bowl, whisk together:
   - 2 cups (480ml) buttermilk
   - 1 egg

### Step 3: Bread Chicken (THE DOUBLE DREDGE)

**Setup:**
1. Remove chicken from brine
2. Let excess buttermilk drip off
3. Set up breading station: flour → buttermilk → flour

**Bread Each Piece:**
1. Dredge in seasoned flour (coat completely, press into crevices)
2. Dip in buttermilk-egg wash (coat completely)
3. Dredge in seasoned flour again (press firmly)
4. Place on wire rack over baking sheet
5. Repeat for all pieces
6. Let rest 20-30 minutes at room temp
7. 🚨 **RESTING TIME CRITICAL** - coating sets, adheres better

### Step 4A: Pressure Frying Method (Most Authentic)

**If You Have a Pressure Cooker:**
1. Add 2-3 cups oil to pressure cooker
2. Heat to 365°F (185°C)
3. Working in batches, add chicken pieces
4. Don't crowd (4-5 pieces max)
5. Close and lock lid
6. Cook 10-12 minutes at pressure
7. Quick release pressure
8. Check temp: 165°F (74°C) internal
9. Remove to wire rack
10. Repeat with remaining chicken
11. 🚨 **FOLLOW PRESSURE COOKER INSTRUCTIONS** - safety first

### Step 4B: Regular Frying Method (Home Version)

**If Regular Frying:**
1. Heat oil in deep fryer or pot to 325-350°F (163-177°C)
2. Need 3-4" deep oil
3. Working in batches, add chicken
4. **Cooking times**:
   - Wings: 12-15 minutes
   - Drumsticks: 14-16 minutes
   - Thighs: 16-18 minutes
   - Breast pieces: 18-20 minutes
5. Turn pieces halfway through
6. Should be deep golden brown
7. Internal temp 165°F (74°C)
8. Remove to wire rack
9. Let oil return to temp between batches
10. 🚨 **USE THERMOMETER** for both oil and chicken

### Step 5: Drain and Serve
1. Place fried chicken on wire rack (not paper towels only)
2. Let rest 5 minutes
3. Coating will crisp up further
4. Serve hot or at room temp
5. 🚨 **BEST WITHIN 2 HOURS** of frying

## Timing

**Total Time: 3-4 hours (includes brining)**
- Brine chicken: 2-4 hours (passive)
- Mix coating: 10 min
- Bread chicken: 20 min
- Rest: 20-30 min
- Fry: 40-50 min (in batches)
- Total active time: 90 min

**Make-Ahead:**
- Brine chicken overnight
- Mix spice blend days ahead (store airtight)
- Bread chicken 2 hours ahead, refrigerate
- Fry just before serving

## Troubleshooting

**Coating fell off:**
- Didn't let rest before frying (need 20-30 min)
- Oil temp too low (coating absorbed oil)
- Moved chicken too early (let it set 2-3 min before turning)

**Burnt coating, raw inside:**
- Oil too hot (should be 325-350°F)
- Pieces too large (cut breasts in half)
- Didn't use thermometer (must check internal temp)

**Soggy coating:**
- Oil temp too low (need 325°F minimum)
- Crowded the pot (temp dropped)
- Drained on paper towels only (need wire rack)
- Covered while hot (steam makes soggy)

**Dry chicken:**
- Didn't brine (mandatory step)
- Overcooked (check temp at 165°F)
- Oil too hot (cooked outside too fast)

**Bland flavor:**
- Didn't measure spices accurately
- Skipped MSG (adds savory depth - can omit but affects flavor)
- Not enough salt
- Old spices (lose potency over time)

**Greasy chicken:**
- Oil temp too low
- Didn't drain on wire rack
- Too much oil in pot

## Variations

### Extra Crispy (KFC Style)
- Add 1/2 cup cornstarch to flour mixture
- Double the amount of coating on each piece
- Fry at slightly higher temp (350°F)

### Spicy Fried Chicken
- Increase cayenne to 2 tsp
- Add 1 tsp hot sauce to buttermilk wash
- Nashville hot chicken style

### Buttermilk Fried Chicken
- Increase buttermilk brine time to overnight
- Add hot sauce to brine
- Tangier flavor

### Korean Fried Chicken Style
- Double fry: fry once, rest 10 min, fry again
- Toss in gochujang glaze
- Ultra crispy

### Oven "Fried" (Healthier)
- Bread as directed
- Place on wire rack over baking sheet
- Spray with oil
- Bake 425°F for 40-45 minutes
- Flip halfway through
- Not as crispy but still good

## Air Fryer Method

**Lower Fat Option:**
1. Bread chicken as directed
2. Spray with cooking spray
3. Air fry at 375°F:
   - Wings: 20 minutes
   - Drumsticks: 22 minutes
   - Thighs: 25 minutes
   - Breasts: 25-28 minutes
4. Flip halfway through
5. Spray with more oil after flipping
6. Check temp (165°F)

## Storage and Reheating

**Room Temperature:**
- 2 hours max (food safety)

**Refrigerator:**
- Store in airtight container 3-4 days
- Coating will soften

**Reheating (To Crisp):**
- Oven: 375°F for 15-20 minutes on wire rack
- Air fryer: 350°F for 8-10 minutes
- **Don't microwave** (coating gets soggy)

**Freezing:**
- Freeze fried chicken up to 3 months
- Wrap pieces individually
- Reheat from frozen: 350°F oven for 30-35 minutes

## Pro Tips

**Restaurant Secrets:**
- KFC uses pressure fryers (300-500 PSI, 365°F)
- Chicken marinated overnight in buttermilk
- Coating is applied by hand (not machine)
- Fried fresh throughout the day (never held more than 2 hours)
- Original recipe still locked in vault (this is closest copycat)
- White pepper is critical (different flavor than black)
- MSG in original recipe (can omit but authentic uses it)

**For Best Results:**
- Brine at least 2 hours (overnight better)
- Use white pepper (different than black - more floral)
- Let coating rest 20-30 min before frying
- Oil temp is critical (325-350°F)
- Don't crowd fryer
- Use thermometer for chicken temp
- Drain on wire rack
- MSG is optional but adds authentic flavor

**About MSG:**
- Monosodium glutamate
- Naturally occurring in tomatoes, cheese, mushrooms
- Adds savory "umami" flavor
- Used in most restaurant cooking
- Safe for most people
- Omit if you prefer (but flavor won't be exact)

**White Pepper vs Black Pepper:**
- White pepper: Milder, more floral, earthy
- Black pepper: Sharp, pungent
- KFC uses both for layered flavor
- Don't skip white pepper
