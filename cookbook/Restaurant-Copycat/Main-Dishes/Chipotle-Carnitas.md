# 🌟 Chipotle Carnitas (Copycat)

## Overview
Tender, juicy braised pork with crispy edges. Chipotle's carnitas are legendary - slow-cooked until fall-apart tender, then crisped on the griddle. Perfect for burritos, bowls, tacos, or salads. This copycat creates the exact flavor: citrusy, porky, with those signature crispy bits.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **PORK SHOULDER/BUTT ONLY**: Needs fat for tenderness (don't use loin)
- **LOW AND SLOW**: 3-4 hours at 300°F (can't rush this)
- **CRISP AFTER BRAISING**: Must crisp on griddle or broiler (signature texture)
- **SAVE THE BRAISING LIQUID**: Use for crisping (adds flavor, prevents drying)
- **DON'T OVERCRISP**: Just edges crispy, interior tender
- **BAY LEAVES REMOVE**: Before shredding (not edible)

## The Chipotle Secret
1. **Pork shoulder/butt** (marbled with fat, stays moist)
2. **Bay leaves** (aromatic, subtle flavor)
3. **Citrus** (orange juice - bright, tangy)
4. **Minimal spices** (juniper berries if you can find them)
5. **Braised low** (3-4 hours until fall-apart)
6. **Shredded** (not chopped - rustic chunks)
7. **Crisped on griddle** (in its own fat - crispy edges, tender middle)

## Equipment
- Dutch oven or large oven-safe pot with lid
- Cutting board and knife
- Two forks (for shredding)
- Large skillet or griddle (for crisping)
- Tongs or spatula
- Ladle or measuring cup

## Ingredients (serves 8-10)

**For Carnitas:**
- 4 lbs (1.8kg) pork shoulder/pork butt, cut into 2" chunks
- 1 tbsp (18g) kosher salt
- 1 tsp (2g) black pepper
- 1 tsp (3g) ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp (1g) dried oregano (Mexican oregano if available)
- 4 bay leaves
- 6-8 juniper berries (optional but authentic - adds subtle pine flavor)
- 1 cup (240ml) fresh orange juice
- 1/2 cup (120ml) water
- 4 cloves garlic, smashed
- 1 medium white onion, quartered

**For Crisping:**
- Reserved braising liquid
- Salt to taste

**For Serving:**
- Lime wedges
- Fresh cilantro, chopped
- Diced white onion
- Salsa (verde or roja)
- Warm tortillas
- Or burrito bowl components

## Method

### Step 1: Season Pork (5 minutes)
1. Cut 4 lbs (1.8kg) pork shoulder into 2" chunks
2. Don't trim fat (it renders during cooking, adds flavor)
3. In large bowl, toss pork with:
   - 1 tbsp (18g) salt
   - 1 tsp (2g) black pepper
   - 1 tsp (3g) cumin
   - 1/2 tsp (1g) oregano
4. Mix to coat all pieces

### Step 2: Braise Pork (3-4 hours)

**Arrange in Pot:**
1. Preheat oven to 300°F (149°C)
2. In Dutch oven or large pot:
3. Add seasoned pork pieces (single layer if possible)
4. Add:
   - 4 bay leaves
   - 6-8 juniper berries (if using)
   - 4 cloves smashed garlic
   - 1 onion, quartered
5. Pour over:
   - 1 cup (240ml) orange juice
   - 1/2 cup (120ml) water
6. Liquid should come halfway up pork (not covering)

**Braise:**
1. Cover pot with tight-fitting lid
2. Place in 300°F (149°C) oven
3. Cook 3-4 hours
4. **Check at 3 hours**:
   - Pork should be fall-apart tender
   - Insert fork - should slide in easily
   - If not tender, cook 30 min more
5. Remove from oven
6. 🚨 **LOW AND SLOW** - can't rush tender pork

### Step 3: Shred Pork
1. Remove pot from oven
2. Carefully transfer pork to large bowl
3. **SAVE THE BRAISING LIQUID** (very important)
4. Strain liquid, discard bay leaves, juniper, onion, garlic
5. Reserve 1-2 cups liquid
6. Let pork cool 10 minutes
7. Using two forks, shred pork into rustic chunks
8. Don't shred too fine (want substantial pieces)
9. 🚨 **REMOVE BAY LEAVES** - not edible

### Step 4: Crisp the Carnitas (THE SIGNATURE STEP)

**Stovetop Method (Preferred):**
1. Heat large skillet or griddle over MEDIUM-HIGH
2. Add shredded pork in single layer (work in batches)
3. Don't stir for 3-4 minutes (let crisp)
4. Add 1/4 cup reserved braising liquid
5. Liquid will sizzle and evaporate
6. Toss pork, let crisp another 2-3 minutes
7. Should have golden crispy edges
8. Transfer to serving bowl
9. Repeat with remaining pork
10. 🚨 **WANT CRISPY EDGES** - not dry and hard

**Broiler Method (Easier for Large Batch):**
1. Preheat broiler to HIGH
2. Spread shredded pork on baking sheet
3. Drizzle with 1/2 cup braising liquid
4. Broil 5-7 minutes
5. Toss pork, broil 3-5 more minutes
6. Watch closely (can burn fast)
7. Should have crispy golden edges

### Step 5: Serve
1. Taste and add more salt if needed
2. Squeeze fresh lime juice over pork
3. Toss to combine
4. Serve hot or warm
5. With cilantro, onions, salsa, limes
6. In tacos, burritos, bowls, or salads

## Perfect Carnitas Indicators
- **Texture**: Tender chunks with crispy edges (not mushy, not dry)
- **Color**: Golden brown crispy bits, moist interior
- **Flavor**: Porky, citrusy, subtle spice (not heavily seasoned)
- **Moisture**: Juicy but not wet (not dry)
- **Shred**: Rustic chunks (not fine shreds)
- **Aroma**: Porky, orangey, slightly caramelized

- Hot sauce available

## Timing

**Total Time: 4 hours**
- Season pork: 5 min
- Braise: 3-4 hours (mostly passive)
- Shred: 10 min
- Crisp: 10-15 min
- Total active time: 30 min

**Make-Ahead:**
- Braise pork up to 2 days ahead
- Store shredded pork in braising liquid
- Crisp just before serving
- Or crisp and reheat (less ideal)

## Troubleshooting

**Pork not tender:**
- Didn't cook long enough (need 3-4 hours)
- Oven temp too low
- Pot lid not tight (liquid evaporated)
- Used wrong cut (need pork shoulder/butt)

**Too dry:**
- Overcooked (past fall-apart stage)
- Didn't save braising liquid
- Crisped too long (just edges, not whole thing)
- Used pork loin (too lean)

**Not crispy:**
- Heat too low for crisping
- Too much liquid in pan
- Didn't let sit undisturbed
- Skipped crisping step entirely

**Bland flavor:**
- Not enough salt (taste and adjust)
- Skipped orange juice (adds brightness)
- Old spices
- Didn't reduce braising liquid for crisping

**Tough, chewy:**
- Didn't braise long enough
- Temperature too high (should be 300°F, not higher)
- Cut wrong (pork shoulder needs low and slow)

## Variations

### Extra Crispy
- Increase crisping time to 10 min per batch
- Use less braising liquid
- More edges get crispy

### Spicy Carnitas
- Add 2 chipotles in adobo to braising liquid
- Add 1 tsp cayenne to seasoning
- Chipotle-style heat

### Slow Cooker Method
- Season pork as directed
- Add to slow cooker with liquids and aromatics
- Cook LOW 8-10 hours or HIGH 4-5 hours
- Shred and crisp as directed

### Instant Pot Method
- Season pork as directed
- Add to Instant Pot with liquids and aromatics
- Pressure cook HIGH 60 minutes
- Natural release 15 minutes
- Shred and crisp as directed

### Carnitas Tacos
- Warm corn tortillas
- Fill with carnitas
- Top with cilantro, onion, lime, salsa

### Carnitas Burrito Bowl
- Rice
- Black beans
- Carnitas
- Salsa, sour cream, guacamole, cheese, lettuce

## Storage and Reheating

**Refrigerator:**
- Store shredded pork in braising liquid up to 4 days
- Keeps meat moist

**Freezer:**
- Freeze in braising liquid up to 3 months
- Thaw in fridge overnight

**Reheating:**
- Stovetop: Heat in skillet with splash of liquid, crisp edges
- Microwave: Heat in 30-second intervals (doesn't crisp)
- Oven: 350°F for 15-20 minutes

## Pro Tips

**Restaurant Secrets:**
- Chipotle braises whole pork shoulder for hours
- Shredded by hand (not chopped)
- Finished on flat-top griddle (gets those crispy bits)
- Kept warm in its own juices (stays moist)
- Simple seasoning (lets pork flavor shine)
- Orange juice is key (brightens flavor)
- Juniper berries add subtle depth (hard to identify but missed if absent)

**For Best Results:**
- Use pork shoulder/butt (fat content is critical)
- Don't trim fat before cooking (renders out)
- Low oven temp (300°F - patient cooking)
- Save braising liquid (gold for flavor)
- Shred by hand (rustic chunks, not fine)
- Crisp in batches (don't crowd pan)
- Taste and salt at end
- Squeeze lime before serving

**Juniper Berries:**
- Small, dark, pine-scented berries
- Used to make gin (that's the flavor)
- Adds subtle background flavor
- Find at spice shops or online
- Optional but authentic
