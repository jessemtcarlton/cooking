# 🌟 Texas Roadhouse Honey Butter Rolls (Copycat)

## Overview
The legendary rolls with cinnamon honey butter that people crave. Soft, pillowy, slightly sweet rolls that are impossibly tender. The honey butter is addictive. This is the exact copycat recipe.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **YEAST MUST BE ALIVE**: Test in warm milk (110-115°F), should foam in 5 min
- **DON'T KILL YEAST**: Liquid too hot (over 120°F) = dead yeast, no rise
- **MUST RISE TWICE**: First rise 1 hour, second rise 30 min - can't skip
- **KNEADING MATTERS**: 8-10 minutes or rolls will be dense
- **TIMING IS LONG**: 2.5 hours start to finish (most is passive rising)

## The Texas Roadhouse Secret
1. **Honey in dough** (sweetness throughout)
2. **Butter-enriched dough** (ultra-tender crumb)
3. **Brush with butter twice** (before and after baking)
4. **Cinnamon honey butter** (the signature - more cinnamon than you think)
5. **Serve piping hot** (from oven to table in bucket)

## Equipment
- Stand mixer with dough hook (or hands)
- 2 large bowls
- Clean kitchen towel
- 9x13" baking pan
- Pastry brush
- Instant-read thermometer (for milk temp)

## Ingredients

**For Rolls** (makes 24):
- 1 cup (240ml) whole milk, warmed to 110-115°F (43-46°C)
- 2 packets (14g) active dry yeast (or 1 tbsp instant yeast)
- 1/3 cup (67g) sugar
- 1/3 cup (80ml) honey
- 1/3 cup (76g) butter, melted and slightly cooled
- 1 large egg
- 1 large egg yolk
- 1 tsp (6g) kosher salt
- 4 cups (480g) all-purpose flour (plus more for kneading)
- 2 tbsp (28g) butter, melted (for brushing)

**For Cinnamon Honey Butter:**
- 1/2 cup (113g) butter, softened to room temp
- 1/3 cup (80ml) honey
- 2 tbsp (16g) powdered sugar
- 1 tsp (3g) ground cinnamon

## Method

### Step 1: Activate Yeast (5-10 minutes)
1. Warm 1 cup (240ml) milk to 110-115°F (43-46°C)
   - 🚨 **TEST WITH THERMOMETER** - too hot kills yeast
   - Should feel warm (not hot) on wrist
2. Add 2 packets (14g) yeast to warm milk
3. Add 1 tsp sugar from the 1/3 cup
4. Stir gently, let sit 5-10 minutes
5. Should become foamy/bubbly on top
6. 🚨 **IF NOT FOAMY** - yeast is dead, start over with new yeast

### Step 2: Mix Dough
1. In stand mixer bowl (or large bowl), combine:
   - Foamy yeast mixture
   - 1/3 cup (67g) sugar (remaining)
   - 1/3 cup (80ml) honey
   - 1/3 cup (76g) melted butter (cooled to room temp)
   - 1 egg + 1 egg yolk
   - 1 tsp (6g) salt
2. Mix on low speed (or whisk by hand)
3. Gradually add 4 cups (480g) flour, 1 cup at a time
4. Mix until shaggy dough forms

### Step 3: Knead Dough (8-10 minutes)
**With Stand Mixer:**
- Knead with dough hook on medium-low speed
- Knead 8-10 minutes
- Dough should pull away from sides of bowl
- Should be smooth, elastic, slightly sticky

**By Hand:**
- Turn out onto floured surface
- Knead 10-12 minutes
- Push, fold, turn, repeat
- Add flour 1 tbsp at a time if too sticky
- Should be smooth, elastic, slightly tacky

**Windowpane Test:**
- Stretch small piece of dough
- Should stretch thin without breaking (like windowpane)
- If tears, knead more

### Step 4: First Rise (1 hour)
1. Form dough into ball
2. Place in greased bowl
3. Turn to coat with oil
4. Cover with clean kitchen towel
5. Let rise in warm place 1 hour
6. Should double in size
7. 🚨 **MUST DOUBLE** or rolls will be dense

**Warm Place Ideas:**
- On top of fridge
- In oven with light on (not heat)
- Near warm stove
- In microwave with cup of boiled water

### Step 5: Shape Rolls
1. Punch down dough to release air
2. Turn out onto lightly floured surface
3. Divide into 24 equal pieces (use scale if possible - about 40g each)
4. Roll each piece into smooth ball:
   - Cup hand over dough
   - Roll in circular motion on counter
   - Tuck edges under to create smooth top
5. Place in greased 9x13" pan (4 rows of 6 rolls)
6. Rolls should just touch each other

### Step 6: Second Rise (30 minutes)
1. Cover pan with kitchen towel
2. Let rise 30 minutes
3. Rolls should puff up, touching each other
4. Should look pillowy and nearly doubled

### Step 7: Brush and Bake
1. Preheat oven to 350°F (177°C)
2. Gently brush tops with 1 tbsp (14g) melted butter
3. Bake 15-18 minutes
4. **CHECK AT 15 MINUTES**:
   - Tops should be golden (not brown)
   - Internal temp 190°F (88°C)
5. Should sound hollow when tapped

### Step 8: Butter Again and Serve
1. Remove from oven
2. IMMEDIATELY brush with remaining 1 tbsp (14g) melted butter
3. Let cool in pan 5 minutes
4. Serve warm with cinnamon honey butter

## Make Cinnamon Honey Butter (Can Make Days Ahead)

### Ingredients:
- 1/2 cup (113g) butter, softened
- 1/3 cup (80ml) honey
- 2 tbsp (16g) powdered sugar
- 1 tsp (3g) ground cinnamon

### Method:
1. Beat softened butter with mixer until fluffy (2-3 min)
2. Add honey, powdered sugar, cinnamon
3. Beat until light and fluffy (3-4 min)
4. Should be smooth, spreadable, whipped
5. Store in airtight container, refrigerator up to 2 weeks
6. Bring to room temp 30 min before serving

## Timing for Dinner

**Full Timeline (2.5 hours):**
- Mix dough: 15 min
- First rise: 1 hour
- Shape rolls: 10 min
- Second rise: 30 min
- Bake: 18 min
- Cool slightly: 5 min

**Make-Ahead Options:**
- After first rise: Refrigerate overnight, bring to room temp, continue
- After shaping: Refrigerate overnight, bring to room temp (1 hour), let rise 30 min, bake
- Fully baked: Freeze up to 2 months, thaw and reheat

## Reheating
- Wrap in foil, 350°F oven for 10 minutes
- Microwave 15-20 seconds (less ideal)
- Brush with butter after reheating

## Troubleshooting

**Rolls didn't rise:**
- Yeast was dead (check expiration)
- Liquid too hot (killed yeast)
- Room too cold (need warm environment)
- Didn't rise long enough

**Dense, heavy rolls:**
- Not enough kneading (need 8-10 min)
- Didn't rise long enough
- Too much flour added

**Dry rolls:**
- Over-baked
- Not enough butter
- Too much flour in dough

**Too sweet:**
- Texas Roadhouse style is sweet - this is authentic
- Reduce honey to 1/4 cup if prefer less sweet

## Variations

### Garlic Butter Rolls
- Add 2 tsp garlic powder to butter brush
- Sprinkle with parsley

### Cinnamon Sugar Rolls
- After baking, brush with butter
- Roll in cinnamon sugar mixture

### Dinner Rolls (Less Sweet)
- Reduce honey to 2 tbsp
- Omit honey butter, serve with regular butter

## Storage

**Room Temperature:**
- 2-3 days in airtight container
- Reheat before serving

**Refrigerator:**
- 5 days in airtight container
- Bring to room temp, reheat

**Freezer:**
- Freeze baked rolls up to 3 months
- Thaw, reheat in foil at 350°F (10 min)
- Or freeze dough after shaping, thaw and rise when needed
