# 🌟 Red Lobster Cheddar Bay Biscuits (Copycat)

## Overview
The most famous restaurant bread in America. Buttery, cheesy, garlicky drop biscuits that melt in your mouth. This copycat recipe is indistinguishable from the restaurant version.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **DON'T OVERMIX**: Stir just until combined or biscuits will be tough
- **COLD BUTTER CRITICAL**: Must be cold and cut into small pieces
- **BRUSH IMMEDIATELY**: Garlic butter must go on while biscuits are hot
- **BAKE TIME TIGHT**: 10-12 minutes - check at 10, golden brown tops
- **SHARP CHEDDAR**: Mild cheddar won't have the right flavor

## The Red Lobster Secret
1. **Bisquick-style mix** (self-rising flour + shortening)
2. **Sharp cheddar** (pre-shredded is OK here)
3. **Garlic powder in batter** (not just on top)
4. **Butter brush twice** (once at 8 min, once when done)
5. **Drop biscuits** (not rolled - rustic texture)

## Equipment
- Mixing bowls (2)
- Whisk
- Fork or pastry cutter
- Ice cream scoop or large spoon
- Baking sheet
- Parchment paper
- Pastry brush

## Ingredients

**For Biscuits** (makes 10-12):
- 2 cups (240g) all-purpose flour
- 1 tbsp (12g) baking powder
- 1 tbsp (12g) sugar
- 2 tsp (6g) garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp (3g) kosher salt
- 1/4 tsp (0.5g) cayenne pepper (optional but authentic)
- 1/2 cup (113g) cold butter, cut into small cubes
- 1 cup (100g) sharp cheddar cheese, shredded
- 3/4 cup (180ml) whole milk (cold)

**For Garlic Butter Topping:**
- 4 tbsp (56g) butter, melted
- 1/2 tsp (1.5g) garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp (0.5g) dried parsley flakes
- Pinch of salt

## Method

### Step 1: Preheat Oven
1. Preheat oven to 450°F (232°C)
2. Line baking sheet with parchment paper
3. Must be fully preheated

### Step 2: Mix Dry Ingredients
1. In large bowl, whisk together:
   - 2 cups (240g) flour
   - 1 tbsp (12g) baking powder
   - 1 tbsp (12g) sugar
   - 2 tsp (6g) garlic powder
   - 1/2 tsp (3g) salt
   - 1/4 tsp (0.5g) cayenne (if using)
2. Whisk well to combine and aerate

### Step 3: Cut in Butter (Like Pie Crust)
1. Add 1/2 cup (113g) cold butter cubes to dry ingredients
2. Using fork or pastry cutter, cut butter into flour
3. Work until mixture resembles coarse crumbs (pea-sized)
4. Should look like wet sand with pea-sized butter pieces
5. 🚨 **KEEP COLD** - work quickly, don't use hands (melt butter)

### Step 4: Add Cheese and Milk
1. Stir in 1 cup (100g) sharp cheddar cheese
2. Add 3/4 cup (180ml) cold milk
3. Stir with fork just until combined (about 10-15 stirs)
4. 🚨 **DON'T OVERMIX** - dough should be shaggy and lumpy
5. Some dry flour streaks OK
6. Overmixing = tough biscuits

### Step 5: Scoop and Bake (10-12 minutes)
1. Using ice cream scoop or large spoon:
   - Scoop heaping 1/4 cup portions onto baking sheet
   - Space 2" apart
   - Don't smooth tops (rustic = authentic)
2. Should make 10-12 biscuits
3. Bake at 450°F (232°C) for 10-12 minutes
4. 🚨 **CHECK AT 10 MINUTES**:
   - Tops should be golden brown
   - Bottoms should be light golden
   - If pale, continue 1-2 more minutes

### Step 6: Make Garlic Butter (While Baking)
1. Melt 4 tbsp (56g) butter
2. Stir in:
   - 1/2 tsp (1.5g) garlic powder
   - 1/4 tsp (0.5g) dried parsley
   - Pinch of salt
3. Mix well, keep warm

### Step 7: Brush with Garlic Butter (IMMEDIATELY)
1. Remove biscuits from oven
2. **IMMEDIATELY** brush tops with garlic butter (while HOT)
3. Brush generously - soak the tops
4. Let sit 2-3 minutes
5. Brush again with more garlic butter
6. Serve warm

## Perfect Cheddar Bay Biscuit Indicators
- **Texture**: Fluffy, tender, melt-in-mouth
- **Top**: Golden brown, brushed with garlic butter
- **Bottom**: Light golden (not dark brown)
- **Inside**: Flaky layers, pockets of melted cheese
- **Flavor**: Buttery, cheesy, garlicky
- **Appearance**: Rustic, irregular (not uniform)

## Timing for Dinner Party
**30 minutes before serving:**
- Mix biscuits (5 min)
- Bake (12 min)
- Brush with butter (2 min)
- Serve warm

**Can make ahead:**
- Mix dry ingredients day before
- Cut butter into dry mix, refrigerate (day before)
- Add milk and cheese, scoop, bake when ready

**Reheating:**
- 350°F oven for 5-7 minutes
- Microwave 15-20 seconds (less ideal, gets soft)
- Brush with more garlic butter after reheating

## Troubleshooting

**Biscuits are tough:**
- Overmixed batter (stir just until combined)
- Used warm butter (must be cold)
- Over-baked

**Biscuits spread too much:**
- Butter was too warm
- Not enough flour
- Oven not hot enough

**Not cheesy enough:**
- Used mild cheddar (need sharp)
- Not enough cheese
- Cheese not mixed in well

**Dry biscuits:**
- Over-baked
- Not enough butter brushed on top
- Not enough milk in batter

**Not golden brown:**
- Oven too low (needs 450°F)
- Under-baked
- Not brushed with enough butter

## Variations

### Extra Cheesy
- Increase cheddar to 1.5 cups (150g)
- Add 2 tbsp (10g) Parmesan to topping

### Jalapeño Cheddar
- Add 2 tbsp (20g) diced pickled jalapeños to batter
- Drain well before adding

### Bacon Cheddar
- Add 4 strips cooked bacon, crumbled
- Fold into batter with cheese

### Herb Garden
- Add 1 tbsp fresh herbs (chives, parsley, dill)
- Mix into batter

## Storage

**Room Temperature:**
- Store in airtight container 1-2 days
- Reheat before serving

**Refrigerator:**
- 3-4 days in airtight container
- Bring to room temp, reheat in oven

**Freezer:**
- Freeze baked biscuits up to 2 months
- Thaw and reheat in 350°F oven (10 min)
- Or freeze unbaked scoops, bake from frozen (add 2-3 min)

## Why This Recipe Works

**Science:**
- High heat (450°F) creates steam quickly (flaky layers)
- Cold butter creates pockets (flaky texture)
- Minimal mixing prevents gluten development (tender not tough)
- Baking powder double-action (rises in oven)
- Drop method keeps them rustic (authentic)

**Technique:**
- Cut-in method (like pie crust) = flaky
- Cold ingredients = rise better
- Quick bake = stays moist
- Garlic butter while hot = absorbs better
