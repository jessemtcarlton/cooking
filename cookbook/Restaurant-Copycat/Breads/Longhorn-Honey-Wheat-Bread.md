# 🌟 Longhorn Steakhouse Honey Wheat Bread (Copycat)

## Overview
The warm, sweet wheat bread served at every Longhorn table. Soft, fluffy, slightly sweet with honey butter melted on top. Impossible to stop at one slice. This copycat recipe creates the exact texture and sweetness.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **YEAST TEMPERATURE**: 105-115°F only - hotter kills yeast, cooler won't activate
- **HONEY BUTTER MUST BE MADE**: This is THE signature - don't skip it
- **MINI LOAF PANS IDEAL**: Longhorn uses small loaves (4x6"), not full-size
- **RISE TIME CRITICAL**: Must double in size each time (1 hour each rise)
- **BRUSH WHILE HOT**: Honey butter goes on immediately after baking

## The Longhorn Secret
1. **Wheat and white flour blend** (texture and flavor balance)
2. **Honey in dough** (subtle sweetness throughout)
3. **Mini loaf format** (easier to serve warm slices)
4. **Honey butter on top** (melts into warm bread)
5. **Served warm** (within 20 minutes of baking)
6. **Unlimited refills** (part of the Longhorn experience)

## Equipment
- Stand mixer with dough hook (or by hand)
- 4 mini loaf pans (4x6" each) OR 2 regular loaf pans (9x5")
- Mixing bowls
- Clean kitchen towel
- Pastry brush
- Instant-read thermometer

## Ingredients

**For Bread** (makes 4 mini loaves or 2 regular loaves):
- 1 1/4 cups (300ml) water, warmed to 110°F (43°C)
- 2 tbsp (30ml) honey
- 1 packet (7g) active dry yeast (2 1/4 tsp)
- 2 cups (240g) all-purpose flour
- 1 cup (120g) whole wheat flour
- 1 1/2 tsp (9g) kosher salt
- 3 tbsp (42g) butter, softened
- 2 tbsp (30ml) vegetable oil

**For Honey Butter** (THE SIGNATURE):
- 1/2 cup (113g) butter, softened
- 1/4 cup (60ml) honey
- 2 tbsp (16g) powdered sugar
- Pinch of salt

**For Brushing:**
- 2 tbsp (28g) butter, melted

## Method

### Step 1: Activate Yeast (5-10 minutes)
1. Warm 1 1/4 cups (300ml) water to 110°F (43°C)
   - 🚨 **USE THERMOMETER** - temp is critical
2. Add 2 tbsp (30ml) honey to warm water
3. Sprinkle 1 packet (7g) yeast over honey water
4. Stir gently
5. Let sit 5-10 minutes
6. Should become foamy and bubbly
7. 🚨 **IF NOT FOAMY** - yeast is dead, start over

### Step 2: Mix Dough
1. In stand mixer bowl (or large bowl):
   - Foamy yeast mixture
   - 2 cups (240g) all-purpose flour
   - 1 cup (120g) whole wheat flour
   - 1 1/2 tsp (9g) salt
   - 3 tbsp (42g) softened butter
   - 2 tbsp (30ml) vegetable oil
2. Mix on low speed until shaggy dough forms

### Step 3: Knead Dough (8-10 minutes)
**With Stand Mixer:**
- Knead with dough hook on medium-low
- 8-10 minutes until smooth and elastic
- Dough should clear sides of bowl
- Slightly tacky but not sticky

**By Hand:**
- Turn onto floured surface
- Knead 10-12 minutes
- Should be smooth, elastic, springs back when poked
- Add flour 1 tbsp at a time if too sticky

### Step 4: First Rise (1 hour)
1. Form dough into ball
2. Place in greased bowl
3. Turn to coat with oil
4. Cover with clean kitchen towel
5. Let rise in warm place 1 hour
6. Should double in size
7. 🚨 **MUST DOUBLE** - this creates the light texture

### Step 5: Shape Loaves

**For Mini Loaves (Longhorn Style):**
1. Grease 4 mini loaf pans (4x6")
2. Punch down dough
3. Divide into 4 equal pieces (about 180g each)
4. Shape each into small loaf:
   - Pat into rectangle
   - Roll tightly from short end
   - Pinch seam closed
   - Tuck ends under
5. Place seam-side down in greased pans
6. Should fill pans about halfway

**For Regular Loaves:**
1. Grease 2 regular loaf pans (9x5")
2. Divide dough in half
3. Shape each into loaf
4. Place seam-side down in pans

### Step 6: Second Rise (45-60 minutes)
1. Cover pans with kitchen towel
2. Let rise 45-60 minutes
3. Dough should rise above rim of mini pans
4. Or double in size for regular pans
5. Should look puffy and pillowy

### Step 7: Bake

**For Mini Loaves:**
- Preheat oven to 350°F (177°C)
- Bake 20-25 minutes
- **CHECK AT 20 MINUTES**:
  - Golden brown tops
  - Internal temp 190°F (88°C)
  - Sounds hollow when tapped

**For Regular Loaves:**
- Preheat oven to 350°F (177°C)
- Bake 30-35 minutes
- Same doneness indicators

### Step 8: Brush and Serve
1. Remove from oven
2. IMMEDIATELY brush tops with melted butter (2 tbsp)
3. Let cool in pans 5 minutes
4. Remove from pans
5. Slice while warm
6. Top with honey butter
7. Serve immediately

## Make Honey Butter (Can Make Days Ahead)

### Ingredients:
- 1/2 cup (113g) butter, softened
- 1/4 cup (60ml) honey
- 2 tbsp (16g) powdered sugar
- Pinch of salt

### Method:
1. Beat softened butter with mixer until fluffy (2-3 min)
2. Add honey, powdered sugar, salt
3. Beat until light, fluffy, and well combined (3-4 min)
4. Should be smooth and spreadable
5. Store in airtight container
6. Refrigerate up to 2 weeks
7. Bring to room temp 30 min before serving

## Timing

**Total Time: 2.5 hours**
- Activate yeast: 10 min
- Mix and knead: 10 min
- First rise: 1 hour
- Shape: 10 min
- Second rise: 45-60 min
- Bake: 20-25 min (mini) or 30-35 min (regular)

**Make-Ahead Options:**
- After first rise: Refrigerate overnight, bring to room temp, continue
- After shaping: Refrigerate overnight, bring to room temp (1 hour), let rise, bake
- Fully baked: Freeze up to 3 months, thaw and reheat

## Troubleshooting

**Bread didn't rise:**
- Yeast was dead (check expiration)
- Water too hot or too cold
- Room too cold for rising
- Didn't rise long enough

**Dense, heavy bread:**
- Not enough kneading
- Didn't rise enough
- Too much whole wheat flour (measure accurately)

**Too dry:**
- Overbaked (check at minimum time)
- Not enough honey butter
- Cooled too long uncovered

**Not sweet enough:**
- Need to use honey butter (this is critical)
- Make sure honey is in dough
- Can increase honey to 3 tbsp in dough

## Variations

### Extra Honey
- Increase honey in dough to 1/4 cup (60ml)
- Makes sweeter, moister bread

### All White Flour
- Use 3 cups (360g) all-purpose flour (no whole wheat)
- Lighter texture, less wheaty flavor

### Cinnamon Swirl
- Roll dough with cinnamon sugar before shaping
- Creates marble effect

### Oats on Top
- Brush tops with water before baking
- Sprinkle with rolled oats

## Storage and Reheating

**Room Temperature:**
- 3 days in airtight container or bread bag
- Slice as needed

**Refrigerator:**
- 1 week in airtight container
- Can dry out, best to freeze instead

**Freezer:**
- Wrap tightly in plastic, then foil
- Freeze up to 3 months
- Thaw at room temp
- Reheat wrapped in foil at 350°F for 15 min

**Reheating:**
- Wrap in foil, 350°F for 10-15 minutes
- Microwave individual slices 15-20 seconds
- Toaster or toaster oven for slices
