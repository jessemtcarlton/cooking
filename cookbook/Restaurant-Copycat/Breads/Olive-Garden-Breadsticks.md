# 🌟 Olive Garden Breadsticks (Copycat)

## Overview
The iconic unlimited breadsticks that made Olive Garden famous. Soft, fluffy interior with crispy garlic-butter crust. Salted and brushed generously with garlic butter. Impossible to eat just one.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **YEAST TEMPERATURE**: 105-115°F only - hotter kills yeast
- **RISE TIME MATTERS**: Must double in size (about 1 hour)
- **BRUSH WHILE HOT**: Garlic butter must go on immediately after baking
- **DON'T OVERBAKE**: 12-14 minutes max or they'll be hard
- **GARLIC SALT AT END**: After butter, not before

## The Olive Garden Secret
1. **Fast rise recipe** (only 1 rise needed)
2. **No eggs** (keeps them light, not rich)
3. **Generous garlic butter** (brushed twice - before and after baking)
4. **Coarse salt on top** (the signature finish)
5. **Serve in cloth-lined basket** (keeps warm, absorbs steam)

## Equipment
- Stand mixer with dough hook (or by hand)
- Baking sheets (2)
- Parchment paper
- Pastry brush
- Clean kitchen towel
- Instant-read thermometer

## Ingredients

**For Breadsticks** (makes 12):
- 1 1/2 cups (360ml) water, warmed to 105-115°F (40-46°C)
- 1 tbsp (12g) sugar
- 1 packet (7g) active dry yeast (2 1/4 tsp)
- 4 cups (480g) all-purpose flour (plus more for kneading)
- 2 tbsp (28g) butter, softened
- 2 tsp (12g) kosher salt

**For Garlic Butter:**
- 1/2 cup (113g) butter, melted
- 1 tsp (3g) garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp (1g) garlic salt

**For Topping:**
- Coarse sea salt or kosher salt (for sprinkling)

## Method

### Step 1: Activate Yeast (5-10 minutes)
1. Warm 1 1/2 cups (360ml) water to 105-115°F (40-46°C)
2. Add 1 tbsp (12g) sugar
3. Sprinkle 1 packet (7g) yeast over water
4. Stir gently, let sit 5-10 minutes
5. Should become foamy and bubbly
6. 🚨 **IF NOT FOAMY** - yeast is dead, start over

### Step 2: Mix Dough
1. In stand mixer bowl (or large bowl):
   - Foamy yeast mixture
   - 2 tbsp (28g) softened butter
   - 2 tsp (12g) salt
2. Add 4 cups (480g) flour gradually
3. Mix on low until shaggy dough forms

### Step 3: Knead Dough (6-8 minutes)
**With Stand Mixer:**
- Knead with dough hook on medium-low
- 6-8 minutes until smooth and elastic
- Dough should clear sides of bowl
- Slightly tacky but not sticky

**By Hand:**
- Turn onto floured surface
- Knead 8-10 minutes
- Should be smooth, elastic, slightly springy
- Add flour 1 tbsp at a time if too sticky

### Step 4: Rise (1 hour)
1. Form dough into ball
2. Place in greased bowl
3. Turn to coat with oil
4. Cover with clean kitchen towel
5. Let rise in warm place 1 hour
6. Should double in size
7. 🚨 **MUST DOUBLE** - this is the only rise

### Step 5: Preheat and Prep
1. Preheat oven to 400°F (204°C)
2. Line 2 baking sheets with parchment
3. Make garlic butter:
   - Melt 1/2 cup (113g) butter
   - Mix in 1 tsp (3g) garlic powder
   - Mix in 1/2 tsp (1g) garlic salt
   - Keep warm

### Step 6: Shape Breadsticks
1. Punch down dough to release air
2. Turn onto lightly floured surface
3. Divide into 12 equal pieces (about 65g each)
4. Roll each piece into 8" long rope:
   - Roll with palms, applying even pressure
   - Work from center outward
   - Should be uniform thickness (about 3/4" diameter)
5. Place on prepared baking sheets (6 per sheet)
6. Space 2" apart

### Step 7: Brush with Garlic Butter (Before Baking)
1. Brush each breadstick generously with garlic butter
2. Use about half the garlic butter
3. Make sure to coat all sides
4. Let rest 5 minutes while oven preheats

### Step 8: Bake (12-14 minutes)
1. Bake at 400°F (204°C) for 12-14 minutes
2. **CHECK AT 12 MINUTES**:
   - Should be light golden (not brown)
   - Firm to touch but still soft
3. Don't overbake - Olive Garden's are soft, not crispy

### Step 9: Butter and Salt (IMMEDIATELY)
1. Remove from oven
2. **IMMEDIATELY** brush with remaining garlic butter
3. Be generous - they should glisten
4. Sprinkle with coarse salt while still wet
5. Let cool 2-3 minutes
6. Serve warm

- **Interior**: Soft, fluffy, pull-apart texture
- **Aroma**: Garlic, butter, yeasty

- **Taste**: Garlicky, buttery, slightly salty, not sweet

## Timing for Dinner

**Total Time: 2 hours**
- Activate yeast: 10 min
- Mix and knead: 10 min
- Rise: 1 hour
- Shape: 10 min
- Bake: 14 min
- Cool slightly: 5 min

**Make-Ahead Options:**
- After rising: Refrigerate dough overnight, bring to room temp (30 min), shape and bake
- After shaping: Cover and refrigerate up to 4 hours, bring to room temp (15 min), bake
- Fully baked: Freeze up to 2 months, thaw and reheat

## Reheating
- Wrap in foil, 350°F for 8-10 minutes
- Brush with more garlic butter after reheating
- Microwave 15-20 seconds (less ideal - gets soft)

## Troubleshooting

**Breadsticks are hard/crusty:**
- Overbaked (should be light golden, not brown)
- Not enough garlic butter
- Let cool too long uncovered

**Dense, not fluffy:**
- Didn't rise long enough
- Yeast wasn't active
- Over-kneaded
- Too much flour

**Not garlicky enough:**
- Not enough garlic powder in butter
- Didn't brush enough
- Use fresh garlic powder (old loses potency)

**Too salty:**
- Used too much coarse salt on top
- Light sprinkle is enough
- Garlic salt already in butter

## Variations

### Extra Garlic
- Add 2 minced garlic cloves to butter (in addition to powder)
- Increase garlic powder to 2 tsp

### Cheesy Breadsticks
- Sprinkle grated Parmesan on top with salt
- Add 1/2 cup shredded mozzarella to butter

### Herb Breadsticks
- Add 1 tsp Italian seasoning to garlic butter
- Sprinkle with dried parsley

### Asiago Cheese (Olive Garden's Special Version)
- Mix 1/2 cup grated Asiago into dough
- Sprinkle more on top before baking

## Storage

**Room Temperature:**
- 2 days in airtight container
- Reheat before serving

**Refrigerator:**
- 5 days in airtight container
- Reheat in foil

**Freezer:**
- Freeze baked breadsticks up to 3 months
- Thaw and reheat wrapped in foil at 350°F (10 min)
- Or freeze shaped dough, thaw in fridge overnight, bake when ready
