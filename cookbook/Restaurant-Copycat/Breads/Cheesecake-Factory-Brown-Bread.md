# 🌟 The Cheesecake Factory Brown Bread (Copycat)

## Overview
The most requested recipe in Cheesecake Factory history. Dark, slightly sweet, incredibly soft bread with a unique cocoa color and hint of molasses. Served warm with creamy butter. This copycat nails the exact flavor and texture that people obsess over.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **COCOA POWDER IS MANDATORY**: Dutch-process cocoa creates the signature color (no chocolate taste)
- **MOLASSES + HONEY**: Both are required - unique sweet depth
- **ESPRESSO POWDER**: Enhances cocoa color, doesn't taste like coffee
- **YEAST MUST ACTIVATE**: 110-115°F water, should foam in 5 minutes
- **MINI LOAF PANS**: Cheesecake Factory uses small loaves (crucial for texture)
- **SERVE WARM**: Room temp bread doesn't have the same magic

## The Cheesecake Factory Secret
1. **Dutch-process cocoa powder** (creates dark color without chocolate flavor)
2. **Molasses + honey combo** (complex sweetness)
3. **Espresso powder** (deepens color, no coffee taste)
4. **Wheat and white flour blend** (soft but substantial)
5. **Mini loaf format** (stays moist, serves warm slices easily)
6. **Butter while hot** (melts into bread)

## Equipment
- Stand mixer with dough hook (or by hand)
- 4 mini loaf pans (5.75" x 3.25") OR 2 regular loaf pans (9x5")
- Mixing bowls
- Clean kitchen towel
- Instant-read thermometer
- Pastry brush

## Ingredients

**For Bread** (makes 4 mini loaves or 2 regular loaves):
- 1 1/2 cups (360ml) water, warmed to 110°F (43°C)
- 1 packet (7g) active dry yeast (2 1/4 tsp)
- 3 tbsp (45ml) honey
- 2 tbsp (30ml) molasses
- 2 tbsp (28g) butter, melted and slightly cooled
- 1/4 cup (50g) dark brown sugar, packed
- 3 tbsp (18g) Dutch-process cocoa powder (must be Dutch-process)
- 1 tsp (3g) instant espresso powder (optional but recommended)
- 1 1/2 tsp (9g) kosher salt
- 2 cups (240g) all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 cups (180g) whole wheat flour
- 2 tbsp (28g) butter, melted (for brushing)

**For Serving:**
- Softened butter (salted or unsalted)

## Method

### Step 1: Activate Yeast (5-10 minutes)
1. Warm 1 1/2 cups (360ml) water to 110°F (43°C)
2. Pour into bowl or stand mixer
3. Add 1 tsp sugar from brown sugar measurement
4. Sprinkle 1 packet (7g) yeast over water
5. Stir gently, let sit 5-10 minutes
6. Should become foamy on top
7. 🚨 **IF NOT FOAMY** - yeast is dead, start over

### Step 2: Add Wet Ingredients
1. To foamy yeast mixture, add:
   - 3 tbsp (45ml) honey
   - 2 tbsp (30ml) molasses
   - 2 tbsp (28g) melted butter (cooled to room temp)
   - 1/4 cup (50g) dark brown sugar
2. Stir to combine

### Step 3: Add Dry Ingredients
1. In separate bowl, whisk together:
   - 3 tbsp (18g) Dutch-process cocoa powder
   - 1 tsp (3g) espresso powder
   - 1 1/2 tsp (9g) salt
   - 2 cups (240g) all-purpose flour
   - 1 1/2 cups (180g) whole wheat flour
2. Add to wet ingredients
3. Mix on low speed (or stir by hand) until shaggy dough forms

### Step 4: Knead Dough (8-10 minutes)
**With Stand Mixer:**
- Knead with dough hook on medium-low
- 8-10 minutes until smooth and elastic
- Dough should clear sides of bowl
- Should be soft, slightly tacky (not sticky)

**By Hand:**
- Turn onto floured surface
- Knead 10-12 minutes
- Should be smooth, elastic, soft
- Add flour 1 tbsp at a time if too sticky
- Don't add too much flour (keeps bread soft)

### Step 5: First Rise (1 hour)
1. Form dough into ball
2. Place in greased bowl
3. Turn to coat with oil
4. Cover with clean kitchen towel
5. Let rise in warm place 1 hour
6. Should double in size
7. 🚨 **MUST DOUBLE** - creates the soft texture

**Warm Place Ideas:**
- On top of refrigerator
- In oven with light on (no heat)
- Near (not on) warm stove
- In microwave with cup of just-boiled water

### Step 6: Shape Loaves

**For Mini Loaves (Cheesecake Factory Style - RECOMMENDED):**
1. Grease 4 mini loaf pans (5.75" x 3.25")
2. Punch down dough to release air
3. Turn out onto lightly oiled surface
4. Divide into 4 equal pieces (about 200g each)
5. Shape each into mini loaf:
   - Pat piece into rectangle (about 5" x 4")
   - Roll tightly from short end
   - Pinch seam closed
   - Tuck ends under
6. Place seam-side down in greased pans
7. Should fill pans about halfway

**For Regular Loaves:**
1. Grease 2 regular loaf pans (9x5")
2. Divide dough in half
3. Shape each into loaf same way
4. Place seam-side down in pans

### Step 7: Second Rise (45-60 minutes)
1. Cover pans with kitchen towel
2. Let rise 45-60 minutes
3. Mini loaves: should rise to top of pan
4. Regular loaves: should dome above rim
5. Should look puffy and soft

### Step 8: Bake

**For Mini Loaves:**
- Preheat oven to 350°F (177°C)
- Bake 25-30 minutes
- **CHECK AT 25 MINUTES**:
  - Deep brown color (almost mahogany)
  - Internal temp 190-195°F (88-91°C)
  - Sounds hollow when tapped
- Should be dark (cocoa makes it dark, not burnt)

**For Regular Loaves:**
- Preheat oven to 350°F (177°C)
- Bake 35-40 minutes
- Same doneness indicators

### Step 9: Brush and Cool
1. Remove from oven
2. IMMEDIATELY brush tops with melted butter (2 tbsp)
3. Let cool in pans 10 minutes
4. Remove from pans to wire rack
5. Cool 10 more minutes
6. Slice and serve warm with butter

## Timing

**Total Time: 2.5-3 hours**
- Activate yeast: 10 min
- Mix and knead: 10 min
- First rise: 1 hour
- Shape: 10 min
- Second rise: 45-60 min
- Bake: 25-30 min (mini) or 35-40 min (regular)
- Cool slightly: 10 min

**Make-Ahead Options:**
- After first rise: Punch down, refrigerate overnight, bring to room temp (30 min), continue
- After shaping: Cover, refrigerate overnight, bring to room temp (1 hour), let rise 30 min, bake
- Fully baked: Freeze up to 3 months, thaw and reheat

## Troubleshooting

**Bread didn't rise:**
- Yeast dead or expired
- Water too hot (killed yeast) or too cold (didn't activate)
- Room too cold for rising
- Not enough time (must double)

**Too dense:**
- Not enough kneading
- Didn't rise enough
- Too much whole wheat flour (measure level cups)
- Added too much flour while kneading

**Tastes like chocolate:**
- Used natural cocoa instead of Dutch-process (wrong type)
- Dutch-process has neutral flavor, natural cocoa is acidic/chocolatey

**Dry bread:**
- Overbaked (check at minimum time)
- Too much flour in dough
- Cooled too long uncovered

**Not dark enough:**
- Used natural cocoa instead of Dutch-process
- Didn't use espresso powder (optional but helps)
- Need to bake until darker (cocoa makes it dark, not burnt)

## Variations

### Darker Color
- Add 1 tsp more espresso powder
- Increase cocoa to 1/4 cup (24g)

### Sweeter
- Increase honey to 1/4 cup (60ml)
- Increase brown sugar to 1/3 cup (67g)

### Dinner Roll Version
- Shape into 16 rolls instead of loaves
- Bake at 350°F for 15-18 minutes
- Perfect for gatherings

### Cinnamon Swirl
- Roll dough with cinnamon sugar before shaping
- Creates marble effect with dark dough

## Storage and Reheating

**Room Temperature:**
- 3 days in airtight container or bread bag
- Stays soft thanks to molasses and honey

**Refrigerator:**
- Not recommended (dries out faster)
- If must refrigerate: 5 days, reheat before serving

**Freezer:**
- Wrap loaves tightly in plastic, then foil
- Freeze up to 3 months
- Thaw at room temp 3-4 hours
- Reheat before serving

**Reheating (CRITICAL FOR BEST EXPERIENCE):**
- Wrap in foil, 350°F oven for 15 minutes
- Microwave individual slices 15-20 seconds
- Toaster or toaster oven for slices (creates crispy outside, soft inside)
