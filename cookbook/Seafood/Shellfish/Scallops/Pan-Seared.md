# Pan-Seared Scallops

## Overview
Restaurant-quality scallops with golden crust. VERY easy to overcook. When done right: sweet, tender, buttery. When wrong: rubbery hockey pucks.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **DRY SCALLOPS ONLY**: "Wet" scallops (treated with chemicals) won't brown
- **MUST BE DRY**: Pat dry 3x - moisture = steaming, not searing
- **HOT PAN ESSENTIAL**: Not hot enough = gray, rubbery scallops
- **DON'T MOVE**: Touching = no crust
- **90 SECONDS PER SIDE**: Overcook by 30 seconds = ruined
- **COOK IN BATCHES**: Crowding = steaming, not searing

## Target Doneness
- **PERFECT**: Opaque outside, slightly translucent center (120-125°F)
- **OVERCOOKED**: Completely opaque, firm, rubbery
- 🚨 **PULL EARLY** - carryover cooking continues

## Equipment
- Large cast iron or stainless skillet (NOT nonstick)
- Paper towels (lots)
- Tongs or thin spatula
- Timer

## Ingredients (serves 2-3)
- 1 lb large sea scallops (dry, not wet)
- 2 tbsp high smoke-point oil (grapeseed or avocado)
- 2 tbsp butter
- Kosher salt
- Black pepper
- Optional: lemon wedges, fresh herbs

## Scallop Selection (CRITICAL)
**Dry vs Wet Scallops:**
- **DRY** (what you want): Natural, untreated, ivory/pinkish, smell sweet
- **WET** (avoid): Treated with STP (preservative), bright white, smell chemical
- Ask fishmonger for "dry scallops" specifically
- Dry cost more but worth it

**Size:**
- Large: 10-20 per pound (BEST for searing)
- Medium: 20-30 per pound (OK but harder to not overcook)
- Small: Too small to sear properly

## Method

### Step 1: Prep Scallops (30 minutes before)
1. Remove side muscle (small tough piece on side)
2. Pat VERY dry with paper towels
3. Place on clean paper towels on plate
4. Refrigerate uncovered 20-30 minutes
5. 🚨 **DRYING IS CRITICAL** - exposed to air = drier = better crust
6. Pat dry AGAIN right before cooking

### Step 2: Heat Pan (5 minutes before cooking)
1. Heat skillet over MEDIUM-HIGH to HIGH
2. Let heat 5 full minutes (must be HOT)
3. Add oil - should shimmer immediately
4. When oil starts to smoke slightly, ready to cook
5. 🚨 Turn on exhaust fan

### Step 3: Season and Sear First Side (90 seconds)
1. Pat scallops dry ONE FINAL TIME
2. Season one side only with salt and pepper
3. Place scallops in pan seasoned-side DOWN
4. Space them out - at least 1" apart
5. 🚨 **DON'T MOVE THEM**
6. **SET TIMER FOR 90 SECONDS**
7. Should hear aggressive sizzle
8. If not sizzling loudly: pan wasn't hot enough

### Step 4: Flip and Sear Second Side (60-90 seconds)
1. Peek under one scallop at 90 seconds
2. Should be deep golden-brown
3. If pale: wait 30 more seconds
4. Season top with salt and pepper
5. Flip with tongs (should release easily)
6. **SET TIMER FOR 60-75 SECONDS**
7. Add butter to pan
8. Tilt pan, baste scallops with butter

### Step 5: Remove and Rest
1. Check doneness:
   - Press gently - should give slightly but spring back
   - Peek at side - should be opaque with small translucent center
2. Remove to plate immediately
3. 🚨 **DON'T OVERCOOK** - better slightly under than over
4. Let rest 1-2 minutes (will continue cooking)

## Doneness Tests
**Perfect:**
- Golden-brown crust both sides
- Opaque outside
- Slightly translucent center
- Springs back when pressed
- Internal temp 115-120°F

**Overcooked:**
- Completely opaque
- Very firm when pressed
- Rubbery texture
- Shrunk significantly

## Serving
- Serve immediately
- Squeeze lemon over top
- Drizzle with brown butter from pan
- Garnish with fresh herbs

## Troubleshooting
- **Won't brown?** Scallops too wet or pan not hot enough
- **Stuck to pan?** Not ready to flip - wait 15 more seconds
- **Rubbery?** Overcooked - reduce time by 15-30 seconds next time
- **Shrunk a lot?** Wet scallops - buy dry next time
- **Steamed looking?** Pan overcrowded or too much moisture

## Pro Tips
- Work in batches - don't crowd pan
- Scallops should sizzle when they hit pan
- Resist urge to flip early - let crust form
- Save brown butter in pan for sauce
- Can cook bacon first, use bacon fat instead of oil
- Larger scallops are more forgiving (harder to overcook)

## Advanced: Butter Basting Technique
After flipping:
1. Add 2 tbsp butter, garlic clove, thyme to pan
2. Tilt pan so butter pools
3. Spoon hot butter over scallops continuously
4. Adds flavor and ensures even cooking
