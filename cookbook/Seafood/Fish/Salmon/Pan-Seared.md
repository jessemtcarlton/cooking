# Pan-Seared Salmon (Perfect Every Time)

## Overview
Crispy skin, tender flaky interior. The key: skin-side down for 90% of cooking time. Near-foolproof method.

## 🚨 CRITICAL WARNINGS 🚨
- **SKIN MUST BE DRY**: Wet skin = soggy, not crispy
- **PRESS DOWN**: Skin curls up if not pressed - ruins sear
- **DON'T FLIP TOO EARLY**: Cook 90% on skin side
- **MEDIUM-HIGH HEAT ONLY**: Too high = burnt skin, raw inside
- **PULL AT 120°F**: Will reach 125-130°F (perfect) while resting
- **WILD VS FARMED**: Different cooking times - farmed cooks faster

## Target Doneness
- **Medium-Rare**: 120°F (slightly translucent center) ⭐ **BEST**
- **Medium**: 125-130°F (slightly opaque center)
- **Well-Done**: 135°F+ (don't do this - dry salmon)

## Equipment
- Large stainless or cast iron skillet
- Thin flexible spatula
- Instant-read thermometer
- Timer

## Ingredients (serves 4)
- 4 salmon fillets (6 oz each, 1-1.5" thick)
- Skin-on preferred
- 2 tbsp neutral oil
- Kosher salt
- Black pepper
- 2 tbsp butter (optional)
- Lemon wedges

## Preparation (30+ minutes before)

### Step 1: Dry the Skin (CRITICAL)
1. Pat salmon dry with paper towels
2. Place skin-side UP on paper towels
3. Refrigerate uncovered 30 minutes
4. 🚨 **AIR-DRYING = CRISPY SKIN**
5. Pat dry again before cooking

### Step 2: Score the Skin (if thick)
1. If skin is thick, score it (prevents curling)
2. Make 3-4 shallow diagonal cuts through skin
3. Don't cut into flesh
4. 🚨 If skin is thin, skip this step

## Method

### Step 1: Heat Pan (5 minutes)
1. Heat skillet over MEDIUM-HIGH heat
2. Let heat 5 minutes
3. Add oil - should shimmer
4. Tilt pan to coat

### Step 2: Season and Place (skin-side down)
1. Pat salmon dry ONE MORE TIME
2. Season both sides with salt and pepper
3. Place skin-side DOWN in pan
4. 🚨 **IMMEDIATELY PRESS DOWN** with spatula for 30 seconds
5. Prevents skin from curling
6. Should sizzle - if not, increase heat

### Step 3: Cook Skin-Side (6-8 minutes)
1. **SET TIMER FOR 6 MINUTES** (for 1" thick)
2. Reduce heat to MEDIUM after 1 minute
3. 🚨 **DON'T FLIP YET**
4. Watch sides - should see salmon cooking up sides
5. When salmon is 75% opaque (cooked 3/4 way up), ready to flip
6. Should release easily from pan

### Step 4: Flip and Finish (1-2 minutes)
1. Flip salmon flesh-side down
2. **SET TIMER FOR 60-90 SECONDS**
3. Optional: Add butter to pan
4. Check internal temp with thermometer (from side)
5. 🚨 **PULL AT 120°F** for medium-rare
6. Will rise to 125-130°F while resting

### Step 5: Rest (3-5 minutes)
1. Transfer to plate
2. Tent loosely with foil
3. **SET TIMER FOR 3 MINUTES**
4. Temp will rise 5-10°F

## Perfect Salmon Indicators
- **Skin**: Crispy, golden-brown, easily removable
- **Flesh**: Flakes easily with fork
- **Color**: Opaque outside, slightly translucent center (medium-rare)
- **Texture**: Tender, moist, not dry
- **Temp**: 125-130°F after resting

## Serving
- Serve skin-side up (shows off crispy skin)
- Squeeze fresh lemon over top
- Optional: top with compound butter or sauce

## Thickness Adjustment Chart
| Thickness | Skin-Side Time | Flesh-Side Time |
|-----------|----------------|------------------|
| 3/4" | 5-6 min | 60 sec |
| 1" | 6-7 min | 60-90 sec |
| 1.25" | 7-8 min | 90 sec |
| 1.5" | 8-9 min | 90-120 sec |

## Troubleshooting
- **Skin not crispy?** Not dry enough or heat too low
- **Skin stuck to pan?** Not ready to flip - wait 1 more minute
- **Salmon curling?** Didn't press down or need to score skin
- **Dry and overcooked?** Cooked too long - pull at 115-120°F next time
- **White albumin oozing?** Heat too high or overcooked - reduce temp

## Pro Tips
- Start checking doneness at 5 minutes for thinner fillets
- Wild salmon cooks slower than farmed (leaner)
- Can cook from frozen: Add 3-4 minutes to skin-side time
- Save crispy skin - many people's favorite part
- If skin sticks after cooking, run spatula under with water

## Skinless Salmon
If using skinless:
1. Reduce skin-side time to 4-5 minutes
2. Flip, cook 2-3 minutes
3. More prone to overcooking - watch temp closely
